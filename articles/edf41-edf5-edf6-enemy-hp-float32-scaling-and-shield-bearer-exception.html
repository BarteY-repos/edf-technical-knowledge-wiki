Enemy HP across EDF4.1, EDF5 and EDF6: float32 scaling, mission factors and Shield Bearer exception

ConversionGame technical referencesEnemy healthEDF 4.1EDF 5EDF 6
Technical reference · Source-scoped static analysis; no R129 gameplay test

The three game generations use a recognizably shared model: base object durability, a source level adjustment, a difficulty/mission-progress factor, and multiplayer scaling. The result is a floating-point number, not an integer automatically rounded by the enemy-spawn code.

The shared ingredients

In the studied EDF5 executable, an object's maximum and initial HP are assigned from the same calculated float. For normal enemies the factor is assembled in this order:

  1. Multiply the spawn's HP multiplier by the object's game_object_level_adjust (default 1).
  2. Calculate mission progress t = mission row / (mission rows - 1) and interpolate between the selected difficulty's [L, M] level bounds.
  3. Multiply the level-adjusted factor by L + (M - L) × t, then divide by 10.
  4. Multiply by game_object_durability (default 100), the difficulty's enemy-HP factor and the multiplier indexed by the number of players.

The scripted spawn factor can be different from 1. In EDF5, the SetMissionLevel/BVM mission factor can further affect spawns. In EDF6, the same underlying HP pattern survives with relocated object fields and ModeList indices. This is a source-level structural correspondence, not a claim that all three games use identical difficulty tables or health outcomes.

Why HP often has decimals

The observed EDF5 code uses single-precision SSE operations at each step. The calculation is not equivalent to first multiplying everything at double precision and rounding once. As a source example, the EDF5 Black Ant in offline mission 2 on Inferno has calculated HP of approximately 966.6055, so the smallest whole-number damage amount that reaches zero is 967. The exact stored float and the damage comparison, rather than an integer HP stat, determine death.

The EDF4.1 Shield Bearer exception

EDF4.1's AlienTrailer (Shield Bearer) reads the second difficulty factor ModeList[mode][6][difficulty][3][1] where ordinary enemy classes use [3][0]. In the source tables this makes its online HP approximately 1.2× the ordinary-rule result from Normal upward; on Easy the relation is about 0.83×. Offline, both factors equal 1. This is not the same as adding 1.2 to a multiplayer multiplier.

The EDF6 AlienTrailer uses the ordinary helper and reads factor [3][0] instead. Therefore carrying the EDF4.1 exception forward unchanged would misrepresent EDF6.

Mission-specific modifiers are real

In a survey of 98 EDF4.1 scripts, 34 included at least one non-unit enemy-spawn HP factor. The records contained 1,373 such entries across the surveyed difficulties; values ranged from 0.1 to 5. Examples include difficulty-varying Deroys in m119, Transport Ships in m304, and stronger charging bees in m137. These are script overrides, not a different hard-coded enemy species formula.
