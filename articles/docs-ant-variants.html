Ant Enemies — Resource Variants

EDF 6DocumentationGameplay & AI
Technical reference · Community-provided Japanese memo; source-specific and not independently rerun

A single-looking enemy family can have multiple distinct SGO configurations. The resource SGO, animation MRAB and model MDB are three different identities. Color variants can share geometry but change parameters with ant_ChangeModelParam.
