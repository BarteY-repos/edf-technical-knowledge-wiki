EDF 4.1 original MAD geometry: 16-map surface survey

EDF 4.1Maps & NavigationMaps
Technical reference · Exact source geometry records; offline diagnostic graph only; no runtime NAV qualification

At a glance

What this page answers: This survey measures original EDF4.1 MAD static collision surfaces in 16 map families; it is not a recovered navigation mesh.

How to read it: Use the per-map triangles, vertices and body samples to distinguish 3D geometry from a simplified top-down footprint. Multiple mesh pools need separate index rebasing.

Evidence boundary: The R21 16-map sample and later R20 or seven-SK studies have different scopes. None alone proves walkable paths in EDF6.

What was measured: a bounded, read-only analysis of original EDF 4.1 map geometry, using native source MAC/MAD data. Evidence: original game asset records inspected offline; not a recovered game pathfinding graph and not a runtime playtest. Results below are from the R21 sample dated 15 November 2026 and apply to that specific corpus and selection method.

What the survey actually establishes

All 16 inspected map families yielded parseable source geometry from a retained, root-preserving 18-family source archive; 31 static-body samples contain 284,840 triangles in total. For an hkpStorageExtendedMeshShape with multiple meshstorage pools, vertices need independent pool-local index decoding and rebasing before a combined triangle list is meaningful. Each trianglesSubparts subpart has its own offsets, transform and index/count bounds; simply concatenating indices without the subpart mapping can corrupt the apparent geometry.

The earlier two SK_CRATER01 road bodies that had been rejected by a single-storage assumption were successfully decoded: sk_craterroad01 2,430 triangles and sk_craterroad02 2,394 triangles. This is a native source-mesh format observation, not evidence that a particular NPC can traverse the roads.

3D adjacency is not 2D footprint overlap

For the sample, candidate surface triangles were filtered by an absolute normal-based 55° slope rule and joined only when their quantized three-dimensional edges matched (weld tolerance 0.001 source units). Triangles overlapping in the XZ plane but lying at different heights were not joined. This matters for multi-storey buildings, caves, roofs and bridges: a 2D top-down polygon graph cannot by itself tell whether two surfaces connect physically.

An absolute-normal slope check is orientation-agnostic: ceiling triangles may qualify alongside floor triangles. Edge-connected components are therefore diagnostic geometry components, neither guaranteed walkable areas nor independently proven Havok navmesh islands. Winding, obstacles, agent width, step height, stairs/doors and links between different bodies remain separate problems.

Notable native geometry cases

  • IG_CAVE01: the two examined collision bodies have 13,452 and 22,164 original triangles, respectively, and yield 11 and 19 independent edge-connected slope-candidate components. They are not 30 validated NPC regions.
  • IG_HORNETCAVE01: one sampled collision body has 27,440 triangles and 60 candidate components; its vertically separated and potentially ceiling-facing surfaces must not be conflated with paths.
  • NW_KOUSOUBLD01: a single named field body has 43,751 triangles and one diagnostic candidate component. A single component does not prove streets or buildings are navigable to NPCs.
  • SK_CRATER01: the two road meshes contain 4,824 source triangles combined; these meshes had been missed by the earlier single-meshstorage decoder.
  • NW_HILLYCITY_LIGHT: the separate previously retained strict original field extraction has 45,505 triangles; the R21 two-body sample is 53,866 triangles. They are different scopes. The historical R1D HKT and NVMD identity questions remain separate from this sample.

Evidence boundaries and missing data

The survey did not cover all 1,591 static mesh candidates, did not reconstruct the mountain terrain heightfield from SK _fld2mgrid.shfb / RAB source, and did not connect separate bodies through ramps, doors, bridges or placed obstacles. No agent-specific clearance, turn radius or locomotion profile was proved. This report produced no new HKT/NVMD navigation asset and includes no Windows/EDF6 runtime validation.

The original unmodified map assets are not redistributed in this wiki. The summary and per-body derived measurements below are documentation only.

Complete R20 original-MAD body census (different scope from R21)

The earlier original-asset R20 pass covered all parsed MAD body records across the same 16 project map families. This is a source-record population census, not the 31-body R21 triangle/edge-connectivity sample. Its machine summary reports 18,342 bodies, 17,489 static bodies and 1,591 candidate static mesh bodies. Counts do not include a reconstructed terrain-walkability graph or prove every body contributes to NPC navigation.

An earlier R19 name-based hint would have selected just 181/1,591 static-mesh candidates. The source-data classification in R20 found 1,410 additional mesh candidates missed by that naming heuristic (88.6% of the candidate set). An asset's name is therefore not a safe universal classifier of static collision geometry.

Newer seven-map placed-body contact scope

The R24–R28 seven-SK-map contact survey is a different, later selection: 1,770 source MAPB placements of qualifying static meshes were analyzed against terrain, with 505,633 placed triangles and four unresolved terrain-domain cases. These values must not replace or be added to the 31-body / 284,840-triangle 16-family sample in this article. Both remain source-only.

Nine non-SK maps: original MAD collision geometry is layered, not one terrain height

A later, separately scoped nine-map original-source survey examined non-SK cities, industrial zones and underground maps. It indexed 1,504 original static collision-mesh bodies. These are not additional bodies to add to the older 16-family count in this article: the selection rules, geometry families and measured scopes differ.

The survey completed the static-mesh body pass in three underground/cave families: IG_CAVE01 (4/4 bodies), IG_HORNETCAVE01 (1/1) and NW_UNDERGROUND01 (48/48). Its remaining six city families required bounded spatial sampling. For cave candidates, a single 4 m X/Z column can contain multiple distinct horizontal collision sheets at different Y heights, alongside side walls. Overlapping X/Z footprints do not prove that surfaces connect or that one is walkable. A ceiling cannot be promoted to an NPC floor merely because its triangle normal is roughly vertical.

A separate original-MAD triangle census fully processed 1,451 static-mesh bodies and 925,622 triangles across the six city families, while its detailed 4 m windows remained bounded. The source-tile follow-up covered 768 of 8,402 planned 64 m windows (128 per city map), producing 2,420,574 classified cell fragments; there were 46,618 columns with multiple candidate sheet strata and 2,390 columns showing opposed source winding at coincident surfaces. The unprocessed 7,634 windows must not be called complete NAV coverage.
