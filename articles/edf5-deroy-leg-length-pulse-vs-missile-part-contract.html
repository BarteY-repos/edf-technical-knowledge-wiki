EDF5 Deroys: leg-length variants, pulse turrets, missiles and destructible parts

EDF 5Game technical referencesEnemies / bosses
Technical reference · Source analysis only (unless a source fact is contributor-verified); no new EDF runtime test

EDF5's Deiroi SGOs are differentiated by a body configuration and parts_config assembling the legs and weapons. The Japanese bestiary distinguishes Deroy (pulse-turret type) from Deroy B (missile-equipped type), with short/long variants. In particular, the _m suffix in e509_deiroi_m refers to the missile version, not a medium body; a legacy raw catalogue glossed it “Deroy (Medium)” and is contradicted by the SGO parts list.

EDF5 root SGO Base body durability Leg weapon configuration
e509_deiroi 1350 Four pulse turrets
e509_deiroi_m 1350 Missile turret replacing the corresponding pulse configuration
e509_deiroi_s 825 Short legs, one pulse turret, level adjustment 0.7
e509_deiroi_sm 825 Short legs, one missile turret, level adjustment 0.7
e509_deiroi_l 1500 Three pulse turrets plus one missile turret
e509_deiroi_xl 2250 Ten pulse turrets plus one missile turret

The short body adjusted baseline is 825 × 0.7 = 577.5. Raw part durabilities are pulse turret 40, missile turret 200, toe 260 and leg joint 1. The DeiroiFallEffect definitions are separate objects that reference corresponding Deroy SGOs; they should not be counted as live Deroy body instances.

This explains why counting “Deroy” SGO filenames alone may overcount actors, and why interpreting _m as a size mislabels the weapon configuration. The source cross-checks exact SGO parts against the Japanese bestiary; source values are not gameplay HP after difficulty scaling.
