Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-wiki-generator-first-setting

The EDF 4.1 Japanese wiki counts a 2100 generator (#edf41-bvm-call-layouts) at its owner's first setting only: a later handler that sets the same owner's generator again (a new wave size, often larger) adds no rows. M30 Bloody Mountain (online only, m407): three tunnels, each set to 10 Red Ants by two different handlers, wiki 10 x 3 = 30 (the summed settings give 60). M83 Encircling (M88 online, m138): eight ships' generators set to 6 / 6 / 9 (black, spider, bee) and later re-set to 9 / 9 / 13; the wiki's 59 is the ships plus the first settings (51). With this rule both missions match on every difficulty, and M65 Fortress Attack (M69 online, m134) matches at Inferno once its fortress parts are set apart (#edf41-wiki-vs-scripts-3-ships; 50 on both sides, Normal 1 off). A generator's count is a wave size, not a total (file-formats.md#edf41-generator-waves-repeat).

Basis: kinds41.py 30 69 88 --rec, the two settings read in the scripts (insrange41.py m407 316 495), then the rule added to scratch File_Storage/Logs/catalog/bvm_vs_wiki41.py (owner = the operand two instructions before the SGO path; output bvm_vs_wiki41_kh125.txt)

The enemies: 2 medium Deroys (two 2101 batches of 1), Flying Vehicles 6 x 4 then 3 x 6 (2100 on the hull's hatches), and Small Dragons (DragonSmall401) 6 x 4: the final-form handler (after ObjectAction 6 and 1, call 3200) sets the hull's hatches 0-3 to 6 each, and a later handler re-sets the same four to 8, which the wiki does not count (#edf41-wiki-generator-first-setting); summed, the evaluator gives 56.
