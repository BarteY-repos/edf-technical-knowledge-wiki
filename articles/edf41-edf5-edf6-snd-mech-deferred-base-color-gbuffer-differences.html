Legacy `SND_MECH` shading across EDF4.1, EDF5 and EDF6: why colors change

ConversionGame technical referencesRendering / material compatibilityEDF 4.1EDF 5EDF 6
Technical reference · Source static evidence with unverified behavior labelled; no new in-game R132 test

SND_MECH.SGO and SND_CHARA_2COLORCHANGE.SGO exist across multiple EDF generations. The examined Deferred pixel shader implementations do not preserve the same base-color formula or output register contract. This explains why an old model's color and reflect texture cannot be assumed to look identical after a direct cross-generation import.

Base color formula

Let b be the base albedo times the diffuse/material color, and f a reflect-related component. The DXBC comparison found:

Version o0.rgb G-buffer target count Other observed information
EDF4.1 b 4 Reflect term also written to o3.y
EDF5 b 6 Separate reflection contribution in deferred combine
EDF6 b + f × (1 − b) 5 sqrt(f) written to o3.x; roughness derived from prior specular power in o3.y

The EDF6 formula raises low-color channels toward white as the reflection factor grows. The source compares a legacy gold robot, where the base (0.25,0.13,0) can be pushed toward (0.72,0.68,0.63), against a native EDF6 golden machine using a BRDF material and stronger metallic behavior.

The formulas were read from real shaders, but they do not alone prove the final pixels for every lighting, exposure and sRGB configuration. Texture color-space flags, environment maps and post-processing were not isolated to the same degree in the comparison.

Why a binary shader swap is unsafe

The differences include the number of output render targets, semantic contents of o3, G-buffer resource binding order and some vertex outputs. The loader can resolve custom shader names from app:\Shader\DX11, but simply supplying an older .sgo with the same material parameters would not restore compatible deferred-rendering semantics.

For general materials, [metallic, roughness] fields also influence ambient and directional weighting downstream; changing an old shader's specular_color is not necessarily equivalent to changing the EDF6 material's metallic parameter.
