Radar item categories and colors

  • edf5: crate category dword (0 weapon, 1 armor, 2 and 3 health); six static float4 color globals filled at first draw; the marker drawer per kind | ref: edf5/radar.md#radar-crate-category, #radar-colors | evidence: novae (ajane)
  • edf6: item category byte (0 weapon, 1 armor, 2 and 3 health) read in the radar's item loop; radar colors are RGBA constants per kind (neutral, allied, items, enemies, vehicles, weapon icons) | ref: edf6/radar.md#radar-item-loop, #radar-color-constants | evidence: static
  • edf41: the same crate categories are taken from the DropItemManager+0xA60 list; five colors are copied to static globals CC9180..CC91D0 on the first draw | ref: edf41/hud.md#edf41-radar-dispatcher | evidence: static.
  • Correction: categories agree across games. EDF6 writes the radar colors as immediate RGBA constants; EDF5 and EDF4.1 copy their palette to global state at first draw, not necessarily during executable startup. The EDF6 HUiHudRader has been mapped in edf6/radar.md#radar-center-sides; this is build-scoped native evidence rather than proof that every EDF5 renderer detail is identical.

Map bounds (key: RTTI MoveAreaManager)

  • edf41: MoveAreaManager is the object behind global [CC85A8] - 8, with minimum +0x10 and maximum +0x20 (as in EDF5); the radar border renders red | ref: edf41/hud.md#edf41-radar-stencil-border, edf41/globals.md#edf41-global-singletons-2 | evidence: static (crosswalk).
  • edf5: bounds object behind a global with a getter (min corner at +0x10, max at +0x20), most likely the MoveAreaManager | ref: edf5/radar.md#radar-mission-border, edf5/globals.md (125AC68) | evidence: novae (ajane)
  • edf6: reached from the game system at +0x40 (X/Y/Z min at +8..+0x10, max at +0x18..+0x20) | ref: edf6/globals.md#game-system-fields | evidence: static (Cheat Engine tables)
  • Differences noticed: different access path and field offsets; same idea (an axis-aligned min/max box).
  • Confidence in the mapping: medium (same concepts and class names, separate authors).
  • Added: 2026-10-16 src: novae (ajane), KittopiaCreator's EDF6 research

Radar range, scale and marker dimensions

The native radius is 200 m in each game, not an adjustable player option. Constructors write per-title scales once; patching the underlying 200.0 reference changes the range. Differences include the icon scale and the altitude term, not merely the palette. Source: the Knowledge Hub crosswalk/radar-and-map-bounds.md, game-native refs below; static/source-reported, no gameplay retest.

GameRadar position scale and visible rimMarker and altitude behavior
EDF4.1HudPlayer_Rader (vtable AA8EB0), position scale 0.4 at +0x5B0, rim 80 units.Fixed ±5 quad; height contribution (0.4 × 1.5)² × dy, limited to ±80. The radar is a 3D model reached by unprojecting a screen point.
EDF5HUiHudRader, scale 0.6 at +0x8E0, rim 120.Dot size 1.2 at +0x8E4 scales a ±5 quad; height term (0.6 × 1.5)² × dy, clamped ±80.
EDF6HUiHudRader, scale 0.6 at +0x8B0, rim 120.Dot size 1.2 at +0x8B4 scales a ±5 rectangle; height term 0.5 × dy, clamped ±80.

Source range constants (build-specific): EDF4.1 AB68FC loaded at 400F2E, EDF5 EE66FC at 4CE64E, EDF6 1765A70 at 82C746. The two older games share a reported stencil-clipped circle draw order, whereas EDF6's equivalent order is not fully mapped. Differences in heading pitch fallback and player colors must not be collapsed into one universal renderer.
