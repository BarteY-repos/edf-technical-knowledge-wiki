Boarding and leaving audio: occupancy and change flags

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: vehicle-seat-change-bits

Reading map for this research record

  2. Seat scan and transition detection

The reading map does not add an in-game verification or replace the exact native offsets and exceptions.

Full original vehicle research — expand (1,280 characters)

Boarding and leaving sounds come from seat change bits: the 16-bit veh+0x628 holds in its low bits "seat i occupied" (as the vehicle last saw it) and at bit i << [veh+0x620] (the seat count, written by the constructor 629450 at 629C8F) "seat i changed this frame". The seat scan in OnPreUpdate 62EEC0 (loop 62F160..62F1BD; 62F1AA mov [rsi+0x628],dx; mov ecx,[rsi+0x620]; shl ax,cl) flips a seat's bit and sets its change bit when the seat's rider (seat +0x268) disagrees with it; clearing a seat (634940) sets the change bit too. The car, helicopter and mech updates (674B1A, 652713, 6437D0) call 632A60(veh, board preset, leave preset) (a car's car_base_se_table [8] / [9]), which plays the boarding sound at a changed seat that is occupied and the leaving sound at one that is empty (7B4510; the test at 632AC4..632ACE: shl al,cl twice, test [rsi+0x628],al); the base update clears the change bits at 630596 (and [+0x628],[+0x624]). The occupancy bits are 8 wide (shl al,cl), so seats past 8 are not tracked; the change bits 16 (shl ax,cl). So a rider created straight into a seat (an NPC crew made in the air or in the car) makes the next scan play the boarding sound; setting the occupied bit and clearing the change bit for that seat silences it (the mod's runs not in game).
