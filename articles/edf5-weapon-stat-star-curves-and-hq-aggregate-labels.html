EDF5 weapon star curves, stat slots and aggregated HQ star labels

EDF 5Game technical referencesWeapon progression
Technical reference · Source-scoped static analysis; no R129 gameplay test

A weapon may show an overall star label beside an HQ statistic, but the engine computes individual stat modifiers first. The stars for ammo count, damage, accuracy, firing interval, reload, range and related properties can progress independently.

Per-stat data

The EDF5 source describes an upgradeable stat as an array of six entries:

[value_at_5_stars, stat_kind, star_slot, top_star, Ra, Mu]

The first number is a stat's value at five stars, not at zero stars. star_slot selects which stored upgrade level applies to this statistic. Ra is the deterioration share at zero stars and Mu sets the curve. The source example for PA-11 LS damage is [16.0, 8, 1, 8, 0.5, 0.5]: approximately 8 damage at zero stars, 16 at five stars, and 18.1 at its top of eight stars.

Let s be the stat's current star rating. For a statistic where higher is better, the formula is:

[ V(s)=V_5\left((1-Ra)+Ra\left(\frac{s}{5}\right)^{Mu}\right). ]

For a statistic where lower is better, such as reload time or inaccuracy spread:

[ V(s)=V_5\left((1+Ra)-Ra\left(\frac{s}{5}\right)^{Mu}\right). ]

Examples of stat kinds in the source: ammo count 0, damage 8, accuracy 13, projectile speed 16, reload time 21 and firing interval 25. A plain numeric SGO value without a star array is not upgraded by this mechanism.

Why the HQ line shows a star average

The equipment screen builds one line from one or more underlying stat kinds, then calculates a truncated average of their star levels and top levels. A stat whose maximum is under 5 can be rescaled for this display. This explains why an individual line can show an intermediate label or + even when some underlying statistic has already reached its own cap. The UI shows a filled star when the displayed average equals its corresponding top value, rather than directly checking each raw stored stat for equality.

The display evaluator and the underlying weapon value evaluator are not interchangeable. An aggregated visual label cannot reconstruct the complete per-stat upgrade state of the weapon.
