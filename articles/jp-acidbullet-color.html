AcidBullet RGB: source experiments and color hypothesis

ConversionCommunity Japanese WorkbookShadersEDF 5EDF 6
Technical reference · Translated contributed workbook notes; sample data, negative experiments and explicitly labeled inferences

Workbook observations

The Japanese workbook groups several AcidBullet color experiments under 解析!A1851:..., including examples annotated RGB=2,2,2, RGB=1,1,2, RGB=1,1,3, RGB=1,2,2, RGB=1,2,3, RGB=1,2,5 and other sample triples (解析!A1885:FC1896). Those examples originally used embedded screenshots; the public wiki does not redistribute the screenshots.

The author reports that an input ratio R:G:B = 1:2:4 appeared to produce zero saturation and therefore proposes possible channel factors of R×4, G×2, B×1. This is a hypothesis derived from one family of visuals, not a measured shader constant or guaranteed linear light-space transformation. The workbook explicitly phrases it as “seems there are the following coefficients” (解析!A1887:B1890).

Why a global RGB multiplier would be unjustified

Many effects may vary in texture mask, material/shader family, HDR tonemap, fog, source-color space and per-particle state. A screenshot matching one neutral triplet cannot establish a universal color conversion for every acid projectile, let alone every EDF material. In particular, no test here separates raw parameter handling from final display color.

Implementation guidance: preserve source RGB values, annotate the proposed coefficients as hypothesis, and test calibrated input/output values under a controlled build and effect before baking factors into a converter. A screenshot-based perceived hue is not a direct measurement of a floating-point register or texture sample.

Remaining questions

  • Is the effect's color field interpreted in linear or gamma-encoded space?
  • Does the hypothesized channel factor apply in the projectile code, the material/shader, or only the selected texture/effect?
  • Are the displayed examples measured at identical exposure and under the same color pipeline?

Source: mod製作メモ.xlsx, 解析!A1851:FC1896. No new image analysis or EDF runtime measurement was performed; screenshot assets were intentionally excluded from public redistribution.
