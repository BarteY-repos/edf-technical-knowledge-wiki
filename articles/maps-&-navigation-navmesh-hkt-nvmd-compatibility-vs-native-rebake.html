NAVMESH HKT and NVMD: compatibility migration vs native rebake

UniversalMaps & NavigationNavigationEDF 4.1EDF 5EDF 6
Source-backed research / case study · Runtime

At a glance

What it is: Making an old navigation resource readable by EDF6 and reproducing a native EDF6 rebake are two different research targets.

Where it fits: The studied bridge deals with Havok graph/mesh structures and DMVN/NVMD metadata; each may change independently during conversion.

Important distinction: A successful parser, graph rewrite or matching face count is not proof of matching native topology or in-game actor pathfinding.

Evidence level: Reconciled structural, binary and runtime evidence across EDF5→EDF6 and EDF4.1 reconstruction work.

Two different goals

Implementation rule: Distinguish:

  1. compatibility migration — produce target-readable/usable navigation data while preserving source semantics as closely as possible;
  2. exact native rebake — reproduce a target-native or historical byte/topology oracle.

These are not the same problem.

HKT

Observed native behavior: NAV HKT requires Havok-generation migration (EDF5-era Havok 2015/64-bit representation to EDF6-era 2020/32-bit representation in the qualified workflow).

Exact native parity is an inverse-bake/rebake problem. EDF6 native data may retessellate topology, and runtime HKT does not serialize every generator setting needed to reconstruct the original bake mechanically.

Do not assume: Header/version edits or topology-preserving reserialization reproduce native EDF6 output.

Qualified tagfile-generation bridge

In the retained EDF5→EDF6 compatibility line, source NAV HKT is a 64-bit Havok tagfile identified as SDKV20150100; the EDF6 target family is a 32-bit SDKV20200100 tagfile. The working converter does not merely patch that string: it decodes source graph/nav structures and reserializes them against the target type registry.

High-value measured deltas include:

  • source/target nav faces remain 16-byte records in the qualified path;
  • nav edges remain 20 bytes and vertices 16 bytes;
  • graph nodes/edges remain 8-byte records;
  • graph connection representation grows from 16 to 20 bytes in the measured bridge;
  • source volume connections are dropped where the qualified EDF6 target has them empty;
  • EDF6 adds/uses target-side annotated-streaming-set wrappers;
  • the source hkaiNavMeshQueryMediator role becomes the EDF6 hkaiQueryMediator form used by the bridge;
  • source 64-bit user data is narrowed to the target 32-bit representation where required.

The converted shared-map HKT files preserve the source geometry exactly enough for compatibility validation, but they are intentionally not claimed byte/topology-identical to native EDF6 rebakes. A representative CAVE501 comparison illustrates the distinction: the retained EDF5 navmesh has 21,778 vertices while the native EDF6 rebake has 6,883.

NVMD

Observed native behavior: NVMD requires structural migration, including the measured 0x120 → 0x130 / record-0 expansion family.

The measured record-0 change is 4 → 8 bytes, with downstream offsets rebased. Some shared-map NVMD outputs become byte-identical to native EDF6 after applying the measured conversion, but that success does not imply the paired HKT should also be byte-identical: target-native HKT is frequently a fresh rebake.

Implementation rule: NVMD identity/faceData correctness is a separate gate from HKT geometry. Exact NVMD does not prove exact HKT, and exact HKT structure does not prove AI behavior.

Streaming, graph and geometry are separate axes

Keep these independent during diagnosis:

  • tile geometry/topology;
  • streaming/seam connections;
  • graph/tree serialization;
  • clearance/generator settings;
  • section/face identity;
  • AI query/perception behavior.

A corrected graph decoder fixed narrow-int/hkHalf16 interpretation in research tooling without changing raw serialized ITEM payloads. That tool correction must not be mistaken for a runtime navigation fix.

RoomB negative evidence

A real high-only source streaming bridge was found penetrating a closed convex collider by about 1.1083 m. The structural detector reproduced the issue, but removing that stitch in the R2O experiment was runtime-negative for the observed AI wall symptom.

Known exception: Physical wall collision itself was confirmed: actors and web projectiles cannot cross. The remaining symptom is more consistent with AI targeting/perception/query behavior than absence of the physical wall.

Implementation rule: A structural anomaly can be real without being the behavioral root cause.

Source-graph cross-corpus audit: 18 navigation variants

A bounded source-only study screened 18 low/high navigation variants for a closest-edge streaming bridge that passes through a closed convex static collision primitive. This was a diagnostic for one class of invalid streaming relation, not a complete map connectivity or native AI visibility test. The criterion was a solid crossing of at least 0.05 m and at least 90% of the tested edge bridge, with a 1 m agent-height sample offset and a scoped, placement-linked hkpListShape convex child. Other shape types, placement orientations and AI query paths were not exhaustively evaluated.

EDF4.1 exact-oracle work

EDF4.1 R1C demonstrates the stricter case: byte-exact HKT/NVMD reproduction can be required as an oracle. R1D remains non-exact even when triangle counts and NVMD match; exact hashes are the gate there.

Havok TYPE wire generation: a concrete compatibility trap

HKT type metadata may be semantically readable by a permissive offline parser while using a wire envelope incompatible with the native target loader. The retained source-generated NAV TYPE bridge was corrected from a legacy-compatible record family:

TPTR / TSTR / TNAM / FSTR / TBDY / THSH / TPAD

to the native EDF6 Havok-2020 family:

TST1 / TNA1 / FST1 / TBDY / TPAD

Both represent the studied 89-type symbolic NAV schema. The modern generated TYPE section measures 3,960 bytes. Its parsed semantic fingerprint matches the independently inspected native diagnostic even though the raw TYPE bytes are intentionally not copied from the game. Older tooling emitted unnecessary body records for rootless/zero-option types and a synthetic zero-filled pointer table because the old parser tolerated them; that permissiveness was not proof of actual engine compatibility.

The corrected source-only writer passed paired low/high offline gates on CAVE503, HEIGEN507, NW_EUROPE01 and DESERT508 (8 candidate outputs). A later current-authority ledger classified the modern TYPE path as runtime-positive, superseding the earlier document that correctly described it as runtime-pending at that earlier date. These statements apply to the qualified conversion family, not arbitrary HKT writers or exact native navmesh rebakes.

When physical collision passes but AI still ignores the wall

A concrete example is the EDFROOM solid-wall / AI-targeting experiment: observed spiders could attack toward allied NPCs through a physical wall, even though web projectiles stopped on the wall. A real high-HKT cross-solid streaming connection was measured and removed in a one-variable experiment, but the later gameplay test was negative. This distinguishes collision blocking, streaming topology, agent/target selection and line-of-sight. A navigation anomaly does not automatically explain AI perception or projectile impact.

Working with a measured NVMD binary, not just a version number

The source-owned NVMD converter gives a concrete implementation reference: DMVN, a 0x2C-byte little-endian header, sections A/B/C/D and a B-record widening from four to eight bytes in 0x130. Its added float is a compatibility default in the source-only conversion, not a reconstruction of the target's native agent data. The exact field-by-field tables, offset equations and qualification limits are available in NVMD / DMVN binary format.

The examined 14 source high/low NAV HKT outputs for seven shared maps preserve the source graph/geometry in the measured migration path, but do not reproduce native EDF6 rebakes. Native conversion parity must be assessed independently for topology, streaming connections and agent settings. Some exact-edge matching tests returned high precision for native connections under strict tolerance but failed to cover the more general EDFROOM geometry; filtering to exact matches would therefore destroy valid source connectivity.

Coarse graph/clearance encoding in the EDFROOM pair

The DM019 graph edge records remained 8 bytes on both sides of the examined compatibility pair. Face-cluster adjacency exactly yielded 84 LOW and 374 HIGH stored intercluster edges. The examined mcpData is a pair of bytes (interpolant + clearance); faceOffsets are uint32, and edge-pair/vertex clearances are uint8. An earlier decoder confused the 2-byte upper-half-of-float32 hkHalf16 with a 4-byte float, producing false bEdgeCost differences. For the values and limits see native HKT graph and clearance reference.

Correct serialization is not a native navmesh rebake, and structurally correct clearance cache arrays are not a runtime AI LOS guarantee.

EDF4.1 source terrain without a legacy HKT: measured R31/R32 boundary

The seven original EDF4.1 SK SHFB maps audited for the native 4 m terrain/HKT/NVMD case contain source heightfield and placements but no NAVMESH.HKT in that retained map corpus. Thus the EDF5→EDF6 existing-HKT converter is not a substitute for extracting the original 4.1 walkable surface. What remains reusable is its verified target format, graph/streaming/clearance representation, and source-derived geometry validators.

The original SK_HEIGEN02 16-tile bounded window yielded 2,048 4 m source triangles and 24 streaming seams in both LOW/HIGH structural candidates; its NVMD checksums match while its HKT checksums differ. That earlier bounded result is no longer the full extent of source-derived construction: SK_SANGAKU01 and SK_SANGAKU02 now each have a complete 4 m LOW/HIGH native-structured Havok2020 HKT/neutral NVMD pair, with different tile, face, seam and original RMPA-link connectivity counts by variant. See the four full-domain structural records and graph crosswalk. Neither the new structural readback nor the old window proves active NVMD, 3D collision/agent clearance or EDF6 gameplay; this source-derived SK path is separate from the HillyCity R1D exact-oracle reconstruction.

Measured native LOW versus HIGH topology (EDF5/EDF6)

A matched NW_EUROPE01 original-file sample shows native LOW at 4 tiles and HIGH at 16 tiles in both game generations, with genuinely distinct face/edge counts per variant. A root erosionRadius=0 in this sample must not be read as an NPC collision radius. Original EDF4.1 lacks corresponding transferable source NAVMESH.HKT in the audited corpus. See native LOW/HIGH and EDF4.1 source-collision/clearance evidence for exact source counts and caveats.
