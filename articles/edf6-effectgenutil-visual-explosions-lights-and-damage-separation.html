EDF6 visual effects: EffectGenUtil, explosions, particles, lights and damage separation

EDF 6Game technical referencesEffects and rendering
Technical reference · Source-declared grades; this edition does not add native game runtime testing

A convincing explosion in EDF6 can include particles, lighting, camera shake, audio, damage and destruction. Those are separate mechanisms. The native EffectGenUtil singleton is a visual-effect dispatcher in the examined EDF6 build: its functions create flames, explosions, shockwaves and other visual treatments, but do not by themselves inflict gameplay damage or play the damage object's death sound.

The debug registration db::CreateEffectTest lists effect invocations and their arguments. It is a useful static catalogue, not a promise that every debug command is exposed to retail players.

Representative native game calls

The investigated UfoCarrier508 death invokes the large-explosion visual path near its center and separately performs area damage with a 60-unit radius through another native call. Its sound is also handled separately. MovingFortress death and UfoMother511 destruction similarly use effect helpers as one part of a larger object-state transition. Thus copying only an effect's identifier or animation does not copy a boss's damage, state machine or explosion sound.

The source also identifies ChargeEffect_StdParticle, used for weapon charge glows; and LineStripObject, whose render task retains the matrix supplied when the trail was constructed. A trail's motion and orientation cannot safely be inferred from the moving owner alone if the renderable holds its own original transform.

Lifetimes and ordering

EffectGenUtil is instantiated in scene initialization and may be null outside a mission. Its visual emitter and light managers are distinct from sound-channel managers. A StencilLight initializer includes position, colors, radius and lifetime in frames; an internal path changes those parameters before display. Persistent emitters own update and draw jobs and should not be assumed to disappear merely because a parent model's draw call stops.

Boundary

These are source-level observations from steam-678ccb46, not fresh EDF6 visual or damage tests. In particular, a named explosion helper may invoke camera shaking or other sub-effects but its mere presence does not establish damage volume, sound cue, or player hit rules. Examine the caller's separate damage and audio operations for those facts.
