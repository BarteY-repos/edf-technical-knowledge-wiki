EDF5 allied NPC combat: target search, burst line-of-sight and fire conditions

EDF 5Game technical referencesAllied NPC combat
Technical reference · Source analysis only (unless a source fact is contributor-verified); no new EDF runtime test

EDF5 soldier AI separates target acquisition from line of sight and trigger authorization. This explains why a soldier can face or retain a distant enemy without continuously shooting.

  1. The hostile-target search runs approximately once every 60–119 frames. It visits teams hostile to the soldier's own team and retains the nearest eligible object as a weak reference. The native scan has no explicit search-distance ceiling or line-of-sight condition in this phase.
  2. A separate visibility ray begins roughly 1.5 metres above the soldier, points toward the target and is capped at 300 m. The source's collision query uses layer 22.
  3. The ray stage operates in blocks: about 30–44 frames of active checking followed by 60–89 frames of delay. Its visibility byte can be cleared while off-cycle or in other character states.
  4. The engage state finally gates the trigger on visibility, facing (dot product greater than 0.8) and distance up to max(25, 0.5 × first weapon's range), provided no conflicting face-order is active.

These are control-logic thresholds in the studied native build, not a promise that the gun's projectiles physically travel exactly this range, nor a full model of every NPC subclass. External forced-target mission commands can be additional inputs.

Source: edf5/soldier-ai.md, fact edf5-target-and-fire (static disassembly, exact build steam-5d11de53). The note compares the logic with EDF6 but does not claim byte-identical class layouts.
