Overview

MDB carries model geometry and a hierarchy of objects, bones and material references. The vertex declaration tells the engine how to interpret each mesh stream; a plausible-looking vertex array is not enough without matching indices, weights and material slots.

Compatibility rules

Preserve vertex declarations and bone hierarchy. When a skeleton is changed, re-evaluate animation bind pose, SHKT physics anchors, sockets and script/object references together. The documented retargeting workflow moves joints at both ends and rest-pose definitions rather than translating one bone in isolation.

Successful MDB parsing does not prove correct rendering, physics or navigation. Typed loader mismatch documents an example in which a valid MDB was handed to the wrong consumer.
