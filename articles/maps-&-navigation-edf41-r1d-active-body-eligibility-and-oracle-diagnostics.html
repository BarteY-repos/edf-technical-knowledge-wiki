EDF 4.1 navigation reconstruction: collision-body eligibility versus group ranking

ConversionMaps & NavigationNavigationEDF 4.1EDF 6
Technical reference · Source geometry and historical oracle; active-body filter unresolved

A source map may expose the correct set of geometry groups without exposing the correct active collision bodies within those groups. This distinction is central to the studied EDF4.1→EDF6 NW_HILLYCITY_LIGHT navigation reconstruction.

Current technical correction

A later source-semantic reconstruction does reproduce the exact historical 4,971 body-level footprint census from 4,994 pre-dedupe instances and 23 FixB semantic removals, while retaining the exact 65/1,405/251/1,153/1 group/placement/rotation census. It is an offline candidate, not recovered historical source or byte-exact final R1D HKT. The R75 best natural candidate is 381,544 bytes (+32 from the historical target); the 381,528-byte (+16) path uses a non-natural group swap. Selected-source audits through R100 close simple body-role, source-base, MAPB placement identity and shared Havok-object alias hypotheses in this corpus. See current R1D authority rules, MAPO transform evidence, and source identity/audit details.

The older sections below are retained as historical diagnostics from before the R32/R75 source recovery. They describe then-open hypotheses, not the latest pending tests.

What is actually reproduced

A source-static group rank based on geometry transformed by each body's motionState reproduces the retained historical selection census:

Measured quantity Reconstructed / historical
Source-static selected groups 65
Selected placements 1,405
Exact-zero rotations 251
Yaw-only rotations 1,153
Mixed X/Z rotations 1
Historical active body footprints 4,971, still unresolved

These equalities do not prove the historical algorithm has been recovered. Multiple group selections can yield the same aggregated placement census, and group membership alone does not determine the eligible GroupGeometry.pieces set.

The hard distinction between the historical note and retained code

The historical classifier was described as testing a body-level condition max_xz >= 5.75. The retained R1/R1B harness, however, ranks full source-static GroupGeometry, counts primitive pieces and contains no persisted body-filter implementation. A later reconstructed filter is therefore a hypothesis about missing logic, not proven source code.

The latest recovered natural experiment ranks the full geometry by XZ area, requires at least one active raw body and then applies the raw max_xz body filter. It reproduces the historical placement/rotation census but produces 5,026 footprints: 55 too many. A prior R18 baseline produced 4,948: 23 too few under a different candidate. The figures are results of different semantics, not a monotonically converging universal threshold.

Further structural searches did not find a natural category of the 13–14 problematic body cases whose contributions simply account for the missing 55. One remaining known parser/body failure (sk_car_bus01_hk.polymsh3) must stay fail-closed; adding arbitrary bodies to hit the count is not evidence.

Why the exact 4,971 matters

The historical R1D target removes 277 triangles from the prior exact R1C navigation output, with 1,562 vertices and 2,651 triangles. A candidate already matched the tile counts and exact NVMD, yet the LOW HKT remained 16 bytes larger and differed in SHA-256. Matching geometry statistics, target byte length and runtime traversal are three separate tests.

For this reason a correct reproduction requires natural eligibility and exact source-backed ordering rather than numerical fitting. Do not change the 5.75 threshold, inject bodies manually, swap arbitrary groups or delete a seam record to make a length or SHA match.

Earlier proposed discriminator (subsequently audited)

Compare source hkpListShape cached AABB semantics against bounds recomputed from its children with real convex-radius and motion-state behavior; preserve the same population and group ranking rules. If that produces a natural 4,971, record the named group set, per-group body membership, geometry method and deterministic reproduction, not only a count.

Evidence: exact historical R1D oracle; EDF41_R1D_ACTIVE_BUILDER_FRONTIER_2026-11-14_R18.json and superseding R19; associated ListShape-family and linked-primitive censuses. Status: unresolved offline reverse engineering; not an exact nav or runtime PASS.
