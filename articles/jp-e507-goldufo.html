E507 Gold UFO ammunition records and unknown fields

ConversionCommunity Japanese WorkbookGameplay & AIEDF 5EDF 6
Technical reference · Translated contributed workbook notes; sample data, negative experiments and explicitly labeled inferences

Two separately recorded profiles

The Japanese source contains a bullet_big_bomber_info pointer example for E507_GOLDUFO_BIG.SGO (解析!A1665:A1690) and a longer bullet_info XML-like record for E507_GOLDUFO_ACE (解析!A2037:J2086). They must not be assumed to share a single identical parser or flat ABI merely because their asset identifiers begin with E507.

ACE bullet_info record excerpt

The source's comments identify only a subset of its positions:

Record index Sample value Source label / interpretation
0 integer 30 Burst projectiles per attack, according to note
1 integer 1 fireinterval
2 integer 1 firecount
4 float 0.40000001 ammosize
5 float 30 Bullet speed (弾速)
7 integer 15 ammoalive
13 float 0 Changing this reportedly makes attacks aim above or below the target

The nested ptr at index 6 contains additional integers/floats whose purpose is unknown. The author noted that one inner integer sample 8 (bullet_info[6][2][0] if the XML nesting is read literally) caused a crash as soon as the aircraft spawned when changed (解析!A2060:J2062). This is a critical negative experiment; do not treat the value as a safely tunable color/selector enum. The author also says that a nearby float is probably not ammocolor (J2052:J2054).

Implementation rules

  • Preserve unknown pointer nesting and indices when round-tripping SGO/XML; do not “normalize” it into a documented bullet class schema without evidence.
  • A field annotated ammoalive does not automatically establish seconds, frames or lifetime tick units; preserve raw values until runtime measurement.
  • Unchanged native structs are the safe default for fields whose mutation is reported to crash on spawn.
  • Keep GOLDUFO_BIG's bullet_big_bomber_info separate from GOLDUFO_ACE's bullet_info. They are different textual source excerpts.

Source: mod製作メモ.xlsx, 解析!A1665:A1690, A2037:J2086. The source includes uncertain annotations and reported experiments, not a full binary reverse engineering proof for these records. No original resource binary is redistributed.
