EDF6 FieldRender/FMEP: source records, terrain normals and evidence limits

EDF 6Maps & NavigationMaps
Source-backed research / case study · Current-suite exact source contract; runtime parity remains FieldRender-specific

At a glance

What it is: A terrain leaf does not necessarily refer directly to one global material ID. The studied FieldRender path uses local slots, per-field material data and texture arrays.

Where it fits: The article joins native/source record measurements, shader-derived calculations and a distinct non-native preview comparison.

Important distinction: Keep these evidence kinds separate: preview similarity does not prove native final-frame composition, and a local slot index is not a transferable global material ID.

EDF6 FieldRender terrain is a separate material/resource family from ordinary MDB character or static-map materials. This page distinguishes observed native/source records from a later non-native material-preview translation; the latter is not an engine specification.

Native-resource record structure in the bounded samples

The FMEP structural audit used a source Mass record width of 0x50 bytes and MaterialIDList width of 0x10 bytes. In the studied FIX3 source/profile it reported:

  • FMB version 0x110 and 21 shader-configuration rows in that specific record set;
  • a type-2 leaf containing 2 triangles and 4 vertices with a flat zero-height bounding box;
  • a local material list of width 2, shader capacity 2, configuration index 4;
  • shader configuration row 4 (2,1,0,0,0,0,0) for the tested planar FieldRender2 path;
  • packed terrain corner weights 127/127, sum 254, consistent with the observed quantization;
  • source texture-array DDS records 2048 × 2048, 12 mip levels, color BC1/DXGI 71 and normal BC3/DXGI 77.

These are bounded structural observations of a specific resource profile. They are not universal capacities of every EDF6 terrain profile and do not establish a visually correct in-game render.

Terrain normals and logical material values: attribution limit

Controlled native MAP_FIELD_RENDER pixel-shader comparisons in R4 (17 September 2026) established the following packed-normal decode; a later non-native preview translation reproduced it. This is static DXBC-derived behaviour, not yet proof of the final G-buffer/compositor output:

x = 2 * (R * B - 0.5)
y = -2 * (G - 0.5)
z = sqrt(max(1 - x*x - y*y, 1e-5))

The later preview translation also emitted logical metallic=0, roughness=0.980133056640625, specular=0. These logical material values do not independently establish the exact native output channels or final-frame shader. In particular, the bridge's 12 local RGBA8 slot/weight transport is not proven to be the game's original GPU texture layout. Full original implementation details are kept outside the public wiki in the R97 maintainer audit.

For comparison, other measured Common/Water paths used different R*A/G normal decoding; no single cross-family normal decoder is justified.

What the FIX3 comparison cannot prove

The retained FIX3 MAC/MAD/RAB resource carriers differed substantially from the earlier FIX2 version, and the loader path changed too. FIX3 versus FIX2 was not a clean single-variable shader experiment. Structural acceptance and valid nonblack DDS contents did not establish in-game pixel output. The exact native FieldRender compositor/texture bindings still require a matched runtime shader/capture observation.

See shader evidence and deferred compositor scope.

Exhaustive FMB census and non-terminating material slots

A later complete native HEIGEN FMB census validated 1,200/1,200 leaves (plus a 102-leaf CAVE control) across geometry, compressed payloads, mass grids, material selector bounds and sparse material lists. The 16-byte MaterialIDList is not zero/FF terminated: 0xFF denotes an empty position, and active local material count must count all non-0xFF entries. A leaf's separate capacity-like descriptor is not always numerically identical to that count. Full details and the uncertain native role of the separate _2 FMB layer are given in native FMB leaf structure.

The specific experimental shader selector 0x0202 with configuration row 4 is also present in 80/118 native ENKEIRIGHT leaves. That supports native shader selection but does not resolve differences between non-native and native material/orientation records. A preview's apparent normal or colour cannot substitute for actual renderer evidence.

Native ENKEIRIGHT projection/orientation reference

An exact retained ENKEIRIGHT material 0 uses base/color slice 0, detail slice 1, normal slice 0 and two orientation entries with (90,0,0) Euler angles and XYZ scales 7 and 100. The accompanying native planar-projection reference gives the complete source-field comparison and its evidence limits. A matching shader selector does not prove material/orientation equality.

FieldRender in the five-family shader qualification boundary

FieldRender is one of the five families covered by the retained EDF6 shader research. A native shader-oracle inventory that also contains Common, Common Clip, Common Light and Water should not be read as evidence that all five share one material ABI. Terrain uses FMEP/FMB leaf/material selectors and texture-array registrations rather than the ordinary static MDB sampler contract.

The static terrain evidence and experimental renderer translation answer different questions. Reconstructed shader selectors and native DDS slice ranges can validate source-resource interpretation. A Godot preview's 12 local RGBA slot/weight transport is a translation, not a captured native EDF6 SRV/sampler layout. A runtime test must bind a known native FieldRender leaf and verify shader bytecode, actual SRVs, samplers, cbuffer lanes, RTV targets and downstream pixel output. Without that chain, the preview formula and exact final-compositor behavior remain deliberately separate.

See EDF6 shader static/runtime proof matrix and native FieldRender DDS and FMB leaf records.

FieldRender native shader inputs: consumed offsets, padding and DDS interpretation

Static analysis of 315 native FieldRender primary pixel-shader variants recovered the actual g_materials constant-buffer read set, expressed as byte offsets within a GPU material record. This is an observed read footprint, not a declaration that every byte in the source FMEP material structure has GPU meaning:

Location in one GPU material recordStatic shader useConstraint
+0x00, +0x10, +0x18, +0x1C, +0x20, +0x30, +0x40Observed g_materials read offsets across the 315 primary PS variantsByte offsets of reads, not independently proven user-facing parameter names or per-record source-structure offsets
+0x2C, +0x3C, +0x4CNo observed consumer in the native FieldRender shader familyPadding dwords must not be populated with invented shader controls
Source material auxiliary floats +0x44..+0x4CSource-side fields do not automatically map to extra GPU shader inputsSource-record offsets and GPU constant-buffer offsets are different address spaces

The invariant secondary pixel shader and both invariant vertex shaders did not consume g_materials in the retained shader census. The R4 exact terrain projection also normalizes the incoming geometric normal, but does not normalize orientation rows before their dot products; it clamps axis-dot magnitude to 1e-5 before applying the un-clamped m_correction exponent, and uses a separate 0.98000002 basis orthogonalization factor. These rules belong to the native static shader program, unlike the Godot-only packed ID/weight preview transport.

Native sampled texture settings: the recovered g_color_array and g_N_array samplers use linear minification/magnification/mipmap filtering and wrap on U/V/W. The studied disk color DDS identifies DXGI format 71 = BC1_UNORM, not 72 = BC1_UNORM_SRGB; disk format alone does not resolve the in-game shader-resource view's color-space override. In a native mass cell there are 12 local MaterialIDList selectors and four corners of 12 byte-weights each, with potentially empty slots. The preview representation of three RGBA8 IDs and three RGBA8 weight textures is not a proven native SRV arrangement.

Authority: exact static R4/R6 FieldRender checkpoints from 17 September 2026; runtime SRV binding, rasterizer/depth policy and final G-buffer composition remain unverified.

They are reported, not independently checked against a pinned EDF6 output-writing pixel-shader variant and actual render-target state. The 0.980133056640625 roughness is not the 0.98000002 normal-basis adjustment proven in R4: these constants have different roles. Nor are the normal Z reconstruction, directional projection and normal-accumulation 1e-5 safeguards interchangeable with the color-denominator 1e-7.

Projection authority retained: R4 distinguishes Mode 0 (one projection), Mode 1 (two directional projections), and Mode 2 (three projections). In Mode 2 the row1-axis projection selects material/orientation slot 1 when dot(N,row1(pair0.color)) > 0, otherwise slot 2; the selected face uses (row0,row2). The separate pair-0 side projections still use (row2,row1) and (row0,row1). The geometric normal is normalized before weighting, but native orientation rows are not normalized before their dot products. An external two-mode summary cannot supersede these controlled DXBC observations.

Verification still needed: pin the exact native MAP_FIELD_RENDER shader permutation and output signature; inspect stores/swizzles, render-target formats and write masks for all G-buffer outputs; resolve viewXYZ_id packing, g_world_matrix convention, and later light/compositor passes. A reported specularRGB of zero does not imply the terrain receives no reflections or other specular contribution in the finished image. See deferred shader-pipeline semantics and native planar orientation example.

FieldRender native-name lookup: mass grid and texture arrays

For source searches, the important terrain inputs are g_mass, g_mass_origin, g_mass_size_xz, g_mass_divide_count_xz and g_material_id_list. The September R4/R6 static shader reconstruction selects a mass-grid cell from surface/world XZ, clamps its grid coordinates, and uses the fractional part of the grid position to interpolate the four corner weights. In explanatory notation:

gridPosition = (position.xz - g_mass_origin.xz) / g_mass_size_xz.xy
cell = clamp(int2(gridPosition), 0, g_mass_divide_count_xz - 1)
fraction = frac(gridPosition)

g_material_id_list refers to the terrain leaf's local material mapping, not a global material-number space. The native decoded FMB mesh stores a 0x10-byte sparse mapping (0xFF means empty slot) and a separate shader layer count; the studied R4/R6 translation used a particular 12-slot preview transport. Those numbers must not become one alleged native 12-material hard limit. A slot being addressable does not mean it is occupied or has nonzero weight. The shader-reconstructed path bilinearly interpolates four RGBA8 corner weights and scales by 1/255; the final material-color sum uses the retained sumWeights + 1e-7 guard. Native EDF6 has separately identified 1–15-layer shader permutations; see the FMB layer/count distinction. Counts from one preview or one leaf are not format-wide maxima.

The terrain shader's position-projected g_color_array and g_N_array supply color/detail and normal samples, rather than requiring ordinary per-vertex FMB UVs. Projection Modes 0, 1 and 2 retain the specific axis/plane assignments described above. The R×B, inverted-G normal reconstruction and +Y 1e-5 accumulation protection belong to this FieldRender family, not automatically to Water. These searchable names restate existing R4/R6 findings and the reconciled 17 November ground-shader intake; they are not a new native-runtime qualification.
