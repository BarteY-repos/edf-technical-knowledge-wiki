MDB version (key: MDB header version)

  • edf41: MDB v20 (0x14); vertex declaration format 24 = 4-byte half2 TEXCOORD | ref: edf41/file-formats.md#mdb-version | evidence: static
  • edf5: not recorded in the hub yet (public tools: Smileynator's Blender addon and AUK233 edf-tools handle EDF5 MDB; ops/community-tools.md) | ref: none | evidence: unverified
  • edf6: MDB v32 (0x20) | ref: edf6/file-formats.md#mdb-version | evidence: static
  • Differences noticed: the EDF41-to-EDF6 project converts v20 -> v32 offline (all 44 TESTLIGHT models, the 9 Hector MDB, and 667 map models representable); its first version emitted zero bytes for the format 24 lane, fixed later.
  • Confidence in the mapping: medium (converter-level; converted models were loaded in game as part of a map, but the map crashed later in collision).
  • Added: 2026-10-15 src: EDF41-to-EDF6 handoff (shared by Bartey)
