EDF5-derived underground mission map URI and LIST flag census

ConversionMissions & ScriptingMissionsEDF 5EDF 6
Technical reference · Audited source-derived EDF5 mission AC map-reference and EDF6-compatible converted LIST flags, offline/static; bounded user runtime samples reported separately

Evidence: Static R4 source-derived mission-script Map() reference census and the corresponding converted EDF6-compatible OFFLINE/ONLINE MISSIONLIST rows, dated 2 September 2026. This is not a newly verified raw-shipping EDF5 LIST dump, nor a runtime test of all missions.

Two distinct identities

A mission has at least two identifiers here: its logical mission name (M001, DM019, etc.) and the exact virtual resource path supplied to the mission's map call (app:/Map/...mac). The EDF6-compatible MISSIONLIST adds a separate mode-specific ordinal and a numeric flag field. A change to one identity does not automatically update the others.

The source-derived audit found 15 distinct underground missions: 11 base, three DLC1, one DLC2. Each appears in both OFFLINE and ONLINE after the studied shared-progress list unification, yielding 30 mode-specific LIST rows. In this specific converted corpus every one carries flags=40 (0x28, ordinary 0x08 plus underground/no-support 0x20). Surface rows retain 8. This is an exact corpus observation, not a license to assign 40 to every cave-like map in other EDF versions.

The complete 30-row dataset is also included as data/EDF5_DERIVED_UNDERGROUND_MAPS_R4_30_MODE_ROWS.csv in the public archive. It preserves pack, mode, zero-based ordinal, exact-case map path and converted LIST flag.

Why underground appearance and support eligibility are different

The studied native EDF6 MissionScriptASImplement path interprets LIST bit 0x20 as a no-support condition. Equipment may still appear red or be selectable in the loadout UI. The gameplay question is whether an Air Raider support/vehicle request succeeds inside the mission, after the active mode/list selection and map initialization.

Historic diagnostics exposed why this matters: a broad modded UI setter affected 23 unrelated call sites, and a separate experimental host-side mode refresh was runtime-negative for the reported restriction symptom. A proposed direct SetWeaponCondition(WEAPON_CONDITION_NO_SUPPORT) after Map() calls was a static-only candidate. These experiments do not supersede the source-derived row values or prove a game-wide rule for all online hosts.

The parent underground weapon-condition article documents later bounded user in-game observations for M000, M001 and DM019, separately from the 15-mission R4 offline census. Do not count 30 static LIST rows as 30 successful runtime missions.

What this table cannot prove

  • That all 15 modes/mission pairs ran successfully in EDF6 or enforce the correct support rules in multiplayer.
  • That case-distinct strings refer to different or identical resources after CRI/VFS normalization.
  • That the R4 generated LIST bytes are unmodified source EDF5 shipping LIST bytes.
  • That a DLL or mod loader may safely infer the selected mission solely from physical MissionPack parity.
