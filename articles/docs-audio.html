Overview

Audio spans cue names, bank lookup, stream payloads, positional playback, voice/text ownership and resource lifetime. A correct playback ID is not enough if its bank is missing, loaded under a conflicting name or freed while still referenced.

Cross-version notes

An EDF4.1 audio port studied 1,469 source SE entries, including collisions and names absent from EDF6. Its cue map is a case-specific corpus, not a promise that all future banks have the same identity. Different bank kinds require distinct handling, and resource ownership must be respected on shutdown. For generic mission dialogue, match the cue, subtitle and timing systems separately.
