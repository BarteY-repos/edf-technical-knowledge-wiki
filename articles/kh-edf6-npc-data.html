Observations

NPC weapon damage versus Japanese wiki tables

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: npc-weapon-damage-half-wiki

Every EDF6 NPC soldier weapon's AmmoDamage is twice the JP wiki's figure "at Normal and up", for all 24 NPC weapon rows of catalog/names-npcs.md (for example aisoldierrifle01 5 = wiki 2.5, aisoldiershotgun01 3 x 10 = wiki 1.5 x 10, aisoldiersniperrifle02 25 = wiki 12.5); EDF5's wiki gave the SGO's full value, and EDF6's wiki says its Rocket Launcher figure (100 for the SGO's 200) is half EDF5's. So an EDF6 NPC deals half its weapon's AmmoDamage at Normal and up if the wiki measured it (the factor's code is unread).

Basis: weapon SGOs read with File_Storage/Logs/catalog/npc6_info.py against the wiki's tables

Attribution: KH chat 117 (hub read) · 2026-11-12

Proposed NPC-vehicle mission_setup weapon scalingSource: unverified

Evidence: unverified · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: npc-vehicle-mission-setup-factor

An NPC-driven vehicle SGO's mission_setup[0] is a pair whose second value scales its weapons' damage: EDF6's v504_begaruta_ai and v504_begaruta_g_ai (Nix) hold [1, 0.8] where EDF5's same SGOs hold [1, 1], and the JP wiki's EDF6 NPC Nix figures (Revolver Cannon 4.0, Missile Pod 48; "20% lower than the last game") are the weapon SGOs' 5 and 60 x 0.8, while the Gravis (v608_oldrobot_ai, [1, 1], same weapon SGOs) is listed at 5.0 and 60. Other values: v603_flak_weak_ai [1, 0.5] (v603_flak_ai [1, 1]), vehicle403_tank_mission [1, 0.6], v603_flak_mission [1.25, 1], v614_proteus_mk2, _mission and vehicle407_bigbegaruta_flame_ai, _mission [3, 3.5]; every other NPC vehicle [1, 1]. The first value's use is unread (a durability factor is a guess). Inferred from the data and the wiki only; the reader of mission_setup (vehicles.md#vehicle-setup-readers, 62D6E0) was not followed for this pair.

Basis: File_Storage/Logs/catalog/veh6_info.py (prints mission_setup[0]), names6_seed.py --diff v504_begaruta_ai (EDF5 vs EDF6)

Attribution: KH chat 118 (hub read), JP wiki atwiki edf6 page 343 · 2026-11-12

NPC vehicle durability compared with Japanese wiki

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: npc-vehicles-wiki-match

The JP wiki's NPC vehicle tables match the SGOs on base durability for every vehicle it lists (Blacker 700, Varius 700, KG6 Kepler 455, B651 Titan 2400, Epsilon 1600, EMC 1000, Naegling 550, Gun Wagon 650, Caliban 1500, Nix / Gravis / Eiren 1200, BMX10 Proteus 7500, War Barga and Armament Barga 40000) and on base damage for all but one (Blacker 70, Varius 350, Kepler 1.0, Titan 750, Epsilon 300, EMC 10 x 180, Gun Wagon 10 penetrating, Combat Wagon flamethrower 2.5, Eiren lasers 0.15, Proteus Laser Cannon 6, Armament Barga's Copper Cannon "NPC 100"); the exception is the Proteus's Buster Cannon, wiki 60 against v_407bigbegaruta_cannon_ai's 35 with mission_setup[0] [1, 1] (not settled). The Combat Wagons' (v607_robotruck_*_ai) durability is a per-difficulty list [700, 700, 2100] (the wiki: "2100?").

Basis: veh6_info.py against the wiki's tables

Attribution: KH chat 118 (hub read), JP wiki atwiki edf6 page 343 · 2026-11-12
