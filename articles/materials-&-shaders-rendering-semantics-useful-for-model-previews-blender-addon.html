EDF6 model shading semantics: albedo, normal, R/M, occlusion and lighting

UniversalMaterials & ShadersShadersEDF 5EDF 6
Source-backed research / case study · Reconciled

This article records game-oriented material/shader semantics from the supplied rendering description, supplemented where noted by independent native MDB samples and shader analysis. A practical preview that resembles EDF is not itself a proof of the exact final-frame GPU pipeline.

Base color, color masks and cutout

The supplied rendering description gives the model/BRDF base color as:

float3 color = albedo.Sample(uv).rgb * diffuse;

For param_cm0_cm1, it describes sequential change-color interpolation:

float2 mask = param_cm0_cm1.Sample(uv).rg;
float3 color = lerp(diffuse, change_color0, mask.r);
color = lerp(color, change_color1, mask.g);
color *= albedo.Sample(uv).rgb;

This is not equivalent to an arbitrary overall tint. The examined Common_Clip predicate is if (albedoAlpha < alpha_clip) discard. The studied 44 snd_BRDF_Map_Common_Clip materials used alpha_clip=0.5 in 42 cases and 0.3435 in 2 cases.

Tangent-space normal reconstruction

A commonly documented model normal path samples packed channels and reconstructs Z:

float nx = R * A * 2 - 1;
float ny = G * 2 - 1;
float nz = sqrt(max(0.00001, 1 - nx*nx - ny*ny));
nx *= normal_amount;
ny *= normal_amount;

The tangent/binormal/geometric-normal transform and final normalization follow this decode. normal_amount=0 removes tangent-plane perturbation; negative values invert the perturbation; values greater than one exaggerate it. These are direction changes before normalization, not direct stored-normal length multipliers. FieldRender is a separate material path, and its Godot preview's alternate decoder must not be promoted into a native EDF6 compositor formula without a shader capture.

Packed metallic, roughness and special channels

For the documented Common-family param_r_m_* channels: metallic = G × material.metallic, and roughness = R × material.roughness. In the _hr variant, roughness also uses texture alpha: R × A × roughness.

specular_color participates in the specular/Fresnel term, rather than simply tinting diffuse albedo. specular_diffuse_blend is likewise a shader-family semantic, not an ordinary texture slot. Occlusion in the supplied rendering description can be carried in the magnitude of the logical surface normal: normalXYZ = normalizedSurfaceNormal × occlusion. Interpreting this purely as an ordinary glTF-style AO multiplier would not reproduce the described pipeline.

Normal-length interpretation and light response

In the supplied compositor description, normal magnitude k = length(normalXYZ) scales some direct-light terms and affects ambient handling. The clarified description distinguishes a near-zero k < ~0.001 alternate path and profile-specific point-light response. These are source-description claims; they have not been independently re-derived for every shader family in the game build. Do not assume they apply to every special material such as Water, FieldRender or Glass.

Light and emission are family-specific

For the studied Common_Light material, the retained evidence is compatible with a light-mask × light_color emissive channel. That observation does not justify assigning one emission formula to every EDF6 material. Similarly, snd_BRDF_Map_Build_Window and snd_Map_Object_Glass have distinct native parameter/sample contracts.

Evidence limits

The equations above come from a rendering description and bounded native material/shader observations. They are useful for interpreting EDF assets, but the exact render-target binding and final post-lighting composition remain unproven for some families. See rendering evidence reconciliation and FieldRender evidence limits.
