EDF6 healing: pickups, team rescue, support buffs and recorded heal credit

EDF 6Game technical referencesDamage and combat
Technical reference · Contributor static analysis / source-graded observations; no new game runtime in R124

The original EDF6 combat code distinguishes increasing HP, reviving a player, providing a support buff and crediting a healer's battle statistics. These actions share some recipients but are not interchangeable and do not all emit the same network or achievement events.

Ordinary healing and pickup recovery

The native AddHP routine clamps HP between a target's allowed minimum and maximum. It refuses to heal a target in a fully dead state. EDF6 recovery crates (type 2 small, type 3 large) restore 15% and 30% of maximum HP, respectively, on the ordinary collection path. The pickup handler can also heal the picker’s living follower NPCs with a separate configured fraction.

These recovery paths do not necessarily credit a soldier's recorded HealDamage statistic. Source RE explicitly notes that health pickups and revives do not send the same healer-credit message as healing weapons.

Healing messages and credit

A healing hit can be expressed as negative base damage in the hit record. When it is applied, the game can send 0x10000007 to the healer with the target and float amount restored. Native contributors found additional sources of that event:

  • SupportUnitBullet01 type 0 (e.g. a healing support device)
  • the Vehicle507_Rescuetank healing its riders and attributing it to the driver
  • hit processing on a remote target, where the message amount may initially be an estimate because this machine does not own the actual HP

The receiving soldier handler increments HealDamage only when the healer is player-controlled, the target is not self, their teams are not hostile and the target accepts the credited category. Self-healing by a Life Vent therefore does not automatically count in the same way as healing an ally.

Revival moves HP between players

The native player-rescue function gives the revived player half of the rescuer's current HP, subject to the revived player's HP bounds, clears its dead-state flags and restores team participation. The rescuer loses that amount through AddHP(negative value). It is not equivalent to picking up a large health crate or adding the same HP to both participants.

Support is not always recovery

SupportUnitBullet01 types 1–3 are buff operations, not direct HP restoration: the source describes one as increasing a target attribute, another as lowering incoming-damage multiplier and another as increasing a further gameplay multiplier. Their object fields are build-specific; the source does not prove a universal UI label for every subtype.

Evidence boundary

All named routines and message fields are from the investigated EDF6 binary, not a new rescue or online healing test. The source warns that the healer-credit message can be produced even in an edge case with an amount at or below zero; do not treat every observed event as proof that a player gained HP.
