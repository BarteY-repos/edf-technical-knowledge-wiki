EDF 6 — Wiki Checks (community source notes)

EDF 6Community Knowledge HubTroubleshooting
Source-backed research / case study · Contributed research; per-claim static, verified, unverified; not independently re-tested

This is a source-attributed research appendix, not a chronology or a replacement for topic reference pages. The observations were contributed in the EDF Knowledge Hub export, snapshot 2026-11-14, revision 54f89cd, compiled by novae (ajane).

Read each statement with its evidence grade and exact build. Verified means a reported game test, static means reverse engineering or source inspection without a reported game test, and unverified is a hypothesis. None of these observations was newly tested for this wiki release.

Mission pages

Japanese wiki coverage of 206 EDF6 missions

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: edf6-wiki-mission-pages

The EDF6 Japanese wiki's mission pages hold all 206 missions the game lists: 147 for the main campaign (pages 50, 73, 97-99, 102, 103, 105-108, 339, 340, 348, 349), 19 for Mission Pack 1 (466, 468) and 40 for Mission Pack 2 (492, 495-497), each an h2 M<n> <title> numbered as the game's list (EDF6's offline and online campaign lists are the same order), with no gap or repeat; every Japanese title equals the game's title for that number (NFKC, the † loop marks set aside) except six wiki placement notes (#edf6-wiki-late-missions) and Mission Pack 1's M4, which the wiki writes 市街浸食 for the game's 市街侵食 (a wiki typo). Each page's ミッション概要 table gives the map name, whether transport and attack requests work (輸送・攻撃要請), multiplayer weapon-level and armor caps per difficulty, then the allies (出現する味方), enemies (出現する敵) and, in 16 missions, placed objects (出現する配置物: boardable vehicles, once NPC squads) by wave with counts and notes; it gives no weather.

Basis: atwiki_extract6.py (206 missions, every one with an overview table, 2504 + 196 + 612 rows), atwiki6_check.py against catalog/missions.md

Attribution: KH chat 131 (curator), EDF6 atwiki mission pages (saved 2026-11-12) · 2026-11-12

Campaign mission order discrepancies around M138

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: edf6-wiki-late-missions

The campaign list's rows 138-143 (after M137, before the last four) are missions the wiki places earlier in the story, its titles noting where: M138 Unchanging Days II (変わらぬ日々’’, m033_5) "between M40 and M41", M139 Maximum Power (極大勢力, m130c) between M76 and M77, M140 Invasion of the Mechanical Soldiers (機兵大侵攻, m131) between M134 and M135, M141 The Treacherous Summit I (危機迫る山頂’, m140b) between M118 and M119, M142 Town of Darkness (灯火なき街, m114b) between M128 and M129, M143 Pursuer (追跡者, m081b) between M130 and M131. The game's own list holds them in list order with no such note; what unlocks them is not read.

Basis: the wiki's h2 titles against catalog/missions.md (atwiki6_check.py)

Attribution: KH chat 131 (curator), EDF6 atwiki mission pages (saved 2026-11-12) · 2026-11-12

Enemy totals against the scripts

First offline enemy-count evaluator comparison

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: edf6-wiki-vs-scripts

Enemy totals per difficulty, _tools/ac_eval.py (offline, first attempt) against the EDF6 Japanese wiki's mission tables (all 206 missions, enemy rows summed per difficulty band): equal on every difficulty in 128 (83 as they stand, 45 once the counting rules of #edf6-wiki-vs-scripts-rules are applied), at Inferno only in 8, different in 70: 52 of those spawn through enemy generators (#edf6-wiki-vs-scripts-generators), 19 of them and 8 others have wiki rows with no number (∞, n, -, 多数 "many", phase headings in the count column), and 10 differ with neither (#edf6-wiki-vs-scripts-small). The matches name the totals the catalog's mission pages print as the wiki players' own counts.

Basis: scratch File_Storage/Logs/catalog/atwiki_cmp6.py (output atwiki_cmp6_out.txt, --rows per mission) over extract/missions.json and the evaluator's records

Attribution: KH chat 132 (curator), EDF6 atwiki mission pages (saved 2026-11-12) · 2026-11-12

EDF6 Japanese wiki versus AC counting rules

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: edf6-wiki-vs-scripts-rules

Counting rules that bring the EDF6 wiki's mission totals to the scripts' (#edf6-wiki-vs-scripts): (1) the wiki lists background and story ships the scripts make with CreateNeutral (not an enemy spawn) as enemy rows, often in a 演出のみ "cutscene only" wave: Carriers e508_carrier*, Time Ships e611_timeship*, landing craft e512_dropboat, the Ring e614_grandmother (M9 Visitors, m007: 72 = 49 frogs, 22 Time Ships and the Ring), Motherships e511_mothership_light and the Fortress e510_fortress; (2) a Time Ship pod (.EDF6_TimeShip_CreatePodCrue, the wiki's ドロップシップ) is a row of its own beside its crew, one per call; (3) a Ring arm (リング砲台, the script's e614_grandmother_arm_* part) is one row and its turrets (レーザー砲台, プラズマ砲台 ...) more rows the scripts do not spawn (M82 The Big Day (7), m066, 66 / 84 = the script's 42 / 44 + 24 / 40 turrets; M115, m099, 233 + 52); (4) the wiki writes a band as a note in the wave or name cell, 第三波 / (HARDEST以上), 赤アリ / (HARDEST以下).

Basis: atwiki_cmp6.py parts neu, pod and -turrets, each read side by side with --rows; the neutral SGOs counted over every script's Inferno records

Attribution: KH chat 132 (curator), EDF6 atwiki mission pages (saved 2026-11-12) · 2026-11-12

Ship and hive generator owner-row accounting

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: edf6-wiki-vs-scripts-generators

In the EDF6 wiki's mission tables a hive or ship generator's output is a row per owner with the generator's amount (SetEnemyGenerator count): M65 The Enemies Behind (m045_5) lists its 9 small hives with bees 2 x 1, 8 x 6 and red bees 8 x 2, the script's nine .SetEnemyGenerator settings (2, 8 x 6, 8 x 2 = 66); its total stays 2 under the script's (97 / 99 against 99 / 101) because the wiki leaves out the mission's 2 Shield Bearers (e513_shieldbearer; the wiki lists Shield Bearers in only 4 missions, the scripts spawn them in 7). Where the scripts also use SetInstantEnemyGenerator bursts or re-arm generators by event (M7 Hive Cleanup Mission, m005: 8 bursts of 25), the wiki's totals follow no single rule found; 52 of the 70 differing missions have generators, so their totals are not a check of the evaluator.

Basis: atwiki_cmp6.py --rows for M65 and M7, the evaluator's records by line

Attribution: KH chat 132 (curator), EDF6 atwiki mission pages (saved 2026-11-12) · 2026-11-12

Time Ship anchor drop counting change

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: edf6-wiki-anchor-rows

Since KH chat 133 _tools/ac_eval.py records each Time Ship anchor drop as a spawn of the ship's own anchor SGO (mission-spawns.md#ac-timeship-anchor-drop). The EDF6 wiki writes a dropping ship as one テイルアンカー (Tail Anchor) row, its generator output under it, no row for the ship: M24 Tower of Light (m028) lists 6 for the script's 6 e611_timeship and 6 drops; M36 Final Resistance (m029_5) 3 for 3. M92 Observe the Fleet (m108) and M121 Block the Transmission (m107) match with ships and anchors both counted, Mission Pack 2 M39 LV ZZZZ-Y Last Attack (m307) without its 3 drops (145 + 36 = 181); the totals in #edf6-wiki-vs-scripts stand, two missions moved each way.

Basis: atwiki_cmp6.py --all before and after the ac_eval change (atwiki_cmp6_out.txt, atwiki_cmp6_out_kh133.txt), --rows for M24

Attribution: KH chat 133 (curator), EDF6 atwiki mission pages (saved 2026-11-12) · 2026-11-12

Non-generator mission count discrepancies

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: edf6-wiki-vs-scripts-small

The EDF6 missions whose enemy totals differ from the wiki with no generator and every wiki row numbered (#edf6-wiki-vs-scripts): M96 Operation Bird-Buster (m083_8), wiki 8 against 7, lists the Siren's red form Glaukos (グラウコス) as a row of its own though e604_radon is one object holding both forms (catalog/names.md); M138 Unchanging Days II (m033_5) spawns 7 more black-ant eggs giantant01egg on every difficulty (64 / 44) than the wiki's rows (57 / 37); M94 Advance of the Machine Army (m083_4): grenadiers 50 against the wiki's 49; M52 Demons Descend (m047): 20 pod calls against 16 wiki pod rows (Kruul 42 / 48 and 9 ships equal); M25 Sniper Unit (rm025a): spiders 252 against the wiki's 262; not read: M68 The Shadows of Giants (m029_7, wiki 453 / 463 / 361 against 350 / 356 / 288), M129 (m051_5, 2), Mission Pack 2's 16, 22 and 27 (3, 4 and 4 at Inferno).

Basis: atwiki_cmp6.py --rows per mission

Map names

The EDF6 game shows no map names

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: edf6-wiki-map-names

The EDF6 game shows no map names; the wiki's mission tables give each map file that a listed mission loads exactly one name, except nw_kokyujyutaku01 (坂道のある街, once 坂道のある街(夜間) "night"): 39 files, 37 names. Names shared by two files: 浄水場のある街(荒廃) (nw_haikyo601, nw_haikyo602), 超高層ビル街(DLC) (nw_dlcmap601, nw_dlcmap602), 郊外 (nw_henden, nw_henden_winter). The wiki marks EDF6's changed EDF5 maps by a bracket: 荒廃 ruined (nw_haikyo601/602, nw_danti01_haikyo), 再開発後 redeveloped (nw_danti601, nw_rinkai01), 夜間 night (nw_seiyu_night); ig_heigen601 is 平原(殿軍専用), the plains of M64 Rear Guard only, and ig_2000mcity601 高層ビル街(バルガ戦用), the high-rise district for the Barga battle (M136). The wiki's map page (63) lists 33 maps (its heads, without the bracketed variants above but with both 平原 entries). English per name: catalog/names-ja-terms.md; per file: catalog/maps.md.

Basis: atwiki6_check.py (each mission's wiki map against the map its mission.ac loads), page 63's h3 headings

Attribution: KH chat 131 (curator), EDF6 atwiki mission and map pages (saved 2026-11-12) · 2026-11-12

Wiki words

Ambiguous wiki transport ship names resolved

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: edf6-wiki-transport-words

Two of the wiki's short names that could mean either transport are settled by the first mission each appears in: 揚陸艇 ("landing craft", first in M18) is the Cosmonaut Dropship e512_dropboat (whose first listed mission is M18), and ドロップシップ ("dropship", first in M46) is the Kruul Capsule e613_capsule (first listed in M46; the wiki's enemy page gives ドロップシップ as one of its aliases). 大型宇宙船 is the Time Ship e611_timeship (enemy page alias タイムシップ).

Basis: atwiki6_vocab.py (each name's first mission) against the SGO mission lists in catalog/objects-enemies.md

Attribution: KH chat 131 (curator), EDF6 atwiki (saved 2026-11-12) · 2026-11-12

The wiki's "ディロイB" marks the Deroys whose legs carry missile batteries

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: edf6-wiki-deroy-b

The wiki's "ディロイB" marks the Deroys whose legs carry missile batteries; its plain ディロイ sizes are the ones without. M130 Special Vehicle Unit: wiki (小) 2, B(小) 1, (中) 4, B(大) 2, B(特大) 1 against the script's e509_deiroi_s 2, _sm 1, e509_deiroi 4, _l 2, _xl 1; MP2 39: B(中) 2 = _m 2. M66 Descending Hostiles lands its Deroys through DeiroiFallEffect objects whose path names the Deroy (e509_deiroi_fall_l2 -> e509_deiroi_l2, and so for all eight): plain (大) 1 / 2 by difficulty = fall_l2 1 / 2, B(大) 1 = fall_l 1, B(特大) on Hardest and up = fall_xl 0 / 1, plain (中) 6 / 7 = fall and (小) 5 / 10 = fall_s; MP2 29's plain (特大), noted "extra large but no missile batteries", is fall_xl2. So e509_deiroi_l2 and _xl2, whose legs lack the missile segment (catalog/names.md), are the Large and Extra Large Deroys without missiles.

Basis: atwiki6_rows.py against catalog/missions-enemies.md; the fall SGOs' path read with the scratch sgo_strings6.py

Attribution: KH chat 131 (curator), EDF6 atwiki mission pages (saved 2026-11-12) · 2026-11-12

M125 Purple Haze wiki name and scripted class

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: edf6-wiki-names-settled

Wiki names settled against the scripts' spawns: M125's 赤ヘイズ (Hardest and up, 20) is the Purple Haze e618_squid_small_hi (the script's 20 on Hardest and Inferno only); M121's クラーケン(青) is a sixth beam Kraken e616_venus_ggss (wiki 1 + 5 against the script's 5 / 6), so M121's e616_venus_gs spawns none; M116's 黒アリ(噛み) (11, one "durability 60 on any difficulty") are its giantant01b (10; 210 ants in all on both sides; no script HP factor found for the one); MP1 5's 21 コクーン(小) are e663_spider_egg_sac_small at the script's durability x 0.2 (5 more at x 1 are its コクーン(中)), which is the wiki's 小型コクーン of base 250 (1250 x 0.2); MP2 10's 青アンドロイド(赤ビーム) / (青ビーム) are e602_berserker_beama / _beamb (the wiki's interpretations 1 and 2: red beam rapid fire, blue beam spread).

Basis: atwiki6_rows.py against catalog/missions-enemies.md and missions-hp-factors.md; the wiki's enemy entries (atwiki6_enemies.json)

Attribution: KH chat 131 (curator), EDF6 atwiki (saved 2026-11-12) · 2026-11-12

M130 boss and command-ship name resolution

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: edf6-wiki-names-settled-ships

Two wiki ship and boss words settled against the scripts: M130's コマンドシップ is the Mothership e511_mothership_light, the same SGO the wiki calls マザーシップ in M17 and M104 (its enemy page has both entries; the コマンドシップ one gives 729% drops for the "giant cannon and charger", the e511_mothership_genocide cannons' rate); スピネル (M146 Operation Ring-Cracker: Part 2, MP2 40 LV ZZZZ-Z The Ring) is the Ring e614_grandmother, the only boss those scripts spawn (the wiki's phases follow "Spinel durability"); the enemy page has no スピネル entry.

Basis: atwiki6_rows.py against catalog/missions-enemies.md and objects-enemies.md

Attribution: KH chat 131 (curator), EDF6 atwiki (saved 2026-11-12) · 2026-11-12
