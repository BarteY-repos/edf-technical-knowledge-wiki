15.1 DM019 mission semantics

  • The generic pending-expression bug was LIFO instead of source FIFO. The corrected engine gathers contiguous pending expressions and emits them in execution order. In the original 44-pair investigation, 35/44 generated outputs changed somewhere because of this generic ordering bug. R37 retains the FIFO implementation (for pe in reversed(pending) after stack collection) and a regression assertion for it.
  • DM019 enemy03 source order is GiantAnt04×70 → GiantAnt04sb×30 → GoldAnt×5; the old LIFO engine reversed it. enemy02 is different: below HARDEST it uses ordinary GiantAnt04×3, with GoldAnt×3 only on the higher branch. Thus on Easy the first golden trio is not expected, while the later five GoldAnt are unconditional in source.
  • Source DM019 creates three recruitable friend squads and immediately assigns their routes and speeds. There is no later source call that starts their routes only after recruitment. The relevant lowerings are global SetAiRoute(obj,route) and SetAiMoveSpeed(obj,speed); the 44 official-port corpus contained 708 global route calls and 514 global speed calls, with zero method-form instances in that corpus.
  • 2008 is CreateEnemyGroup_Area by opcode, not an SGO-dependent ground/flying heuristic. 2006 is the flying-area opcode. R37 formalizes this distinction and regression-tests both.
  • 2001→CreateEnemy2 remains runtime-confirmed for the wall anthill/spawner case. The lighting omission and spawner orientation were mission-semantic defects, not reasons to mutate map collision.

15.2 DM020 read-only globals

Source constructs tank01_1..tank01_4 only. tank01_5 and tank01_6 are later read/used, but no source constructor/store was found. Do not synthesize vehicles 5/6. Treat this as a declaration/type-inference problem; longer-term read-only global typing should be driven by use sites rather than a universal Object assumption.

15.3 EDFROOM NAVMESH evidence detail

  • Corrected graph decoders fixed a research-tool bug: narrow integer fields had sometimes been read at the wrong width and hkHalf16 could be misread as float32. Serializer output was unaffected where raw ITEM data was preserved, but old absurd decoded values are invalid evidence.
  • Static-tree root-cause theory was tested across all 10 low + 10 high DM019 tiles: one leaf/face, complete leaf permutation, all nodes reachable, every leaf AABB contains its face geometry, tree domain equals the face-vertex union, and no cycles/OOB/missing faces. This is why static-tree tuning is strongly deprioritized.
  • IG_EDFROOM01/02 do not have FMB. Their exact-pair RoomB work uses HKT/NVMD/MAD/MAC; do not ask for a nonexistent FMB.
  • The deep-convex source-consistency detector uses source HKT plus source MAD/MAC context, closed convex primitives only, closest-edge bridge, penetration length >=0.05 m and fraction >=0.90, with fail-closed guards. It selected only DM019 high, IG_EDFROOM01 low and IG_EDFROOM02 high in the audited set; it selected zero on DM019 low, the opposite IG variants, CAVE503, HEIGEN507, NW_EUROPE01, KAIGAN502, SANGAKU506 and DESERT508 low/high. Desert's largest tiny contact with thresholds disabled was only about 0.0139 m.
  • The DM019 high structural candidate was a 2↔3 streaming relation (face24/edge74 ↔ face195/edge735) whose closest-edge bridge gap was about 1.114206 m; about 1.108306 m lay inside a RoomB closed convex, penetration fraction about 0.994704. This is a real structural inconsistency finding, not the behavioral root cause: R2O removed it and the AI-vs-AI wall-targeting symptom remained.
  • Exact reversed-boundary streaming matching achieved 100% precision at strict tolerance in the audited same-map native pairs, but recall was insufficient—especially for EDFROOM. Therefore “retain exact reversed matches only” is invalid. Native EDF6 frequently rebuilds topology; exact parity is an inverse-bake/generation problem, not merely a version-wire patch.

Cross-mission Humanoid_Basic spawn-risk audit — do not relocate by static clearance alone

A 2026-09-09 audit scanned the current 141 shipping missions for Humanoid_Basic-family constructors (e503_*, e506_*, e617_*). It found 70 / 141 affected missions: 54 in MP03, 9 in MP04 and 7 in MP05. Of those, 61 were outdoor, 7 cave/interior and 2 Base502.

The audit did not find a new confirmed broken spawn. The two proven eject/problem cases remained M014 and DM001, both handled by their later mission-specific authority.

Useful negative evidence:

  • M027 / CAVE501: all 11 audited frog base rows were clear of the M014-like standing overlap in native target MAD probes;
  • M062 / CAVE503: all three E506 rows were in large chambers with substantial ceiling clearance;
  • M072_1 and M072_3 / Base502 already had direct runtime PASS;
  • M029 / CAVE504 and M072_2 / EDFRoom02 were retained as focused-smoke candidates, not preemptive-repair targets.

The important policy survived later work: do not relocate additional Humanoid_Basic spawns solely because a static clearance heuristic looks suspicious. M014 H1F already demonstrated that a point can look acceptable statically and still fail at runtime, while other statically constrained-looking points can work. Build a relocation candidate only after an actual eject/stuck symptom is observed and isolated.

The audit initially noted that both known problem cases involved constrained geometry plus immediate locomotion/route context. Later DM001 work showed delayed route assignment did not explain the first-frame eject, so this correlation must not be promoted into a universal causal rule. Use it only as test-priority context.
