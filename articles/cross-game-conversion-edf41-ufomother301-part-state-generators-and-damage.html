EDF4.1 UfoMother301 in EDF6 — a composite boss, not one spaceship object

ConversionCross-game ConversionGameplay & AIEDF 4.1EDF 6
Technical reference · Source/exact-build RE of composite boss and legacy part state; no fresh runtime

Why a model swap cannot preserve this encounter

EDF4.1's original UfoMother301 is a composite boss with one hull controller and several different part classes. The examined EDF6 compatibility path uses an EDF6 UfoCarrier508-based hull and separately created EventAnimationObject parts, but the visible model/animation is not the entire state machine. Script commands, generators, targetability, projectiles, effects and death timing belong to different objects.

The examined native source lists these original components:

Part Measured source organization / purpose
Main hull Movement, slow spin, phases, weak-point exposure and destruction sequence
HatchLow Four generator hatches with independent open/close behaviour
HatchMiddle Four covers; not ordinary damage targets
Cannon Ten subsidiary cannons with delayed activation and fire cycles
BigCannon One main charging/sweeping laser
Panel Four rotating rings with 22 / 40 / 40 / 23 elements

The part instances are created on the hull's first update, attached through their own transforms and locator names, and driven through EDF4.1's original state timing. A model that displays all of them can still be wrong if the expected parts are absent as actual damageable objects or if their mission actions never reach their owners.

Script and generator ownership

The original boss exposes ObjectAction(0..17), GetPartsObject and generator commands. The retained target bridge recognizes source ObjectAction (0x1000000D) and part lookup (0x10000017) as legacy semantics. In that studied mapping, part-group 0 is the main cannon and part-group 1 is the indexed smaller cannons; drops are forwarded to an appropriate hatch under a message of class 0x20100.

Hatches do not spawn enemies merely because the model is “open.” The source state machine opens them before the wave, uses the locator named in the actual generator component, and closes them 240 frames after the final spawn. An exporter must preserve the explicit generator-to-hatch relationship and its script-visible group/index; otherwise the mission can advance without ever creating the correct enemies.

Firing sequences are class state, not just projectile stats

Historical exact-build analysis in the retained source captures, among others:

  • The main cannon's long charge window (about 1200 frames) followed by a 61-shot LaserBullet02 sweep, with separate open and destruction states.
  • A secondary cannon's preparation about 600 frames, with a cycle restarting after a delay in the 1800–3599 frame range; its beam logic fires through four hatch muzzles in sequence, approximately every four frames.
  • Ring panel weapons with distinct laser/plasma rates, including a laser interval bounded below by 30 frames, versus a 300-frame plasma interval in the examined source.

Porting only fire rate and damage in SGO would lose charge phase, muzzle order, animation gating and script activation.

Damage gate and the weak body

The hull's hit filter accepts damage according to the named weak-point body and the main cannon's destruction state. Several covers/hatches remain deliberately invulnerable; parts with an “enabled” flag may otherwise be unregistered or non-targetable. A false positive generic hit or damage applied to the wrong scene object can break the encounter even though the correct boss health is displayed.

The source hull phases include open/close exposure, slow spin, disabled vulnerable point while covered, short bursts after hits, and a multi-phase death that differs from the target carrier's native deletion routine. The preserved class-specific adapter uses a separate source state machine rather than claiming stock UfoCarrier508 exactly matches UfoMother301.

Source scope and caveats

These are inherited exact-build reverse-engineering notes embodied in edf41mother.cpp and the source object's component contracts. Numbers are 60-FPS source-frame state windows, not a promise of identical gameplay timing at arbitrary simulation rates. Full M603 encounter and multiplayer parity must still be tested on the exact installed target; no such test was newly performed for this wiki edition.
