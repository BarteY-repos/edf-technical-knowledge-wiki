EDF6 mission audio calls: Voice, Talk, BGM, SE and localization boundaries

UniversalMissions & ScriptingMissionsEDF 5EDF 6
Source-backed research / case study · Reconciled

A bounded scan of shipped EDF6 MISSION.AC scripts shows a set of repeatable literal-argument call families. Observing a call in source is not equivalent to recovering all audio-bank entries, subtitle text or in-game playback timing.

Observed dialogue and voice usage

The shipping-corpus study classified proven literal usages of Voice, Radio, Talk and Dialog. Dynamic expressions could not be collapsed into static resource keys. Raw or context-dependent families including Voice2 and Talk_AddSingleFollower were not treated as synonyms merely because their names resemble the four established families.

BGM and SE calls

Observed MISSION.AC call Semantic family
Bgm BGM request
Bgm2 BGM request, with additional arguments in known call lowering
CrossFadeBgm BGM crossfade
PlaySe sound-effect request

StopBgm, EventSe and dynamic/non-literal argument expressions were not covered by the retained catalog study. Lack of classification is not proof that the game lacks those calls.

Compiler/bytecode call correspondences

The separately recovered mission-converter evidence includes these specific lowerings:

  • 302 -> CrossFadeBgm;
  • 303 -> Bgm2(song, fade, start_offset);
  • direct source 4000 -> Voice(string);
  • 4004/4005 -> EnableAiRandomTalk / DisableAiRandomTalk;
  • Talk 4100..4110 transaction-family handling.

These mappings apply to the studied mission bytecode; they do not define an exhaustive AngelScript/audio ABI for all builds.

Debugging missing voices and captions

Four layers can fail separately: the MISSION.AC call, the literal resource key, the localized VOICETABLE entry, and the final audio or subtitle presentation. For example, M011 compatibility research observed correct actor/call selection while a target-locale VOICETABLE row was absent. Later subtitle scroll timing was a separate display issue, not proof of an incorrect voice key.

Evidence limits

The historical bounded shipping-corpus parser checks passed 108/108 for Dialogue/Voice with a 101/101 regression, and 96/96 for BGM/SE with 102/102 regression; these were offline data/parser checks, not new game audio-playback tests. AWE/AWB/ACB physical audio-bank formats remain independent from this mission-script call corpus.
