EDF 4.1 seven SK maps: native terrain–object contact and boundary census

ConversionMaps & NavigationCollisionEDF 4.1EDF 6
Technical reference · Seven-map original source-data geometry only (R24–R28), no installed NAV or gameplay

At a glance

What this page answers: Seven original EDF4.1 SK maps have terrain heights, object placements and collision surfaces stored in different resources.

How to read it: Follow SHFB terrain, MAPB placement and MAD body geometry separately before deciding whether objects meet terrain or map borders.

Evidence boundary: Contact counts from R24–R28 use different samples and expansions. They are geometry evidence, not a completed EDF6 navmesh or in-game movement test.

Scope: original EDF 4.1 source SHFB heightfields, static-object MAD triangles and MAPB placements across seven SK_* map families. These are offline observations about geometry and data relationships, not EDF6 pathfinding, vehicle physics, obstacle avoidance, or in-game collision acceptance. This page reconciles five adjacent source audits (R24–R28, 16 November 2026) rather than listing development steps.

The game-data relationship

These seven maps carry source height samples in *_fld2mgrid.shfb. Their placed objects and collision triangles are separate sources: the SHFB defines terrain elevation and its cell layout; MAPB establishes object instances/placements; MAD contains physics shape geometry. A meeting point between a terrain triangle and a placed-object triangle is a geometric contact candidate. It does not say which side of the contact an NPC can enter or whether a Havok pathfinding agent has a passable link.

For the examined terrain triangulation, the FMB-style cell diagonal is A–B–D and A–D–C. Choosing the opposite diagonal changes contact geometry even when the four corner heights agree. This matters especially where roads, bridges or walls intersect slopes. Source actor positions in these reports use a candidate Rz × Ry × Rx placement transform; bit-exact parity with the original engine has not been demonstrated.

R24 tested 28,956 unique sampled edges, excluding 15,558 shared-triangle edge duplicates and detecting 4,673 terrain intersections (none unresolved within its sampled edge scope). R25 tested 14,838 source triangles against 46,606 terrain facets and reconstructed 5,020 raw intersection segments. Those segments yielded 151 closed-loop candidates and 787 open-chain candidates, with zero unresolved source triangles in that sample. Loops/chains are contour diagnostics, not navigation regions or edges.

R27 recorded 147,094 facet-intersection observations on 505,633 transformed/placed triangles, plus 146,989 unique segment observations in its per-instance diagnostic. These are not all the same thing as the 5,020 sampled R25 contours. Five of seven maps had complete classifications within the restricted recognized-static-mesh scope; the crater and valley maps still had boundary exceptions.

Why the R26 and R27 figures differ

The earlier R26 pass matched 1,770 placements but classified 1,765 complete, one rejected and four unresolved; it processed 502,895 placed triangles. A child/linked MAPB record for sk_valleybridge1 lacked sufficient parentage qualification in that pass. R27 examined the original group relationships and retested the valley map: 1,766 complete, zero rejected and four unresolved, now 505,633 placed triangles. R27 did not fix the remaining four geometry exceptions.

The R27 group-list evidence across all 16 inspected map families reported 193 child references in 18 linked groups. This is a source-data hierarchy observation; a child record cannot safely be treated as a freestanding or nonexistent placement simply because a one-level traversal misses the link. These values describe references and groups, not a count of 193 distinct colliders.

Four source-domain edge cases (R28)

The remaining exceptions arise where source triangles and the finite SHFB terrain domain only partially overlap. R28 subdivided intersecting triangles, kept track of the domain edge and counted diagnostic seam segments. It analyzed 6,040 source triangles in four affected source instances, producing 10,179 welded contact segments and 100 seam segments; one vertical/degenerate water-body case required 293 adaptive splits. All four remained partly outside the source field domain.

The four rows are source-body instances, not four missing maps. In particular, ig_valley01_water should not be assumed to be a traversable floor merely because its geometry crosses the terrain domain. R28 classified five water-body triangle cases as vertical/degenerate domain overlaps, and its inside-domain fragment analysis reported no ambiguous facets. That does not prove that original EDF 4.1 or target EDF6 applies water, wall or road collision in the same way.

What this evidence does not establish

  • No native EDF4.1 Havok narrow-phase contact outputs or EDF6 installed collision outcomes were collected in these passes.
  • No high/low HKT navigation meshes were generated, installed or played through for these seven maps by this research.
  • No agent-radius, step-height, walkable-slope, off-mesh connection or dynamic-actor policy was checked in game.
  • The placed-body rotation is a source-backed candidate but not bit-exact certified against the original engine; the field-domain seams are not an entire-world completeness proof.
  • These seven SK maps are not all sixteen original EDF4.1 map families; the other nine have different source geometry profiles.
  • The 16-map R1D/HillyCity counts (4,971 footprints, etc.) concern a different navigation reconstruction and cannot be combined with this contact census.
  • The later R29/R30 MissionBundle packaging/discovery work was offline source/runtime-code validation only, not gameplay. It does not promote these source geometric observations to game-proven collisions or fixed NPC pathfinding.

Source provenance and preservation

The five unaltered source summary JSONs are included under reference/r150-source-evidence/ in this release. Corresponding Library source folders are the original CHECKPOINT_2026-11-16_R13_FUNCTIONALITY_WIP_R24_… through R28_… trees; exact paths and SHA-256 are recorded in R150_SOURCE_CENSUS_AUDIT.json. The full original expanded source data (including the multi-megabyte R26 census) remain in their Library source folders and were not replicated as copyrighted game asset payloads in this public wiki.

The two accompanying reader datasets are data/EDF41_R150_SEVEN_SK_MAP_CONTACT_SURVEY.csv and data/EDF41_R150_FOUR_BOUNDARY_BODY_CASES.csv. Each uses an explicit source-pass prefix to prevent mixing incomparable metrics. Evidence grade: original EDF4.1 asset geometry as parsed in a bounded offline analysis; no runtime navigation or gameplay qualification.

What the later 4 m NAV source-grid study adds

A distinct source experiment has now used the same seven SHFB heightfields for an exact 4 m terrain grid layout and small structural HKT/NVMD samples: native terrain and navigation file evidence. The four sk_rockm01 candidate cells from SK_HEIGEN02 are a contact/geometry proposal only; they were not promoted into a blocking mask. The earlier 1,770-placement contact census remains unchanged and must not be added to those new terrain cells as if they were the same unit of analysis.

Later bounded 3D refinement — only SK_HEIGEN02, not all seven SK maps

A separate 265/265 recognized static-MAD/MAPB placement survey for SK_HEIGEN02 probes whether an upright hypothetical agent capsule approaches source object surfaces. Its three test profiles (all 1.8 m high) give:

Illustrative capsule radius Distinct closed-solid candidate cells Distinct open-mesh hint cells
0.25 m 204 455
0.45 m 243 495
0.65 m 277 522

The closed label requires a welded 2-manifold/orientation check, not just a model name or the existence of a MAD shape. Source terrain is an FMB triangular height surface; a roof/road/river/open object cannot be assigned obstacle or floor status from a single proximity test. The earlier 1,770 placement count in the seven-SK-map contact survey is a different corpus and different predicate. Do not add or equate these denominators. The 243 cells belong to exactly one original source map and one illustrative agent profile.

In the limited raw sample rows/columns (272,304,496,528), the diagnostic original-terrain 4-tile LOW and HIGH sample had 512 triangles each. Excluding four unreviewed closed-solid candidate cells yielded 504 triangles each; all four intertile seams remained, three open-only hint cells were retained, HKT changed and NVMD stayed identical. This is not a verified walkability policy, not a native EDF4.1 original NAV, and not an installable EDF6 mission/map fix.

Evidence: original EDF4.1 SK_HEIGEN02 MAPB/MAD/SHFB source corpus, bounded R34/R35 contact/capsule diagnostic reports and R35_OFFLINE_QUALIFICATION.json (source-only; runtime_tested=false).
