Spatial audio in EDF6 mods: listener matrix, equal-power panning and Doppler

EDF 6Audio
Technical reference · Source-reviewed helper equation and historical crash observation; not a universal stock mixer contract

The retained placement model

An exact-source compatibility helper documents the audio-listener representation used by a group of EDF6 mod sound paths (jet/vehicle sounds and cinematic recordings). Its listener record contains 20 float32 values: listener position cam[0:3], padding, and four rows of the inverse world transform beginning at cam+4. The helper converts world position to listener space using those inverse-matrix rows, including translation. Under the documented convention camera-local +X points left.

For the given helper, distance is the length of the local position vector. The lateral fraction is right = -localX / distance when distance exceeds 1 metre; within 1 m it is zero. The normalized equal-power gain pair is:

spread = 0.75
left  = sqrt(0.5 * (1 - spread * right))
right = sqrt(0.5 * (1 + spread * right))

These are the helper's concrete panning equations, not a claim that every stock EDF6 sound system must use exactly the same mixer.

Doppler and optional velocity pointers

The unit vector u points from source to listener. For source and listener velocities, the helper takes each velocity's dot product with u, clamps each closing component to ±0.8 × 340 m/s, and forms:

c = 340 m/s
v_source   = clamp(dot(sourceVel,   u), -0.8*c, +0.8*c)
v_listener = clamp(dot(listenerVel, u), -0.8*c, +0.8*c)
doppler = (c - v_listener) / (c - v_source)

When source distance is at most 1 m, the ratio stays 1.0 and the direction vector remains zero. Crucially, either velocity pointer may legitimately be nullptr. A null source velocity means a stationary emitter, and a null listener velocity means a stationary listener. It must not be blindly dereferenced.

The retained source records a historical exception C0000005 in a cinematic recording path where no velocity was supplied. The lesson is to validate the pointer contract independently of the audio formulas. When reviewing a mod audio hook, verify the incoming listener and source position pointers, the optional velocity pointers, inverse matrix layout, and source/listener coordinate conventions before attributing noise to the audio bank.

Scope

All constants and equations above are directly present in source components/compatibility/src/sound_place.h in the R9 archive. They are a useful, reproducible helper ABI and an account of a historical failure; not a newly verified universal EDF6 runtime audio specification. Mixing, occlusion, attenuation curves and routing to ACB/AWB banks are outside this helper.
