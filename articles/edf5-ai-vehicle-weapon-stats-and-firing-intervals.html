EDF5 AI vehicles: weapon loadouts, damage values and firing intervals

EDF 5Game technical referencesVehicles / weapons
Technical reference · Source analysis only (unless a source fact is contributor-verified); no new EDF runtime test

Player-summoned forms generally use vehicle_setup. AI-driven forms additionally use a mission_setup root that names their mounted weapon SGOs. The native class may be unchanged, but mission ownership, model choice and weapon setups differ.

vehicle402_rocket_ai (Naegling) 550 200 homing rockets, 100 base damage, bursts of ten, blast 8

v515_retrobalam_ai (Barga) 40000 No SGO gun-style melee damage table identified

The source revises community estimates with frame-based settings: Naegling 240 frames (4 s) between bursts, Nix missiles 420 frames (7 s), EMC 180-frame burst plus 120-frame reload (five-second cycle at 60 Hz). These are native parameter timings, not newly measured actual cycles during arbitrary lag or time dilation.

The Caliban's proposed “0.05 HP per tick” was not proved: the matching 0.05 in a weapon SGO is labelled FireAccuracy. The community Barga melee-damage numbers also lack supporting native weapon SGOs in this particular audit and must not be promoted as a confirmed table.
