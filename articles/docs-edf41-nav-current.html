Evidence rules

  • Exact historical output: hashes, geometry counts and retained output can be oracles, but do not reveal the original construction algorithm by themselves.
  • Native parsed source data: demonstrates the existence and distribution of fields in the audited corpus, not automatically their runtime use.
  • Offline reconstructed candidate: may pass a population census but remain byte-incompatible or untested in game.
  • Runtime parity: cannot be inferred without a qualifying in-game test of the exact shipped candidate.

The R1D target remains open despite a natural candidate with the exact 65 / 1,405 / 251 / 1,153 / 1 / 4,971 census. The source-backed work has already excluded global shape dedupe, simple placement aliases, alternate MAPO bases, body-role selector guesses and the specific eight post-selection full-MAPO-transform carve variants. Re-running those searches without a genuinely new source discriminator is not useful.

Conversion boundary: MAPO component transforms, source body motionState and body-local carve placement are related but not interchangeable. Keep map-object collision different from ordinary MDB prop rendering and from mission-side RMPA semantics.

Source class: EDF4.1 source-corpus and reconstruction evidence, with the latest selected checkpoint through R100 (15 November 2026). No new runtime evidence in this documentation revision.

Seven SK map terrain–object contacts

See the source-census comparison of seven SK maps for original SHFB versus MAD contacts across 1,770 recognized static-mesh placements. Contact geometry is not a NAV link; the R24–R28 offline measurements do not qualify NPC movement.

Seven original SHFB maps: native-grid structural evidence (R31–R32)

The original EDF4.1 seven-SK-map 4 m grid and HKT/NVMD structural atlas adds a different, source-mesh route from the historical HillyCity R1D oracle. Seven maps have a verified 250,000-cell 4 m terrain plan each; only bounded 2/4/16-tile source windows have structural LOW/HIGH HKT/NVMD results. No full map HKT, placed-obstacle policy or in-game NPC pathfinding was qualified. Existing EDF5→EDF6 HKT migration knowledge is reused where it applies, not equated with an absent EDF4.1 source NAV file.

Native-source solid and agent-volume evidence from one SK map

The later original-mesh 4 m atlas now records a bounded 64-tile / 256 m × 256 m terrain HKT/NVMD experiment. Even that wider window is not a complete NAV file or a gameplay-positive sample.

The seven-map terrain/contact study has a subsequent one-map, 265-instance 3D refinement. For SK_HEIGEN02, 166 recognized placed MAD meshes satisfy a strict closed-edge-orientation test; 99 are open. An upright example capsule with height 1.8 m and radius 0.45 m touches 243 distinct closed-solid candidate grid cells and 495 open-surface hint cells across the sample. Those dimensions are not extracted EDF6 NPC dimensions. In particular, a road, roof, platform or river/water face may be open and still physically relevant: automatically deleting such cells destroys potentially valid walking surfaces. No native agent profile or gameplay pass has been established.

The article EDF4.1 source collision and NAV limitations distinguishes this original-SK-map obstacle hypothesis from the independent R1D/HillyCity 4,971-footprint oracle reconstruction. Their measured counts and hashes are not interchangeable.

Recent original-source NAV evidence: native LOW/HIGH, portals and group placement (17 November 2026)

The original native LOW/HIGH and collision-clearance study compares native EDF5/EDF6 NAV topology and then tests original EDF4.1 MAC/MAD source geometry in bounded HillyCity/Soukogai windows. Static contacts from point rays, side rays and sparse sphere probes exclude some naive candidate paths; they do not certify untouched paths as walkable. The source actor's group-parent transform and collision activation remain unresolved, so there is no runtime NPC fix.
