Havok version and physics family (key: hk_<version> marker, hkp* vs hknp*)

  • edf41: Havok hk_2014.1.0-r1, Physics2012 hkp* graphs in ragdoll SHKT and map MAD files | ref: edf41/builds.md#edf41-havok-version, edf41/file-formats.md#mad-hkp-collision | evidence: static
  • edf5: Havok hk_2015.1.0-r1, Physics2012 (hkpEntity bodies; crate body layout and collision layers verified in game) | ref: edf5/builds.md, edf5/physics-havok.md | evidence: verified (crate bodies), static (version string)
  • edf6: Havok hk_2020.1.0-r1, new physics hknp* (maps: hknpPhysicsSystemData, hknpCompressedMeshShape ...), plus a legacy reader for Binary Tagfiles <=2014.2 | ref: edf6/builds.md#edf6-havok-version, edf6/file-formats.md#map-collision-hknp | evidence: static
  • Differences noticed: EDF 4.1 and EDF5 share the Physics2012 family; EDF6 moved to hknp, so map collision needs a semantic hkp -> hknp conversion (edf6/dead_ends.md). EDF6 can read 2014-era ragdoll SHKT files, and every Havok class in the nine Hector files exists in EDF6's DLL, but loading them in game was not tested. EDF6 native SHKT files are Havok 2020 tagfiles.
  • Confidence in the mapping: high for the versions; untested for direct loading of old files.
  • Added: 2026-10-15 src: EDF41-to-EDF6 handoff (shared by Bartey), novae (ajane)
