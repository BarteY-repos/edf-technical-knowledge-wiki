These are unresolved game-engine or format questions, not incomplete tasks for any particular editor or loader. A source-only reconstruction or static test is not a game-runtime conclusion.

Scripts, mission AI and gameplay

  • Is there a hidden global, host or slot limit on active generator children, and do rejected spawns queue or disappear?
  • Which precise factor marks completion of E505_GENERATOR_ACTION_FALL so a mission can time actions relative to landing?
  • EDF5 BVM cuscall0 3450 (SetAiSearchRadius) has an understood meaning, but six omitted calls in the accepted compatibility path still leave optional AI search-radius fidelity unresolved.
  • In M200/M201, exact dense-fog visibility depends on environment fog composition, beyond the ABI-correct SceneEffect_Fog call.
  • The source-to-target semantics of M035 Titan route/circling, M015 Shield Bearer demo-airstrike contact, and M210 Starship turret/progression remain partly unverified in the relevant runtime.
  • The EDF5→6 141-mission source converter has broad static evidence, but does not confer exact-release in-game parity for every mission.
  • M060 localization AB001B* lacks a verified donor/equivalent caption row.

Binary formats, shaders and rendering

  • There is no universal EDF5→6 rule for packed FPR mask-channel rebaking or a complete universal legacy SND_CHARA_BASIC shader translation.
  • Exact EDF5→6 differences in every MTAB member, GST/MST save field and MAB selector remain incompletely characterized.
  • Generic AWE/AWB/ACB bank migration and EFARC parsing across generations are not closed for all resource families.
  • A Source Texture V1 DDS export that validates statically still needs gameplay checks for newly generated source-image outputs.

Maps, navigation and collision

  • The EDF4.1 R1D HillyCity source-semantic reconstruction matches the historical 4,971 footprint census, but does not recover the exact 381,512-byte LOW HKT. The natural R32/R75 candidate is 381,544 bytes, with R88–R100 source-identity exclusions. This is not byte-exact or in-game NAV parity.
  • The same collision/NAV approach is not automatically established for all sixteen EDF4.1 map NAV families.
  • Generic terrain FieldRender, MAD broadphase, authored grass/water, destructible-collapse and weather/MAE runtime semantics remain map/profile-specific.
  • Desert508 is a narrower tested case and must not be treated as proof of universal source-only weather synthesis.
  • General road, portal and donor-independent source-geometry guide selection remain unresolved.

Animation, enemies, vehicles and audio

  • Cross-generation CAS/CANM/MAB conversion has known wrapper/version differences, but not a universal semantic mapping for all controllers and track layouts.
  • Friendly EDF4.1 NPC/vehicle navigation, roles, loadout, mounted weapons and restart behaviour have not all been confirmed in the matching EDF6 runtime.
  • Some EDF4.1 boss/large-enemy behaviours (Earth Eater active-panel collision and queries, Shield Hector proxies, DragonBig401 cue mappings) remain class/profile-specific open cases.
  • Highest-LOD foliage faults require an exact affected MDB/material/LOD0 binding cause before generalizing shader fixes.

EDF6 multiplayer and timing

  • Specific FlameBullet02, LightningBullet01 and SolidExpBullet01 remote-state divergences remain candidates for minimal initial-state repair; broad replication is not proven necessary.
  • Full 0x1400 RUDP control semantics, reconnect READY reset outcomes and SceneObject staging/freshness causal chains remain open beyond the retained static tests.
  • The full >60 Hz presentation/simulation split is subsystem-specific; a complete integrated 120-unique-state graphics/actor/VFX/network behavior is not proven merely by a camera-only test.
