EDF5 render pipeline: precompiled DXBC reflection and 16×16 deferred dispatch

EDF 5Game technical referencesRendering / engine
Technical reference · Source static evidence with unverified behavior labelled; no new in-game R132 test

The EDF5 engine was observed to ship 260 DXBC blobs in its executable, alongside model shaders in DX11.cpk. It imports D3DCompiler_47!D3DReflect to inspect compiled shader resources at load time; the renderer creates shader stages and caches some objects by hash. The analyzed executable does not depend on rewriting HLSL source at every map load.

Lighting dispatch in the view loop

Three deferred-lighting compute variants were identified. Their source numthreads is (16,16,1), and the main-view dispatch uses:

Dispatch(ceil(view_width / 16), ceil(view_height / 16), 1)

The selected variant depends on scene parameters and sun-shadow state. The shader binds G-buffer albedo, specular, normals, roughness/metallic, view position, accumulated lighting, AO and the local cubemap grid. A sun-shadow variant adds a shadow-depth SRV. The source identifies an additional scene-flag variant that may be associated with underground rendering; that association is an inference, not a separately observed gameplay mode.

Why this affects water and glass reasoning

A deferred combine writes to the output UAV before the later forward-material phase. The inspected frame region contained no detected scene-color resource copy or resolve between that compute dispatch and the forward phase. Thus source-era forward water/glass shaders do not automatically receive a sampled copy of the lit scene; a shader requiring the scene color would need a corresponding resource binding or additional pass.

This clarifies why material and shader discussions need to distinguish precompiled shader presence, resource binding, actual dispatch, and final on-screen effect. Finding a DXBC blob in the executable is not itself evidence that its shader was used in the game.

Evidence: static reverse engineering and RDEF inspection of steam-5d11de53, no current direct runtime measurement. Source edf5/shaders.md: shaders-exe-blobs, shaders-reflection, deferred-lighting-cs, deferred-lighting-dispatch.
