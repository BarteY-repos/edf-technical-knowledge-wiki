What you will find here

Articles distinguish native observations (game assets/binaries), runtime-tested behavior, source-level or offline reconstruction, and external reference descriptions. This matters: a binary structure identified exactly in a sample is not necessarily a general rule for every game version, and a valid output file is not necessarily accepted during gameplay.

Where a result depends on a particular shader family, resource, platform, build or sample, the article says so. Some systems remain only partly understood; those limits are documented rather than filled with guesses.

Navigation tip: search for concrete names such as snd_BRDF_Map_Common, TEXCOORD, type=4, FieldRender, MAPO, RegisterEvent, or CANM. Every article is indexed by its full text. In the interface, Ctrl+K or / focuses search.

Reading the supporting notes

Some older articles were originally written as converter, add-on or editor research notes. Their exact observations remain available because they can contain valuable numbers and edge cases. Their project names and revision labels are not prerequisites for understanding the main findings.
