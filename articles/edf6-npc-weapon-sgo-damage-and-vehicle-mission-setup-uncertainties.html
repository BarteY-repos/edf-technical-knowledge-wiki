EDF6 allied NPC weapons and vehicles: SGO damage numbers and unresolved factors

EDF 6Game technical referencesGameplay and allied units
Technical reference · Source-derived; evidence grades and build restrictions described in article; no new EDF runtime

The damage number stored in an NPC weapon's SGO is not automatically the damage that an ally deals on a given difficulty. An EDF Knowledge Hub comparison of 24 NPC soldier weapon entries with the Japanese EDF6 wiki found a consistent factor-of-two difference in the quoted Normal-and-above damage figures. That comparison is a strong data observation; the native code path that would independently prove the scaling factor was not identified.

Soldier weapons: measured pairs

EDF6 weapon SGO AmmoDamage in SGO Figure in Japanese EDF6 wiki
aisoldierrifle01 5 2.5
aisoldiershotgun01 3 × 10 1.5 × 10
aisoldiersniperrifle02 25 12.5

All 24 tested soldier-weapon rows followed that relationship. The likely explanation is that the game scales NPC weapon damage to one-half of the SGO amount under the wiki's test conditions. That is an inference from correlated data, not a traced damage routine. EDF5's comparable wiki historically used the full SGO figure, which helps explain misleading comparisons across generations.

Vehicle SGO mission_setup[0] is not a generic damage flag

Research compared the two-element mission_setup[0] list in several allied vehicle SGOs. It found values that numerically reproduce the Japanese wiki's vehicle-weapon reductions, notably:

Vehicle SGO Observed pair Supporting comparison
v504_begaruta_ai, v504_begaruta_g_ai (Nix) [1, 0.8] Weapon figures 5 and 60 × 0.8 = 4.0 and 48
v608_oldrobot_ai (Gravis) [1, 1] Same base weapon figures listed as 5.0 and 60
v603_flak_weak_ai [1, 0.5] Ordinary v603_flak_ai is [1, 1]
vehicle403_tank_mission [1, 0.6] Distinct mission actor preset
v603_flak_mission [1.25, 1] First element differs; role unknown
v614_proteus_mk2 and related mission variants [3, 3.5] Not a standard [1,1] preset

The second value behaving like a damage multiplier is plausible but still unverified. The reader of that pair was not traced. The first value's meaning is unknown; treating it as a durability multiplier would be speculation.

Vehicle base-value cross-check and exception

A broader check found the Japanese EDF6 NPC vehicle wiki usually agrees with the SGO base durability: e.g. Blacker and Varius 700, Titan 2,400, Epsilon 1,600, Proteus 7,500 and War/Armament Barga 40,000. Most weapon entries also agree, but the Proteus Buster Cannon is unresolved: the wiki quotes 60, while one sampled v_407bigbegaruta_cannon_ai SGO has 35 and the associated mission setup is [1,1]. The Combat Wagon variants have difficulty-specific durability entries [700,700,2100], not one universal figure.

Evidence: SGO comparisons, not independent R130 gameplay tests; EDF6 Steam build steam-678ccb46. The Japanese wiki's displayed figures are source comparison data, not original Sandlot documentation.
