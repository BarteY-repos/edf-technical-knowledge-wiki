Scope: Japanese contributor workbook observations and extracted configuration snippets. This is not a newly checked Sandlot native binary; the values are as recorded by the contributor. Game runtime, build version and multiplayer behavior were not reproduced here. Preserve named/unknown fields without inventing universal class contracts.

E660 large pillbug: contact attack plus spinning effect

Source: 解析!A1904:K1928. In the contributor's E660 attack configuration, the nested attack/contact_damage object is not a single literal damage number. Its damage expression contains numeric literals 50 and 30, loads mark84 and mark2, and uses the arithmetic instructions fsma followed by mul. The surrounding runtime marks are not resolved here; reporting 50 as the enemy's actual final damage would be unsupported.

Field or node Recorded data Interpretation limited to this experiment
contact_damage.blast_scale 1 Contributor describes infantry knockback; not an explosion radius
contact_damage.vehicle_blast_scale approximately 0.002 Tentatively vehicle knockback scale, not independently calibrated
contact_damage.enable_speed 10 Contributor reports hit detection being disabled below this body-speed threshold; unit/build not independently proven

The source distinguishes motion-dependent contact damage, knockback, spinning and visual effects. The original game damage function, the runtime interpretation of mark84/mark2 and whether the same configuration is shared by other enemies are open.

E516 Big Grey boss: hand muzzle, projectile pairs and battery configuration

Source: 解析!A1933:O2010. In E516_BIGGREYBOSS_HANDMUZZLE, the contributor extracted a bullet_object with BeamBullet01 and a nested array of indexed parameter/value pairs. In this sample the mapped keys include speed (0 → 8), lifetime (1 → 90), size (8 → 1.2), and other properties not fully decoded. 12 carries a 4-component color vector (2,1,2,1); 14 points to an impact sound cue. The exact native effect of those values has not been retested; the contributor also reports that changing one earlier integer to 1 crashed the tested setup.

The separate gun_battery_config includes a muzzle-name reference, bullet_object, an integer 240 described in the source as a burst-count-like parameter, float 0.1 described as power, a firing sound cue, interval-like integer 1, and named charge_param=180. Do not infer seconds or actual projectiles per burst from the raw integers alone. A gun_battery_group_list references gun_battery_config, laser_gun_battery_config and spear_gun_battery_config; contributor experiments tentatively relate one group selector to choosing enemy targets versus muzzle direction. The latter behavior is not a confirmed engine-wide enum.

These are examples of how a boss weapon separates projectile identity and overrides, battery timing, targeting, and sound. They do not establish all weapon-class field schemas.

Vehicle weapon placement: MAB within the weapon resource

Source: 解析!A1127:M1161. The workbook describes an extra MAB-like payload inside a vehicle weapon SGO and gives a test-edited Blacker cannon example. The XML view labels a MAB header with version="6", an export bone 01 whose parent is v_Null, and transforms:

Part Test-modified values
location (10, 0, 2.8, 1)
scale (0.05, 0.05, 0.25, 1)
rotation (0, 0, 0, 1)

The author explicitly changed these values for an experiment, so they are not stock EDF6 placement offsets. The source guesses that lateral, vertical and forward translations may use world-size units; no canonical unit or universal MAB schema is proven by this sample. An extra embedded MAB is a weapon-attachment transform, not automatically a full skeletal animation file.

Combat Wagon camera: bone-relative offsets and unresolved modeling issue

Source: 解析!A772:AG834, mainly tabulated at A775:I785 with screenshot comparisons. The primary camera is associated with bone robo_head; the sample offsets include vertical 10, lateral 5, depth -23, and an additional selector or scale-like field 1. The contributor changed one field at a time (height 0, lateral 30, depth -6, other field 0.5) and compared camera images. Look-at offset examples include lateral -0.65 and depth 5.95. The source flags a possible underlying model bone-orientation problem and does not prove that editing only the camera offsets solves every vehicle's perspective.

Cross-generation caution and provenance

This contributor research contains precise named objects, field values, observations and negative outcomes, but is not native EDF5↔EDF6 parity evidence by itself. Equivalent class names may parse different numeric layouts in different game builds. Do not infer restored EDF4.1 NPC behavior, a verified EDF6 runtime fix or correct Havok physics from these workbook cells. See the separate MAB/CANM/CAS format reference, EDF6 particle/effect graph and friendly vehicles resource authority.
