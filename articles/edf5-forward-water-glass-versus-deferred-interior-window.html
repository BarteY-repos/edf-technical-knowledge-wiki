EDF5 water, glass and windows: three different reflection paths

EDF 5Game technical referencesRendering / materials
Technical reference · Source static evidence with unverified behavior labelled; no new in-game R132 test

A port or asset viewer can show multiple transparent or reflective-looking surfaces, but their source shaders do not necessarily execute in the same render pass. The EDF5 DX11.CPK analysis distinguishes at least three common families.

Material family Named shader Observed drawing path Reflection/interior data
Water SND_MAP_WATER_TRANSPARENT.SGO Forward, after deferred combine Global environment cubemap, scrolling normals, parallax maps, scene view position
Standalone glass object SND_MAP_OBJECT_GLASS.SGO Forward / ZPass Global environment cubemap, normals and reflect parameters
Building window SND_MAP_BUILD_WINDOW.SGO G-buffer / Deferred (also instanced and ZPass variants) View-dependent interior texture; local cubemap contribution through later lighting combine

The water pixel shader references three scrolling normal maps, three parallax textures, bubble_albedo, the global cubemap, shadow depth and the view-position texture. xgl_user_param includes water_color, reflection weighting, blend_depth, metallic and roughness. The glass-object shader exposes reflection and translucency falloff controls. Neither of these forward shaders binds a scene-color input, so they cannot simply sample a hypothetical fully rendered scene for SSR without a separate binding/change.

Interior mapping is not a true 3D room

The building-window shader samples a two-dimensional interior_texture with a view-dependent offset, including an instanced random seed. It uses window_frame_texture, window_normal, normals and a reflect parameter. That can create the impression of depth or rooms behind windows, but the source does not prove actual interior geometry or a physical space visible through the wall.

The documentation preserves a remaining uncertainty: which visual component of some city images arises from the interior texture and which from the later cubemap combine had not been experimentally isolated. The shader resource bindings support the pipeline separation, not a complete visual attribution for every pane.
