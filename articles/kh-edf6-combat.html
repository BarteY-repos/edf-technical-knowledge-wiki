Team relation matrix record structure

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: team-relations

Default seven-team relation matrix

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: teammanager-relation-defaults

Default team relations (constructor 5E0600..5E06D6, same table written by 5E1E90): diagonal 1 (friendly to itself), then 0<->1 hostile (2), 0<->2 friendly (1), 1<->2 hostile, 1<->4 hostile; everything else 0 (neutral). So team 1 is the enemy (aliens, monsters), 0 and 2 are allies of each other and enemies of 1; 3 is neutral to all (parachutes, the airstrike demo's level), 4 is hostile to 1 only (white on the radar), 5 holds unmanned vehicles, 6 mission environment (spider webs); teams 5 and 6 only have the diagonal. A player is set to team 0 at creation (5A51E1: 54EE70(player, 0, 1) right after 591410 and 54ED40); the author places players, NPC allies and friendly airstrikes in 2 (2 = EDF in its table).

Basis: the eight relation writes (5E0614..5E06C6) and the player's SetTeam call re-read with fn.py; roles of 3 to 6 are the author's

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 5E0600, 5E06D6, 5E1E90, 5A51E1

SetTeam registration and early-return logic

Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: setteam-full

SetTeam 54EE70(object, team, register) in full: returns at once when +0x314 already equals the team and bit 6 (0x40) of +0x380 equals register; otherwise asks vfunc 0xB0 (returns 0 when false), removes the object from its old team (5E1C60, if +0x314 != -1) and clears 0x40, writes +0x314 and, when the team is not -1, also +0x318 (so +0x318 keeps the last real team after a -1); with register set and bit 0x16 of +0x380 clear it sets 0x40 and adds the object to the new team (5E0B70); with 0x16 set it removes it instead. So 0x40 in +0x380 = listed in the TeamManager. Immediate team numbers passed at its call sites: 1 x14, 2 x7, 5 x8, 0 x3, 3 x3, 4 x2, 6 x3, 49 from registers (sites in the source note); the one-team walk 5E0D60 takes 5 at F85D9, FC904, 56D2D0, 56D768, 57360D (boarding prompt, empty vehicles on the map), 6 at 4DE369, 0 at 596E8E, 77C1A4, 8079BD; 5E14E0(manager, 1) at 1F90D5; only team 5 is compared as an immediate after a +0x314 read (F73A0, 62DD0F, 6346E7).

EDF Knowledge Hub record — original wording — expand (3,237 characters)

An object's targets and lock points, as calls: the current target is +0x518 / +0x520, the forced (script lock) target +0x528 / +0x530 (both {object, control block} weak pairs; netcode.md#net-enemy-retarget). 364EB0(root, Weak16 by value) sets both, taking a new weak count for each field before releasing the old ones and consuming its argument once (0xD4 bytes); 387550(root, actor or null) sets the current target only, locking the actor's weak-this from its +0x28 / +0x30 and casting it to GameObjectBase (null clears it). With the forced target empty, GameObjectBase::Update (54BE40) still calls slot 14 to choose, so clearing the fields each frame does not keep an object without a target; slot 14 is void* SelectTarget(root), returning the raw actor or null in rax (the base 54C4B0 enumerates at 54C506 and puts the callback's actor in rax at 54C50C; the caller 54C010 calls [vtable+0x70], reads +0x30 / +0x28 of the result at 54C01C / 54C025, locks it and casts at 54C06A). The hostile walk 5E0F20(manager, observer team, functor) follows relation 2 and skips dead actors (+0x2E8) and, at 5E0FBF, actors with +0x380 & 0x80 (not targetable). SetTeam with -1 writes +0x314 (54EEE0) and keeps +0x318 (54EEE6..54EEEB), so an object detached from its team still has its last team at +0x318. Lock points: +0x368 points to an array of 16-byte {LockPoint*, control block} pairs, +0x378 its count; a LockPoint has +0 its kind, +8 the owning actor, +0x10 the position, +0x29 / +0x2A valid / lockable; 548EF0(actor, out Weak16, index) copies entry index (bound-checked; empty out otherwise) and takes a weak count on its control block (548F28 lock inc [rcx+0xC]), reading +0x378 / +0x368 unconditionally (548EF3 / 548F07), so a null actor crashes; the result is a LockPoint weak pointer, not a game object's. Physics object rays use layer 11; a hit body becomes its object through 108260(bodyId) (11AD2E0, property key 0). The target message (message 1, 54C15D..54C1EB): 7748F0 says only that a session is online (it reads no rcx), root+0x128 bit 0 clear means not a remote copy, root+0x591 enables the family's message, the object alive; 79A460(stream, 0x40) builds the stream (cursor +0x5F0, 0x610 bytes), 12B5790(stream, (char)1) writes the type, slot 37 void(root, stream, actor or null) writes the target (base 54CAA0: 785050 to the network id, written by 774B90), the NetworkObject root+0x120's slot 14 (sub, stream, true) sends, 760180(stream) frees; the receiver's slot 38 (base 54C900) resolves the id with 784C60 and calls 387550 at 54C9A3. A weapon unit's target (6D51C0 -> 6D5734 call 6D2640): it locks its owner from unit +0x2B0 / +0x2B8, reads the owner's current target at 6D2F3B / 6D2F69 (+0x520 / +0x518), takes its lock point 0 with 548EF0 at 6D2FCB and stores it in the bullet InitParam (stack rbp+0x180) +0x60 / +0x68 (6D2FDE / 6D2FEC); before that 32F130(InitParam+0x30, unit+0x330) at 6D28B6 copies the template, its weak pair unit +0x360 / +0x368 into InitParam +0x60 / +0x68 (32F179..32F198), and with no current target the overwrite is skipped, so the template's pair stays. The other weak copy in the fire path (6D05C0 -> 6D068F call 6CEBB0) comes from the fire / holder record +0 / +8, not from the owner's target.

Basis: the author's static reading and its native audit (364EB0's whole machine code run in a fixture); 548EF0..548F2F and 54EEE6 re-read with fn.py

EDF Knowledge Hub record — original wording — expand (1,960 characters)

A vehicle's team is its riders' team, recomputed every frame: the VehicleBase update 630250 (506's slot 5 653680 calls it at 653790), while the vehicle lives, starts from team 5, takes each seat's rider team (any value other than -1 overrides) and calls SetTeam(vehicle, team, true) (6304C7, 630520..630529, 630589..630591); an empty vehicle is team 5; a vehicle with +0x1A & 8 skips it (6304AB). RideAi (vehicle slot 50 633030) creates its rider in C++ without SGO (835C0(*[20B2958], out, &identity 1FFF3D0, &InitParamBase vt 1762068) at 633169 -> new 0x5D0, constructor 5E3A90, vtable 17D7320 = DummyVehicleRider) and hard-codes SetTeam(rider, 2, false) (6331C9..6331D3), so a RideAi vehicle becomes team 2 one frame later whatever team it was given; then level 54E740(rider, 1.0), seat 633C10(veh, rider, 0, 1), child 118AF20, veh+0xE30 = 1. DummyVehicleRider slot 0 5E3B70 (the snapshot slot) also sets team 2. SetTeam does not propagate to riders (506's vfunc 0xB0 is 54ABD0, mov al,1); the script's SetTeamID (wrapper 1C3AA0 -> 1C36D0) walks the child list +0x550 recursively before SetTeam(obj, team, true). Weapon team: each holder's owner is the vehicle (62A51B in the constructor 629450; seat weapon lists seat+0xC8 point at the holders, 62A57E / 62A5E6); weapon+0x214 = the vehicle's +0x314 at creation (633961..633976, 632FDB..632FEE) and every frame (63041A..630427, 633E34..633E42), one frame behind the vehicle's own recompute; firing copies it into the bullet parameters (6976CF / 6976D6: InitParam +0x90) and on to the hit record's team +0x24 (core init 232015..232023). Friendly-fire flag: weapon +0x8B6 (set to 1 when weapons are built, 633950 / 632FCB; RideAi clears it, 6330A2..6330D7, 6330C9) -> core +0xA26 -> hit flags |= 0x20 (23206C..2320CD); direct-hit candidates check teams only for bullets with an explosion (232AA0; AmmoExplosion threshold 68DB14); blast hits without 0x20 filter on rel[target+0x318][team] == 2 (543DD0, 5E1540).

Basis: 6331C9..6331D3 re-read with fn.py; the rest the author's reading ("confirmed" = read; the snapshot trigger and the +0x550 link inferred)

EDF Knowledge Hub record — original wording — expand (1,303 characters)

More of RideAi and team use (#vehicle-team-from-riders): with its dl argument true RideAi calls 62D6E0 (633063) and then vfunc 0x170 (633073; slot 46, 506's 61B770), which reads the SGO's mission_setup and builds weapons from it (61B839 -> 633330); any later weapon rebuild (633330 / 632E90) sets weapon+0x8B6 back to 1 (friendly fire on, so blasts hit team-1 riders' allies too and direct friendly hits are scaled by difficulty instead of ignored) and a mod must clear it again. Weapon holders: +0x18 the bone node (62A516), +0x38 the owner (the vehicle). Besides 6976CF, the bullet-parameter builders 6B1380, 6B08A0 and 6B2750 also copy weapon+0x214 to +0x890 only. In one frame the weapon sync (630421) runs before the team recompute (630591).
