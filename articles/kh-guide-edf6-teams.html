EDF6 Team Identity, Damage Filters and Player Update

EDF 6Community Knowledge HubGameplay & AI
Technical reference · Editorial synthesis of contributor static/game observations; per-claim grades below

Overview

Team membership is a native property used by damage, targeting and HUD logic. Team numbers should not be interpreted as universal allies/enemies without inspecting the call site and active relation matrix.

Format, API or behavior

The inspected base-object team field is +0x314 (ordinary player team 0; common alien team 1), while TeamManager owns relation and aggregated team state. A separate invulnerability bit in obj+0x380 affects damage calculations, with a forced-damage exception. Player update logic has distinct local-player conditions.

Evidence and exact technical details

The following selected source observations retain their original evidence level and build-specific details. Follow their links for the complete evidence method, addresses and attribution.

Team id — static

Team id: obj+0x314 (players 0, the usual alien team 1). The TeamManager's remove-from-team function is 5E1C60.

Exact source: View evidence and source details · build steam-678ccb46

Teammanager layout — static

TeamManager global 20B2978: lock at +8 (a CRITICAL_SECTION, taken with the game's guard helpers 50BC0 enter / 50BE0 leave), teams at [+0x38] (0x38 bytes each, count [+0x48]), each team's damage total at team +0x30. Each team starts with an MSVC std::set of members: node left +0, parent +8, right +0x10, isnil byte +0x19, object +0x20.

Exact source: View evidence and source details · build steam-678ccb46

Invincible flag — static

Flag 0x1 of obj+0x380 is what the developer command set_invincible sets (handler ABF10); the damage function 547C30 skips the HP loss for it (at 5480F8) unless the hit carries forced flag 0x40.

Exact source: View evidence and source details · build steam-678ccb46

Soldier update — static

The soldier update 59ABB0 runs for Ranger, Air Raider, Wing Diver and Fencer; past its [obj+0x340] test (local players only) the instruction cmp byte [rbx+0xD7A],r15b at 59B689 is a 7-byte hook site. obj+0x338 is the player's pad index (keys are polled for pad 0).

Exact source: View evidence and source details · build steam-678ccb46

Team 4 enemy — unverified

A third reading: KittopiaCreator's KruulPodsOnRadar has an optional line "Always be enemy team" that replaces mov ebx,[rsi+0x314] at 3EC8B3 with mov ebx,4, so KittopiaCreator treats team 4 as an enemy team there (radar.md#kruul-pods-radar).

Exact source: View evidence and source details · build steam-678ccb46

Version notes and limitations

A mod that sets team 4 in one HUD path does not prove team 4 means hostile everywhere. State mutations must account for native team-manager ownership and host/client replication.
