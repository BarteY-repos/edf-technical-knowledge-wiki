EDF5 online restrictions: red over-cap equipment and authoritative armor boxes

EDF 5Game technical referencesOnline caps
Technical reference · Build-scoped static analysis; original contributor evidence grades cited; no new R126 gameplay runtime

EDF5 retains two independent kinds of online limits. Weapon levels are compared to a calculated ceiling for the selected mission/difficulty; armor boxes are limited when the host prepares each player's launch record. A red weapon cell is not a complete statement about whether a weapon can be selected in the menu.

Offline versus online metadata

In the original config.sgo, the three offline modes (campaign, DLC1, DLC2) have zero in ModeList's cap-list fields. The three online counterparts carry the indexed cap data. The byte save+0x24571 is set by the online session update when the room's weapon switch is active and the getter provides a positive cap. The armor switch is stored separately. The mode flag at node +0x5C is used to distinguish online from offline creation.

Red does not mean unselectable

EDF5's HUiHQWeaponSelect sets an above-cap bit on the relevant weapon cell; its level text is red. But the studied PressButton handler rejects only cell bits 1 and 2 (cell+0x4C & 6). It does not reject bit 0, which represents being above the online limit. Therefore the equipment menu permits that selection even though the online limit is visibly exceeded. It is incorrect to transfer EDF4.1's stricter confirm-handler behavior to EDF5.

Armor is bounded at online departure

The host's mission-start record writer takes a limit in armor boxes and clamps each participant's count if a nonnegative cap is smaller. The receiver stores the transmitted count in the player-record area. The online spawn route then computes the character's HP using the received bounded count; the offline route reads the locally saved count. Current and maximum HP are stored separately on the soldier.

A useful semantic distinction: boxes are not HP. They become HP only after applying the class-specific coefficient and base. A data comparison of an armor-box count and displayed HP would mix different units.

Question Observed EDF5 answer
Does the offline campaign use online cap lists? No; its ModeList cap entries are zero
Is an over-cap weapon marked? Yes; its cell becomes red
Does the menu block confirmation solely for over-cap bit 0? No
Is armor merely a UI restriction? No; the online launch record clamps the count
Do the offline and online spawn paths read identical armor ownership? No; local save versus received online record
