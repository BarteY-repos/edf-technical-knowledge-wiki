EDF5 ant and hornet variants: textures, speeds, base durability and level adjustment

EDF 5Game technical referencesEnemies / subtypes
Technical reference · Source analysis only (unless a source fact is contributor-verified); no new EDF runtime test

The EDF5 SGOs distinguish ants and hornets through model selection, tint parameters, level adjustment, movement and AI settings. Two definitions that share a model may behave differently, while two appearance variants can use the same engine class.

Ants

EDF5 SGO Base game_object_durability Defining source properties
giantant01, giantant01b 60 Black ant; 01b changes ant_AiSetting to [1,10,30]
giantant01netlightsyncmode 60 Black-ant model; different ant_NetworkIsLightSyncMode
giantant02b 180 Red ant model, red-hit effect
giantant03b 270 Purple/dark-crimson variation of red model; diffuse (0.25,0.5,0.5,1); movement speed 0.45
giantant04 15 Full-size ant model with green tint (0.5,1,0.5,1); movement speed 0.8
giantant04sb 15 Half-size model ari_half.mdb; ant_ex_DamageScale=2.5; grab/capture animation parameters

The small Green Ant is not simply the full-size Green Ant scaled uniformly: it selects another model and has extra grab/capture parameters. Gold Ant base entries are included in the downloadable SGO catalogue; their runtime health is further modified by game_object_level_adjust and game difficulty.

Hornets

EDF5 SGO Base durability Difference
giantbee01 and giantbee01charge 45 Same model; differ in bee_FlyAngle and bee_FlySideMoveSpeed
giantbee02charge 90 Red tint (diffuse starts 1.25,0.1); level adjustment 1.5
giantbeequeen 3000 Ordinary hornet queen, no red-queen adjustment
giantbeequeen02 3000 Red/Demon Queen tint and level adjustment 1.5

For example, the red hornet's source-adjusted baseline is 90 × 1.5 = 135, not simply 90. These are source-side base quantities; online scaling, stage multipliers and per-mission overrides must not be ignored when predicting actual HP.

Evidence: native SGO root comparisons; EDF5 Knowledge Hub facts edf5-ant-hornet-subtypes and edf5-hornet-subtypes, static. In-game subtype naming in the notes is attributed to contributor knowledge, not newly retested here.
