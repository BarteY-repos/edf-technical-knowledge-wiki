Havok (ragdolls and map collision)

EDF 4.1 ragdoll SHKT files are Havok 2014 Binary Tagfiles (hk_2014.1.0-r1)

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: shkt-2014-tagfile

EDF 4.1 ragdoll SHKT files are Havok 2014 Binary Tagfiles (hk_2014.1.0-r1). The nine Hector SHKT files hold 78 rigid bodies and 69 constraints in total and use 76 distinct Havok class names; the Hector301 and Hector401 main bodies each contain 158 objects.

Basis: exact decode to end of file, 9/9

Attribution: EDF41-to-EDF6 handoff (Bartey), TECHNICAL_HANDOFF_ORIGINAL section 3.1, Havok direct-load audit section 1 · 2026-10-01

Maps

EDF 4.1 MAPB records: T1 56 bytes, T2 68 bytes

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: mapb-mac-layout

EDF 4.1 MAPB records: T1 56 bytes, T2 68 bytes; MAPB carries a meaningful 200-byte auxiliary region and five field-11 rel32 pointers. MAC child descriptors carry non-zero u2 values (and u1 values that the converter has to normalize); EDF6 uses zeros.

Basis: the project's map frontend

Attribution: EDF41-to-EDF6 handoff (Bartey), R1E1 runtime crash handoff sections 3.2, 6.5 · 2026-10-03

SHFB terrain vegetation table layout

Evidence: static · Build: steam (EDF 4.1 data files; no code address) · Fact ID: shfb-vegetation-table

Vegetation table of EDF 4.1's SK terrain maps (.shfb, at 0x4C): {resolution, types offset, type count, cells offset, jitter offset}; types of 0x28 {kind 1 grass / 2 model, name, 0, size, random amount, parameter SGO}; cells are (type, jitter) byte pairs, rows along z (SK_HEIGEN02 2000 x 2000 at 1 m, SK_VALLEY01 1000 x 1000 at 2 m). EDF6 keeps plants in the FMB instead (../edf6/file-formats.md#fmb-plants-cables).

Basis: the author's reading of the files (rows along z checked: grass on steep slopes of SK_VALLEY01 / SK_SANGAKU01 averages under a third of the map's mean slope, a transposed grid shows no difference)

SHFB terrain color and texture-index setup

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-terrain-color-settings

Terrain color settings (SHFB): the terrain-setting constructor F84F0 first fills the four texture-index bytes with FF FF FF FF, then writes as many as the layer's setting count; a layer with one color setting has no multiplier (SK_KAIGAN01's sand layers 0 and 4 pack as [8,255,1,255] / [9,255,1,255]), and the terrain pixel shader (file offset 0x9BE120, sha256 44d4d972a0f004b85a76011e4cc2fcc58b7cc22a4f73cf01b96e6afd13703dae) skips the second multiply with an ine ... 255 branch; the other six SK maps have a multiplier on every layer. SK_KAIGAN01's .cols.dds is BC1_UNORM (DXGI 71), 1024 x 1024, 11 slices, 11 mips, bound by its .cols.sgo as cliff01 0, fldsub01 1, fldsub02 2, grass01 / 02 / 03 / 05 3-6, rock01 7, sand01 8, sand02 9, soil02 10 (its SHFB sha256 18c6476ff1da5494a33a88a720845eb08453193abe33155b2d5bf320b1a36fb3). Leaves often use more than five layers: most per leaf and leaves above five in SK_CRATER01 9 / 49, SK_HEIGEN02 6 / 62, SK_KAIGAN01 10 / 103, SK_SANGAKU01 13 / 137, SK_SANGAKU02 10 / 106, SK_STEPMOUNTAIN01 10 / 113, SK_VALLEY01 9 / 79 (EDF6's terrain shaders take up to 15, ../edf6/file-formats.md#terrain-layer-shaders).

Basis: the author's static reading of EDF41.exe and their reading of the seven SK maps; the build id is the hub's EDF 4.1 build, the note names none

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): F84F0

Attribution: hajisensai, All Forces source docs/feedback-2026-10-19-terrain-materials.md (round 24) · 2026-11-07

Game-data tables (SGO, Root.cpk)

EDF4.1 WEAPONTABLE.SGO row structure

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-weapon-table

weapon/_weapontable.sgo (root table) lists 827 weapons as 6-value rows [id, weapon file, category, rarity, level, acquisition] (EDF5's rows add stars and source, ../edf5/file-formats.md#weapon-table-sgo); weapon/_weapontext.sgo (root text_table) holds 827 rows of 6 strings in the same order, [Japanese name, Japanese description, English name, English description, ?, ?], the third pair equal to the Japanese in the rows sampled (a third language slot, unverified) (the weapon SGOs carry no name.* value). The category is class x 10 + kind, the index into config.sgo's SoldierWeaponCategory (one key per index, gaps as 0): 0-6 Ranger, 10-17 Wing Diver (Weapon_Pale_*), 20-25 Fencer (Weapon_Heavy_*), 30-39 Air Raider (Weapon_Engineer_*, 36-39 vehicles), not the class-select order of SoldierInit (Ranger, Wing Diver, Air Raider, Fencer). Rarity runs 0-1.3 (1.0 in 720 rows, 0 in the 5 rows that cannot drop, 0.2-0.9 in 101 high-level rows, #edf41-weapon-rarity-weights; this line said "0.3-1.3" until KH chat 82); acquisition 0 in 787 rows, 3 in 26, 1 in 14 (AssultRifle01 1, level 0).

Basis: decoded from EDF 4.1's Root.cpk with _tools/catalog.py edf41 (scratch File_Storage/Logs/catalog/edf41_survey.py, edf41_cats.py); value meanings by analogy with EDF5, not read in EDF41.exe

Attribution: KH chat 72 (curator) · 2026-11-09

Offline and online mission list SGO records

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-mission-lists

EDF 4.1's mission lists: mission/missionlist.sgo (89 rows) and mission/onlinemissionlist.sgo (98 rows), root table, rows [app:/Mission/<folder>, <folder>] (no flag or progress value, unlike EDF5's four-value rows); titles in missiontext.sgo / onlinemissiontext.sgo, 6 strings per row (Japanese title and briefing, English title and briefing, and a third pair equal to the Japanese in the rows sampled). The online list is the offline list in the same order plus 9 online-only missions (M410, M407, M504, M512, M302, M310, M204, M208, M209 at online 24, 30, 34, 52, 73, 91, 93, 96, 97). demomissionlist.sgo has 3 rows (M_TGS2014 first). Root.cpk has 155 mission.bvm folders in all, test scenes (t_*) among them; the Mission Pack lists are not in Root.cpk or Chunk01.cpk (Chunk01 holds 133 map files only) but in the packs' own archives (#edf41-dlc-archives, #edf41-dlc-lists).

Basis: decoded from Root.cpk (_tools/catalog.py edf41, catalog/missions.md); the row order taken to be the in-game order as in EDF5, not checked in game; the EDF 4.1 Japanese wiki numbers its 98 main missions in this online order, every title equal, and finds the 89 offline ones by title (wiki-checks.md#edf41-wiki-vs-scripts-3)

Native application of online equipment caps

EDF 4.1's config.sgo ModeList has three modes (GameMode_Scenario, GameMode_OnlineScenario, GameMode_Versus)

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-modelist-drop-values

EDF 4.1's config.sgo ModeList has three modes (GameMode_Scenario, GameMode_OnlineScenario, GameMode_Versus); the two scenario modes carry the per-difficulty arrays EDF5 uses for its drop level (../edf5/file-formats.md#modelist-drop-level-values), here Easy [0, 0.8, 0.2], Normal [0, 1.0, 0.2], Hard [0.85, 2.0, 0.5], Hardest [1.76, 3.0, 2.4], Inferno [2.0, 4.0, 2.4]: the same low and high as EDF5 except Hard's low, and far larger spreads on Hard and up (EDF5: 0.25, 0.3, 1.0). If EDF 4.1's loot roll reads them as EDF5's does (../edf5/drop-items.md#loot-roll-level-window), its Hardest and Inferno drops reach 60 levels below the window's top; EDF41.exe's roll reads them so, without EDF5's small margin above the center (#edf41-loot-roll-window, KH chat 81).

SHFB terrain and localized data caveats

These eight source IDs preserve binary/source observations from hajisensai / All Forces on EDF4.1 build steam-57aab039, plus novae (ajane) / FevGrave cross-checks. Values from a tool that copied EDF5 settings into its EDF4.1 tables are not treated as authentic EDF4.1 values.

SHFB block, vertex material and texture array structure

Static · hajisensai: SHFB endian fixup F37A0 provides a field/offset inventory; terrain construction F4B00 uploads its arrays. The game's embedded vertex shader (file offset 9C6030) runs f16tof32 on the low 16-bit half of each height sample, establishing the half-float-height interpretation with an independent shader consumer.

Static: the SHFB terrain is divided into 33×33 blocks of 32 cells; a 0x20-byte block-table record (from file 0x850 + 0x20×index, table at header +0x38) lists its material layers and offsets into vertex blend runs at +0x54. A run stores (layer slot, f16 weight) pairs for participating layers, terminated by a zero-weight pair. Do not assume four fixed channels at every vertex.

Static: a layer table at header +0x3C contains 0x14-byte descriptors pointing to up to two 0x30-byte texture-settings structures with texture index and scale (UV = metres × scale). The material-name list at +0x44/+0x48 distinguishes color textures from _n/_nm normals; color array .cols.dds uses DX10 BC1 slices, bound by .cols.sgo. In SK_HEIGEN02 the sample contains eight 1024² color slices, ordered as the source non-normal names.

Static: SK terrain .norm.dds arrays, referenced by .norm.sgo, contain one slice in some maps and four in others. What each extra normal slice represents was not established; do not assign a speculative meaning from slice count.

Static seven-map census: each heightfield sample's upper 16 bits encode its largest-weight material-layer index; the decoded layer equals the argmax blend weight at 99.9995% of vertices, with weights summing to 1. Header min/max heights at +0x30/+0x34 match decoded half-float extrema (e.g. SK_HEIGEN02 about -10.2266 to 47.25 m). Rare disagreements are corpus exceptions rather than evidence of another encoding.

Static placement cross-check: source MAPB places SHFB at (-1000,0,-1000) for SK_HEIGEN02 and (-1000,118.4,-1000) for SK_VALLEY01; 1,001 samples ×2 m span 2,000 m, with world X from column and Z from row. Across seven maps, 74–100% of placed objects were within 1 m of decoded ground, with median vertical differences 0.000–0.29 m. These statistics corroborate origin/orientation; they do not validate every mesh triangle collision.

Drop-table import warning and one localized mission-title difference

Unverified external-tool attribution · FevGrave, novae: one outside tool's nominal EDF4.1 drop-level ranges reproduce EDF5's numbers (Easy 0.01–0.95, Hardest 1.76–3.0 with 0.3 spread, Inferno 2.0–4.0 with 1.0 spread) and conflict with direct EDF4.1 ModeList values (Easy 0–0.8, Hardest/Inferno spread 2.4). For conflicting fields the decoded EDF4.1 tables take precedence; do not promote copied EDF5 bands to valid 4.1 game data. Its 827 weapon rows, acquisition-class count 787/14/26 and no DLC-specific weapon table did match the separate EDF4.1 corpus.

Static localized-text comparison · FevGrave, novae: the EDF4.1 English main campaign online label for script m162 (offline mission 60 / online 64) is “Bad Premonition”, while the offline list calls it “Ill Omen”. All other compared main-campaign online/offline title pairs agreed in the 472 English/Japanese title check. Generated catalog pages using the offline title and wiki/online pages using the online title therefore legitimately differ here; do not silently overwrite one with the other.
