EDF4.1 Earth Eater: PanelBase, turret locators, weak points and phases

EDF 4.1Game technical referencesBosses and objects
Technical reference · Attribution and grade per source fact; this edition adds no game runtime test

EDF4.1's Earth Eater (UfoMother401) has a fundamentally different organization from UfoMother301. Its “hull” is mainly a layout controller: the visible ceiling and weapons are separate panels, cores, generators and turrets. The research source states that the top-level hull has no model, collision or durability of its own; scripts and part objects control the actual encounter.

The panel family

Each PanelBase can fly into place, shrink/disappear, break and fall, or fly away. A panel's damage gate checks a named SHKT weak-point body from its SGO. Panels with no qualifying weakpoint, including certain Panel01 armor variants, can remain physically visible but invulnerable through that test. This is why hitboxes and collision layers alone do not determine whether a panel accepts damage.

One source-defined weak panel death event destroys other panels within a 200-metre horizontal radius after local randomized delays. A core has a separate, extended death sequence with staged explosions. The code also has a non-reward vanish route distinct from genuine object death: its result should not be counted as the ordinary killing of a destructible enemy.

Where are its turrets attached?

Turret mounts named 砲台 are found in group 0 of an embedded MAB inside the panel's SGO animation_model, not in the basic two-bone MDB or in the SHKT collision file. The source inventory reports:

Panel type Mounts
Main-game Panel01, Panel01S, Panel01SC 10 each
Main-game Panel02B, Panel02C 11 each
Mission Pack 2 DLC_Panel01, DLC_PanelS01, DLC_PanelS02 21 each
Mission Pack 2 DLC_Panel02C 11

A mount does not guarantee that a turret will appear. parts_define contains weighted entries, with [0, weight] representing an empty mount. The native constructor rolls an entry for each 砲台 locator. Hence actual blue/purple/red/green gun totals can vary in the investigated layouts; their exact per-run reproducibility was not fully demonstrated.

Cannons, generators and event wiring

The research source separates SmallLaser (target distance under about 700 m) from Sniper, Plasma and Rapid cannon families (about 500 m in the surveyed path). Their cooldowns and projectile types differ. The large cannon panels, FinalCannon types, generators and final generators have their own attacks/encounters. The generator family uses a generic wave controller supporting periodic refill or one-shot waves.

The original EDF4.1 mission scripts configure these objects through panel layout and ObjectAction calls rather than assuming the entire Earth Eater comes from a single immutable asset. The source's mission counts sometimes come from All Forces' converted mission dataset, and those counts are marked converted-corpus evidence, not fresh vanilla-game counts. For example, one M018 phase is recorded as 361 panels plus a set of turret parts, followed by final-form panels and generators; this is a source-matched layout example, not proof that every difficulty instantiates identical numbers of enemies.

Earth Eater collision and effects: separate concerns

A panel's SHKT/body collision layer, a turret's MAB locator, a script's active/inactive state and its damage filter are distinct systems. A recreated EDF4.1 panel in EDF6 may inherit the wrong collision layer (an investigated EDF6 wrapper could set 17 instead of original panel layer 0) while still having correct visual locators. Conversely, successful shots at the shell do not prove the core's hidden weakpoint behavior is preserved.
