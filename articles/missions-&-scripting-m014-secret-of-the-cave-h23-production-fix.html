Final frog: route assignment is timing-critical

The final カエル04 keeps exact accepted H1E placement. Its initialization must remain:

CreateEnemy(...)
SetAiRoute(...)

with no Wait between them.

A controlled 1.0-second hold failed; restoring immediate route assignment restored correct behavior. This is direct runtime evidence that adding a seemingly harmless delay changes compatibility semantics for this actor. Do not “clean up” this sequence by inserting scheduler waits.

The accepted no-caption native-style frog roar/reaction path remains part of the production composition.

Geometry / encounter compatibility deltas

  • 岩09 remains at the accepted +11 Z rear-wall position.
  • 岩12 is a persistent approach blocker created during mission initialization and removed only after the final frog is created and routed. Creating it only at the final event is too late because the player can pre-position on H1E.
  • The concentrated reinforcement wave preserves source counts: 15 ordinary ants + 10 red ants + 15 spiders.
  • That wave must use its dedicated anchor translated exactly 5.0 units forward along the H1E -> first-route-waypoint XZ direction; do not reuse the final frog/H1E position as the reinforcement anchor.

Do not regress

Do not insert a wait between frog creation and route assignment, move the frog away from H1E without new evidence, revert 岩09 from +11Z, delay creation of 岩12 until the final event, reuse H1E as the 40-enemy wave anchor, reopen the solved roar/subtitle path, or replace the full H18 downstream event graph with a pruned diagnostic graph.

The concentrated reinforcement is three source calls, not a duplicate-spawn artifact

The original EDF5 mission BVM contains three consecutive cuscall0 2002 creation operations corresponding to the concentrated 15 ordinary ants / 10 red ants / 15 spiders wave. Comparing the earlier complete compatibility mission with its source-derived revision found the same enemy-create call sequence, without added, reordered or duplicated creation sites. The unusual visual concentration at one anchor is therefore a placement/geometry issue, not evidence that the converter introduced a fourth wave or duplicated enemies. The accepted resolution changes the wave's independent anchor by the exact 5.0-unit XZ offset already recorded above while preserving the three authentic source wave counts.

The next distributed wave remains 37 + 27 + 37 with its own downstream event graph; it is neither merged with nor substituted for the earlier concentrated wave. A full production candidate may satisfy the local collision and spawn tests while still requiring a final mission-clear sanity run. That distinction is part of the acceptance boundary, not an unresolved source script opcode.

Evidence: original BVM/call comparison M014_H18_EXTRA_WAVE_SOURCE_PARITY_AUDIT_2026-09-10.json and final promoted source-to-target contract M014_PRODUCTION_FIX_H23_PROMOTION_2026-09-11.json; source-derived/static checks and scoped direct modified-game runtime, not a universal enemy-balance law.

How representative was the Humanoid_Basic cave-spawn problem?

A bounded source audit of all 141 EDF5 shipping mission scripts scanned e503_*, e506_* and e617_* Humanoid_Basic-family constructors. It found 70 missions containing those families: 54 in the base pack, 9 in DLC1 and 7 in DLC2. Of these, 61 were classified outdoor, seven cave/interior and two Base502. These are source-constructor and map classifications, not 70 malfunctioning missions.

The earlier M014 and DLC1 DM001 cases were the two demonstrated initialization/ejection problems in the audit. Controlled native target-MAD geometric checks then found 11/11 source frog spawn rows in M027 / CAVE501 free of the same standing-capsule overlap, and 3/3 E506 rows in M062 / CAVE503 in large chambers (measured ceiling distances approximately 83–121 units). M029 / CAVE504 and M072_2 / EDFRoom02 were focused runtime-smoke candidates, not verified broken or automatically repaired missions.

The main discriminator was the interaction of constrained initial geometry with immediate route/locomotion activation, not Humanoid_Basic identity alone. Static clearance is useful but insufficient: a spawn that clears a simple geometric test can still be displaced later by running-game physics. This evidence cannot justify moving every frog/grey spawn in the converted corpus or describing the issue as a clean retail EDF5 defect.
