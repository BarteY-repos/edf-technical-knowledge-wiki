Original NAV LOW/HIGH and EDF4.1 collision clearance — evidence and limits

UniversalMaps & NavigationNavigationEDF 4.1EDF 5EDF 6
Technical reference · Original source-file static research; bounded offline geometry; no live NPC runtime proof

Evidence: selected original EDF5/EDF6 HKT files, bounded original EDF4.1 MAC/MAD geometry, and offline static analysis (17 November 2026). Not evidence of correct NPC pathfinding in a live EDF6 mission. This article concerns game-engine data and measured original-source geometry; the research tools used to inspect them are not part of the game's file format.

Two native NAV resolutions are materially different

The original NW_EUROPE01 pair of maps in EDF5 and EDF6 each contains separate low- and high-detail Havok hkaiNavMesh files. Their native topology is not one shared mesh with different bounds:

Original-file measurement EDF5 LOW EDF6 LOW EDF5 HIGH EDF6 HIGH
NavMesh tiles 4 4 16 16
Faces 4,419 4,059 32,787 30,216
Edges 21,858 20,329 122,457 114,551
Hierarchy nodes 218 205 1,665 1,543

Both variants contain native clearance-cache data. A root erosionRadius of zero in this example does not prove that NPCs have a zero physical radius. The counts describe one matched map and game builds, not universal LOW/HIGH file sizes or an algorithm that can be copied to another map.

EDF4.1 original map sources in the examined corpus do not supply a transferable NAVMESH.HKT file. Thus source terrain and collision geometry must first be identified and qualified before target-style NAV can be generated. A structurally valid HKT/NVMD pair does not prove agent clearance, active-body semantics or gameplay behavior. See HKT/NVMD compatibility versus rebake and EDF4.1 4 m source terrain.

Original EDF4.1 source-body attribution: a floor is not automatically walkable

The source-backed 4 m layer studies in two small map windows separated candidate height/surface samples by whether their original MAPB/MAD body owner was classified as a field source. A nonfield-only layer might represent a platform, bridge, roof, staircase or another meaningful surface; classifying it as nonfield is not grounds to delete it from navigation.

Bounded map/source window Candidate upward sheet nodes Field-candidate Nonfield-only Unresolved
NW_HILLYCITY_LIGHT 568 503 65 0
NW_SOUKOGAI01 226 200 26 0

The numbers apply to the studied two ROIs, not the complete city maps. Field source is a selected source-object anchor, not proof of native walkability or real contact activation. The initial step-only graph already missed obstacles at some connecting edges.

Centerline, side and radius experiments on original source triangles

A four-neighbor grid can make a plausible NAV connection even when real collision triangles intersect that path. Direct original-source tests distinguish observed contacts from candidate edges with no sampled contact.

Map window and NAV profile Candidate edges Centerline contacts Additional side-ray contacts Remaining edges without sampled contact
HillyCity LOW 1,216 93 47 1,076
HillyCity HIGH 1,154 56 41 1,057
Soukogai LOW 419 0 8 411
Soukogai HIGH 401 0 5 396

The original geometric source windows contained 2,035 clipped facets in HillyCity and 724 in Soukogai. Centerline tests sampled three heights (0.25, 0.9 and 1.7 m); side tests added lateral offsets of ±0.75 m. Contacts are source-triangle intersections conditional on actual actor/rigid-body activation and placement. In particular, 0 intersected centerline rays in Soukogai did not prove that the crossing corridor was free.

Separate sparse source-geometric sphere-distance samples at radii 0.25, 0.50 and 0.75 m found further contacts beyond those point-ray samples:

Radius HillyCity new LOW / HIGH Soukogai new LOW / HIGH
0.25 m 1 / 0 0 / 0
0.50 m 4 / 2 7 / 5
0.75 m 18 / 16 19 / 11

These are chosen diagnostic radii, not the measured dimensions of Ranger, Fencer, Hector or any EDF6 navigation agent. The sphere tests use sparse segment axes, not a full upright swept capsule or verified native Havok clearance. Nor should the per-radius counts be added to infer a total number of distinct NPC failures.

MAPB group placement changes the collision interpretation

The bounded HillyCity native-source index showed five group-owner records with 40 child links. Of those linked children, only one resolved to the selected static MAD collision-source route; the other 39 remain unresolved, not nonexistent or non-colliding. For that one child, using its stored pose independently placed its conservative collision candidate inside the selected window, while hypothetically composing its parent group's pose moved its conservative candidate elsewhere. Soukogai had no group links in this control.

This shows sensitivity to transform interpretation; it does not establish whether original EDF4.1 actually applies parent-composed transforms at runtime. Applying parent composition blindly, or discarding 39 unresolved child links, could corrupt NAV exclusions. The source's candidate Rz*Ry*Rx actor pose and activation/layer rules are still not native-runtime verified.

What remains unproven

These measurements do not close full-map coverage, native collision-body activation, actor/group world transforms, layered roofs and caves, agent capsule size/height, ledges, erosion, bridge semantics or native LOW/HIGH polygonization. In-game NPC behavior for Ranger/Fencer/Hector, vehicle movement and the 16-map-family scope remain unqualified. No result here establishes a playable NPC-pathfinding fix.
