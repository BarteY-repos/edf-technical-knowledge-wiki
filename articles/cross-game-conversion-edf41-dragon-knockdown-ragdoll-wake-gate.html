EDF4.1 giant dragon in EDF6 — ragdoll settling, knockdown resets and the 60-second wake cap

ConversionCross-game ConversionCollision & PhysicsEDF 4.1EDF 6
Technical reference · Source-inherited exact-build state RE, historical M166 report and pure gate test

Why a dragon may lie motionless while its AI is otherwise alive

The examined EDF6 DragonSmall class can enter a knockdown state from a hit with a nonzero knock value, from a hit while already down or from active ragdoll state. Every subsequent qualifying hit while down can restart the knockdown state clock. A source EDF4.1 DragonBig401 mapped to this target class may therefore appear unable to recover even while target logic continues updating.

In the studied EDF6 path, entering the state plays fly_damage_large, enables selected ragdoll bodies and later transitions to the down clip. Recovery depends on both elapsed time and an aggregate measure of ragdoll motion:

settle = Σ over ragdoll bodies [mass × (|linear velocity| + |angular velocity|)]

The engine's recovery condition in the investigated build is:

elapsed > 1.5 seconds AND (settle < 2000 OR elapsed > 60 seconds)

The original EDF4.1 DragonBig had the same 1.5 s / 2000 settling condition in the examined path but no 60-second hard cap. This means “wakes up at sixty seconds” is not universal source parity; it is a target-native safety behaviour. Conversely, repeated knockdown hits may continually reset the clock, so observing several sixty-second episodes does not necessarily mean that the recovery test itself is disabled.

Why diagnosis must measure the state variables

For a dragon lying still, inspect:

  1. Whether the hit really carries knock value and whether new hits re-enter the state.
  2. Whether its ragdoll is active and which bodies contribute to the velocity sum.
  3. The actual state clock (not the wall-clock elapsed since the first hit).
  4. Whether the target's S_down → wakeup transition runs with settle < 2000 or exceeds its cap.
  5. Whether a source-vs-target mismatch in the ragdoll body mass/speed makes settle stay too high even when the visible dragon looks stationary.

Do not fix this by replacing the enemy with a permanently upright model, deleting collision or globally disabling ragdoll. The problem is a state transition and physical settling contract, which has to be qualified with the exact actor model and mission.

Historical 2026-10-17 M166 observations reported the giant dragon staying down for about 60 seconds at a time. The corresponding source includes pure unit-tested gating logic, but this article does not report a newly rerun runtime or multiplayer test.

Source: EDF4.1 R10 HF4 R9 components/compatibility/src/edf41dragon_wake.h and its source-linked exact EDF41.exe / EDF6.dll state analysis.
