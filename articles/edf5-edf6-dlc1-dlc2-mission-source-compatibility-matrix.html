EDF5→EDF6 DLC1/DLC2: 29-mission source and RMPA evidence matrix

ConversionMissions & ScriptingDLC encounter and mission resourcesEDF 5EDF 6
Technical reference · Source-derived; separates static evidence and the limited modded-runtime results

The retained source-corpus audit covers all 29 EDF5 DLC missions hosted in EDF6: 15 DLC1 entries and 14 DLC2 entries. It compares native EDF5 MISSION.BVM-derived script behavior, ported MISSION.AC, and mission MISSION.RMPA target/reference profiles. This is a specific versioned port audit, not a demonstration that stock EDF5 and stock EDF6 missions share an identical format or gameplay.

The four non-exact AC missions were DM001, DM015, DM006_2, DM024; the two non-exact mission RMPAs were DM001 and DM021. These are not automatically defects: each corresponds to a previously documented gameplay/ABI/resource compatibility exception.

Three counterintuitive game-behavior cases

  • DM002 (DLC2 #2, Engage Aliens 4): the sparse enemy groups and unusually wide spawn layout are present in the clean EDF5 source. They are not proof that the port dropped waves.
  • DM010 (DLC2 #7): repeated or overlapping waves arise from source time-or-clear rearming. The source chain is finite and reaches MissionClear; duration alone does not prove an infinite spawn loop.
  • DM020 (DLC1 #6): absent tank01_5 / tank01_6 constructors reflect the source behavior. Adding them would be an ungrounded content change.

DM017: route discrepancy is a watch item, not a proven bug

In the examined source RMPA, the inner route named 飛行カエル03_2 refers to points/names corresponding to 飛行カエル03_1. The tested reference profile zeros that inner route. Similar sparse zeroing occurs deliberately in other target profiles. With no runtime symptom attributed to this exact discrepancy, it was retained unchanged. A controlled RMPA-only comparison would be needed before presenting it as a fix.

Limits

The 2026-09-21 audit determined that no additional production mission regeneration was justified by its evidence. Later source and runtime work may supersede particular missions; these 29 entries document the state and reasons of this bounded comparison, not immutable truths about every subsequent patch. For separately detected int/float scalar problems see ten-mission numeric census. For mission routing/storage semantics see MISSION.RMPA structure.
