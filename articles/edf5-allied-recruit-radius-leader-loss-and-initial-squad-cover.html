EDF5 recruitment: 10-metre nearby-player check, squad reassignment and initial protection

EDF 5Game technical referencesAllied NPC AI
Technical reference · Source analysis only (unless a source fact is contributor-verified); no new EDF runtime test

EDF5 mission natives CreateFriend (1010), CreateFriendGroup (1012) and CreateFriendSquad (1011) set the object's recruitability state. In a scripted squad, the leader gets the specified canRecruit setting and subordinates can later become recruitable after their original leader disappears. The separate RecruitObject native (3152) can directly attach one unit to another through SetFollow.

An unled soldier whose may-join flag is set searches the relevant player/team group for the nearest live HumanBase within 10 world units. The online branch avoids treating a remote avatar copy as the local recruit target in the observed logic. Recruitment attaches the soldier, selects a formation direction at approximately 2–5 units from the player and uses a temporary reassignment timer.

When a leader dies, the source shows an unfollow operation, route release, cooldown and potential new-player recruitment. It does not mean every unled NPC is automatically recruitable; the original SGO/native flag must still allow it.

The first-30-seconds source pattern

The researcher also found a 1800-frame initial budget in certain un-recruited squad formations, with reduced factors for visibility/targeting, damage and weapon output while the formation remains rooted in its original NPC leader. Recruiting into a player-led chain removes that condition. The field meanings were interpreted partly by analogy with matching EDF6 code and are not fully independently established in EDF5. Consequently the exact amount of incoming damage mitigation in live EDF5 should not be asserted solely from these registers.
