Projectiles & Ammunition

EDF 6DocumentationGameplay & AI
Technical reference · Community-provided Japanese memo; source-specific and not independently rerun

EDF6 weapons can emit nested projectile types. The outer weapon SGO and the inner Ammo_CustomParameter structures jointly determine behavior; changing only the child bullet class may lose guidance, damage flags or rendering.

Useful experiment-derived class differences

ClusterBullet01 sample field positions

Source 解析!B1467:J1474 describes the following, within that exact field profile:

  1. per-frame speed multiplier (sample 0.99);
  2. baseline emission angle (sample 1.57 radians);
  3. angle variance (3.14 spans a full turn in the author's notes);
  4. discrete direction mode: 0 downward, 1 upward, 2 forward, 3 forward/upward; values ≥4 appeared rightward;
  5. guidance flag.

Parent AmmoAlive influences start of child emission. A low particle speed cannot substitute for a timing field. The note warns that extreme travel distance caused crashes.

Detection and damage are separate

BombBullet02 detector type uses a configurable delay before arming. The contributor distinguished a trigger responding when any part of the enemy enters the volume from one requiring the enemy center to enter it. The stored numeric mode-to-behavior mapping is incomplete; do not assert all enum values.

AmmoExplosion in that detector example is thus a detection radius, distinct from a separate AI search radius such as the accepted-omission SetAiSearchRadius(3450) see compatibility note.

Sentry/projectile chaining

The source reports that using an intermediate ClusterBullet01 can allow classes requiring extra ammo-model handling to be spawned downstream; one grenade variant was tested. It does not demonstrate a working sentry that deploys another sentry. See also Weapon RNG and projectile reconstruction for multiplayer concerns.

Source: 解析!B65:O236, 解析!B1060:K1090, 解析!B1467:J1486, 解析!B2013:K2031 in the community workbook. Values should be treated as class/profile-specific experiments.

Damage before and after projectile contact

Native bullet Flag 1 / Flag 2 and layer filtering distinguishes a blocking ray from the shape-cast used to hit a target. Source-level attack curves are available for insects and tadpoles, drones and Deroys, Erginus/Archelus and Android/Scylla/Siren/Haze. Projectile damage must not be equated with final HP loss before count, collision and ModeList are known.
