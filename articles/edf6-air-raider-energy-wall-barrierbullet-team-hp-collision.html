EDF6 Air Raider energy wall: BarrierBullet01 geometry, hostile-shot filtering and durability

EDF 6Game technical referencesNative projectiles and support
Technical reference · Build-specific source evidence; no new gameplay test

The energy wall deployed by Air Raider equipment such as EWEAPON196 is implemented in the examined EDF6 executable as BarrierBullet01. Despite its appearance as a stationary shield, its lifecycle and contact handling belong to a bullet-derived native object.

Shape and placement

Its Ammo_CustomParameter carries five entries: an arc in radians, radius, height, three-axis scale and local offset. The wall's curved collision surface is divided into approximately int(arc / 0.0872) segments, centered along the object's local +Z axis, with an additional 2 m skirt below it. The source identifies collision layer 14 and an associated BarrierInfo metadata record.

Once the deployed bullet touches the environment, a state flag anchors its position. The update path still synchronizes its collision transform with its stored bullet transform rather than treating it as arbitrary static map geometry.

Why friendly and enemy shots interact differently

The wall records a team at creation, inherited from its firing/support-unit context. Its candidate selection filters hostile teams' bullets. This is the native basis for the familiar result in which enemy projectiles collide with the wall while allied projectiles can pass. The team is captured once at construction; the research does not establish an arbitrary dynamic team-change mechanism for an already-deployed wall.

The object has a float durability/HP field. Incoming damage messages reduce it, with an exception for the cited forced-replay flag. At zero HP or the object's deletion condition, the wall is removed; the original path does not create a generic shatter effect at destruction. In online play the object sends an appropriate delete notification unless its special state suppresses that send.

Evidence limitations

The source includes instruction-level RE and a private native-mapping test of damage reducing the wall's HP until deletion. That is not a complete gameplay test of every shield equipment, network peer or damage type. Numeric offsets and vtables refer to the studied Steam build and must not be applied to another executable blindly.
