Player soldier skeletons (key: soldier model bone names)

  • edf41: the player models are ArmySoldier.mdb (Ranger) and Engineer.mdb (Air Raider) in OBJECT/ARMYANDENGINEER.MRAB, PaleWing.mdb in OBJECT/PALEWING.MRAB, HeavyArmor.mdb in OBJECT/HEAVYARMOR.MRAB (its LoDs are named HeavyArmo_Lod1 / _Lod2), each with _Lod1 / _Lod2 | ref: crosswalk/models-mdb.md | evidence: static
  • edf6: the player SGOs the port rigs them onto: P505_RANGER, P506_WINGDIVER, P507_FENCER, P508_AIRRADER (bones 91 / 99 / 110 / 85 after the port). Bones pair by name (44 to 52 per class) except: 4.1 kubi = EDF6 head; 4.1 otherFinger0..2_l / _r = EDF6 middle0..2_l / _r; the Fencer's backWeapon_l / _r and backWeaponJoint_l / _r are swapped (4.1's left is EDF6's right). EDF6 bones with no 4.1 counterpart (armor pieces, the Air Raider's radar and back slide, the Fencer's skirt and covers) were parented to the nearest 4.1 bone, and the Wing Diver's booster bones folded onto mune; after the fit the largest joint error is 3 cm (Ranger, Air Raider), 5 cm (Fencer) and 6 cm (Wing Diver), the hands turned by up to 26 degrees | evidence: static
  • Differences noticed: the same skeleton family with a few renamed and mirrored bones, so EDF 4.1 outfits run on EDF6 soldiers after a rename map; animations keyed by bone name need the same map.
  • Confidence in the mapping: medium (the author's fit report edf41port/outfits.json, built by its tools/make_edf41_outfits.py).
  • Added: 2026-11-07 src: hajisensai, All Forces source edf41port/outfits.json (round 24, KH chat 57)

  • edf41: categories by class as the 4.1 weapon table numbers them: Ranger 0-6, Wing Diver 10-17, Fencer 24-25 (in the ported set), Air Raider 31-39 (31 and 36-39 the support calls and vehicle requests) | ref: none yet (no category fact line for EDF 4.1) | evidence: static
  • edf5: the categories in the ported set: Ranger 0-8, Wing Diver 107 / 108, Fencer 200-209, Air Raider 302-320; most keep their number in EDF6 | ref: none yet | evidence: static
  • edf6: the category the port gives each (and the weapon class it runs on): 4.1 Ranger 0-6 keep their number (Weapon_BasicShoot, Weapon_Throw for 6); 4.1 Wing Diver 10 -> 100, 11 -> 101, 12 -> 102, 13 -> 103, 14 -> 104, 15 -> 105 (Weapon_BasicShoot, or Weapon_Throw for 4.1's thrown 15 weapons), 17 -> 105; 4.1 Fencer 24 -> 204 (Weapon_HeavyShoot), 25 -> 205 (Weapon_HomingShoot); 4.1 Air Raider 31 -> 313 / 314 (Weapon_LaserMarkerCallFire), 32 -> 302 (Weapon_Sub), 33 -> 303, 34 and 35 -> 305 (Weapon_BasicShoot); the 84 support calls (edf41port/calls.json): 31 -> 311 (smoke candles, Weapon_BasicShoot) or 312 (radio calls, Weapon_RadioContact), 36 -> 306 or 8, 37 -> 307 or 10, 38 -> 308, 39 -> 309 or 320 (all Weapon_Sub). EDF5 keeps its number except 304 -> 305, 107 -> 114 and 108 -> 120 (classes Weapon_BasicShoot, Weapon_Sub, Weapon_Throw, Weapon_Accessory, Weapon_ImpactHammer 200, Weapon_Shield 202, Weapon_HomingShoot 205, Weapon_LaserMarkerCallFire 313). Known EDF6 meanings: 100 Weapon_Pale_Short, 111 Weapon_Pale_SupportShort, 305 Weapon_Engineer_Special, 308 vehicle requests (edf6/weapons.md#basicshoot-fire-slot, edf6/netcode.md#net-vehicle-request-configure) | evidence: static
  • Differences noticed: EDF 4.1's Wing Diver and Air Raider ranges are numbered differently from EDF5 / EDF6's (10s vs 100s, 30s vs 300s); the port moved 284 4.1 weapons and 61 EDF5 weapons (28 and 17 skipped), each with its own SGO built from the source table's values (weapon items; unmapped_stats counts values with no EDF6 key).
  • Confidence in the mapping: medium (the author's mapping, run in game; the categories' meanings in each game's table are not in the hub).
  • Added: 2026-11-07 src: hajisensai, All Forces source edf41port/weapons.json, edf5port/weapons.json (round 24, KH chat 57)
