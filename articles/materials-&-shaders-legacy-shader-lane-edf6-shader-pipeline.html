EDF6 legacy shader ABI: SND_CHARA_BASIC and unresolved compositor routing

EDF 6Materials & ShadersShaders
Source-backed research / case study · Reconciled

Some EDF6 material resources retain older parameter families including SND_CHARA_BASIC and specular_pow. Their presence must not be silently interpreted as an ordinary snd_BRDF_* shader or translated into a modern roughness scalar.

A bounded runtime-positive source/material conversion preserved a legacy Simple_Clip ABI instead of renaming it to a BRDF family. That establishes a compatibility boundary, not the exact native final-frame compositor routing of every legacy material.

Unresolved native renderer question

For a named legacy pixel shader with specular_pow and no BRDF marker, the presently retained evidence does not establish whether its outputs feed exactly the same scene-compositor render targets as newer Common/BRDF paths. Verifying that would require examining:

  1. final SV_TARGET writes and output register count/shape;
  2. the destinations of specular_pow, specular_color, light_smooth;
  3. actual shader-bound render targets and compositor inputs on the studied game build;
  4. a frame capture, not just a visually plausible replacement material.

Until then, a legacy material's source ABI is the reference. No universally correct specular_pow -> metallic/roughness conversion formula follows from the available evidence.
