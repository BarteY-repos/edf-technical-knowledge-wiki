EDF6 gold drones, Imperial drones and Deroys: shots and level curves

EDF 6Game technical referencesGameplay & AI
Technical reference · Static/source-backed community evidence; verified test results identified separately; no R117 native runtime tests

Evidence: Static, build-specific analysis of EDF6 steam-678ccb46, contributed to the EDF Knowledge Hub (14 November 2026, revision 54f89cd). The data below is sourced from native enemy level-setup routines and named SGO rows. It does not certify in-game DPS or online damage for every scenario.

Gold drones (UfoSmall507): bullet_info is a mini expression language

The gold drone's bullet_info is not a fixed damage scalar. Each entry can be a literal or a sequence of operations on raw level L. The native evaluator supports addition, multiplication, bounded minimum/maximum, reciprocal, interpolation and other operators. The resolved values determine burst count, spacing, bullets per shot, damage, speed, size, lifetime and (for bomber variants) explosion size/gravity.

Variant and projectile Shot pattern Damage per bullet Other properties
e507_goldufo, _hi: BeamBullet01 Five shots per burst, spaced five frames; five bullets in a line per shot 0.07L + 1 Speed 0.015L + 0.5; size min(0.003L + 0.3,0.6); life 300 frames
_ace: LaserBullet01 30 single shots, spaced one frame max(0.0476 × (0.6L+4),0.1) Speed 30; size 0.4; life 15 frames
_charge: LaserBullet01 Unlimited burst; one shot every max(10/(0.02L+0.8)+1,2) frames max(0.0714L,1) Speed 30; size 0.4; life 30 frames
_big: PlasmaBullet01 Three bombs in a line per shot, 45 frames apart 4.5L per bomb Life 2000 frames; interpolated size, gravity, radius and speed

For _big, let t = clamp((L-10)/150,0,1). The source interpolation uses radius lerp(15,25.5,t), size lerp(8,13.6,t), speed lerp(0.45,0.5625,t) and gravity lerp(0.8,1.6,t). These fields should not be mistaken for a single explosive damage number.

Interpretation limit: A burst's number of bullets is not the number that hit the player. The drone AI's choice to begin a burst was outside the cited trace.

Imperial drones (UfoSmall515): battery rows and difficulty-sensitive damage

Imperial drones instead read battery_config: per-muzzle rows with a bullet key, shot count, interval, damage source and sound information. The observed level factor for their bullet damage is:

F(v) = 1 + (v - 1) × 0.5, where v=L/10.

The source can select a difficulty-indexed base-damage row before applying F(v), so level and difficulty are different inputs.

Variant Base damage before F(v) Pattern / important distinction
e515_imperialufo 5.5 Six shots 12 frames apart; interval can shorten with level, bounded below by three frames; one projectile per shot
_ace 2.0 128 shots 30 frames apart; 16 bullets per shot, spread 0.1; right battery delayed 15 frames; interval remains fixed in observed row
_prototype [5.5,5.5,11,16.5] by difficulty Six shots three frames apart; speed 2; life 120; blast radius 2.25

For the prototype, the source's difficulty lookup is not interchangeable with L: Easy and Normal use the first damage value in the cited selection rule, while Hard/Hardest/Inferno use the corresponding later values. The exact row-index logic is explained in the source appendix.

Deroys (Deiroi): the hull and legs use different curves

The main body and its leg batteries are separate consumers of the SGO attack configuration:

Weapon Derived per-bullet / per-missile damage Evidence boundary
Main-body PlasmaBullet01 1.5L + 20 Body's deiroi_battery_config; base SGO item 3 = 20 in examined variants
Leg beam (PulseBullet01) 1.4 × v, where v=L/10 Leg-part row, static inference from level setup
Leg missile (EntityMissileBody) 10 × v Leg-part row, with missile durability also 10 × v through a separate native scaling function

At L=10, these raw formulas yield 35 for the main plasma shot, 1.4 for a leg beam bullet and 10 for a leg missile. These numbers are not necessarily observed HP loss after game-mode and collision/damage routing.

The binary also reads deiroi_fire_config to determine level-sensitive leg fire timing. That timer path was not fully traced, so no definitive Deroy attacks-per-second curve is claimed here.

Model for interpreting enemy configs

  1. Identify the actual native enemy class and SGO row; a shared visible mesh does not guarantee shared attack code.
  2. Distinguish raw level L from normalized v=L/10 and from the difficulty-selected table value.
  3. Resolve how many projectiles a fire call creates and how they spread.
  4. Only then consider online damage and collision filters.
