Where a follower goes

EDF4.1 follow-state guard fallback

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-spot

The follow state (SoldierBase slot 58 2B5920, phase 1) drops to guard (thunk 2BB5F8) when the leader +0x2C0 is gone, asks for engage (2BB604) while the target +0x14A0 lives, and calls move-and-look 2B48E0(soldier, 1.0, 1.0, 0.05, &anchor +0x1410, &offset +0x1440, &look) at 2B5BDD (1.0 = AB6644, 0.05 = AB6498). The move target is anchor + offset in x and z. The look point is anchor + +0x1460 in x and z, with y = the soldier's own y + +0x1464. No ChaseLeader bit is tested, as in EDF5 (../edf5/squad-follow.md#edf5-follow-spot).

Basis: the 2B5920 dump; constants with flt.py

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2B5920, 2BB5F8, 2BB604, 2B48E0, 2B5BDD, AB6644, AB6498

Follow-state speed threshold and movement

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-look-mirror

In the follow state, when the soldier's horizontal speed squared (+0xC50 x and z) is at least the square of +0xE98 and the look point lies behind its way to the spot (dot < 0), the look point is mirrored forward, L - 2(L.d)d with d the unnormalized way (2.0 AB6700; 2B5B1F..2B5BAE), as in EDF5 (#edf5-follow-look-mirror, there +0xE30 / +0x10A8). The voice line 走り出す: the leader's step +0x1400 (x, z) squared over 0.0064 (AB6430) counts +0x14CC up; at 90 frames with the cooldown +0x14C8 at 0 it sets the cooldown to 300 + rand(600) and plays, when a leader up the chain has +0x1A8 set (#edf41-npc-talk-triggers).

Basis: the 2B5920 dump; constants with flt.py

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): AB6700, 2B5B1F, 2B5BAE, AB6430

Leader position anchor updated each frame

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-anchor

The anchor: with a leader the AI update 2B3C30 writes +0x1410 = the leader's position +0x90 every frame (2B3F02..2B3F0A; the store is the 7 bytes 0F 11 87 10 14 00 00 at 2B3F0A), with no condition, as in EDF5 (#edf5-follow-anchor); +0x151C = whether the leader has a route (+0x270 set); +0x1400 = the leader's previous position +0x13F0 minus the current, then +0x13F0 = the current (2B3F70..2B3FD5); without a leader +0x1400 is zeroed (2B3EE6). A route point replaces the anchor and zeroes both offsets (2B4354..2B4384, #edf41-route-watchdog).

Basis: the 2B3C30 dump; novae (ajane)

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2B3C30, 2B3F02, 2B3F0A, 2B3F70, 2B3FD5, 2B3EE6, 2B4354, 2B4384

Attribution: KH chat 183 (autorun) · 2026-11-14

The steering: a virtual stick, no path controller

EDF4.1 direct movement versus EDF5 path controller

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-steering

Unlike EDF5, whose move-and-look hands the point to a path controller (#edf5-follow-spot, [soldier+0x690]), 4.1's 2B48E0 steers directly: it takes the way from the soldier (for a remote copy, +0xC8 bit 0, its received position +0x1020 when +0x1010 is set) to anchor + offset in x and z, returns the distance minus the first float (1.0, the arrive radius, inferred) and, when that is above 0 (or a route is active), writes a move vector to the AI's stick block: +0x970 (x) and +0x978 (z), scaled to a speed (the route's +0x278 on a route; the speed pick 2B4EE2..2B50F8 not read). It always writes the look: +0x980 / +0x984 = the pitch and yaw errors toward the look point (against +0xCB0 / +0xCB4, wrapped by FA3E0) x the third float (0.05). The AI update runs the stick block through 299C60 (+0x970) at its start.

Basis: fn.py reads of 2B48E0 (head, 2B4AC7..2B4D60, 2B50F0..2B5245, --writes)

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2B48E0, 2B4EE2, 2B50F8, FA3E0, 299C60

Follower obstacle detection and contact normals

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-obstacle

Obstacles in 2B48E0: while the soldier touches something (+0x510) whose normal +0x530 faces against the way (dot < 0), every 60 frames (+0x1494) it sweeps a box from its position (y raised by AB671C) 3 m (AB6744) toward the spot through its collision object [+0x1498] (5FC970, 5FBEE0); when nothing is hit it sets +0x997 = 1 (a jump, inferred) and clears the timer +0x1470; otherwise a side-step kept in +0x1480 / +0x1490 for 60 frames (+0x1470; not read in detail).

Basis: fn.py read of 2B4AC7..2B4D60; --writes over the rest

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2B48E0, AB671C, AB6744, 5FC970, 5FBEE0

The offset: picked at random, in world space

SetFollow formation offset initialization

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-join-offset

Joining: SetFollow B31A0 calls slot 14 2B2B90(self, leader, keepOffset), which sets the base offset +0x1430 = self minus leader with y = 0 (where the soldier stood) and +0x151D = 0; without keepOffset it sets the re-pick timer +0x1420 = 300 + rand(900) frames; with it, 271220 lengthens the offset to at least the leader's +0x2D8 and copies it to +0x1440 (EDF5's 152490 only shortens, #edf5-follow-join-offset). On a walk-up join (#edf41-recruit-near-player) the 2 + rand x 3 m written to +0x1440 (2B3E26..2B3E80) is overwritten in the same frame by the copy from +0x1430 (2B4171, since +0x151D = 0): the follower keeps the spot where it stood (up to 10 m), as in EDF5. RecruitObject (3112) also passes keepOffset 0 (#edf41-friend-natives-join-flag).

Basis: fn.py reads of 2B2B90, 271220, B31A0; the flow in the 2B3C30 dump

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): B31A0, 2B2B90, 271220, 2B3E26, 2B3E80, 2B4171

EDF4.1 follow offset re-pick countdown

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-offset-pick

Re-picking, inline in the update (EDF5 calls 30CF30, #edf5-follow-offset-pick): +0x1420 counts down and at 0 (2B3FDC..2B40C7) sets +0x151D = 1, +0x1420 = 900 + rand(900) frames (15 to 30 s at 60 fps), +0x1430 = a random direction (51B7C0 around (0, 1, 0) AB7890, half-angle π AB674C) with y = 0, set (E5A60) to length +0x1510 + u x (+0x1514 - +0x1510), u = rand(10000) x 0.00010001 (AB63D8), at least 0; SetUp writes 3.0 and 10.0 there (#edf41-squad-offsets). Then 2B8150(self, 0) sends the pick online (#edf41-ai-sync-message). No FixAiPosition test and no seed field as in EDF5; the leader's facing is used nowhere: a world-space offset.

Basis: the 2B3C30 dump; fn.py read of E5A60; constants with flt.py

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2B3FDC, 2B40C7, 51B7C0, AB7890, AB674C, E5A60, AB63D8, 2B8150

Scaled follow offset in AI update

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-offset-scale

Each frame the used offset is formed (2B40D5..2B4193): once +0x151D is set, +0x1440 = +0x1430 x s / +0x1514 (1.0 AB6644 / 10), where s = (for a soldier with +0x2A bit 3) +0x2DC if >= 0, else +0x2E0 if >= 0, else 10.0 (AB6790), and 10.0 without the bit; else a plain copy. Then, with a leader, 271220 lengthens a nonzero offset shorter than the leader's +0x2D8 to that length. EDF5 reads a per-object field +0x3BC (10.0) where 4.1 has the constant (#edf5-follow-offset-scale).

Basis: the 2B3C30 dump; fn.py read of 271220

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2B40D5, 2B4193, AB6644, AB6790, 271220

Follower setup scale and distance fields

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-formation-radius

The scale fields: the object setup 28CBD0 writes +0x2D8 = 0, +0x2DC = +0x2E0 = -1.0 (28CEAC..28CEBC), then reads game_object_formation (9FEB00) into +0x2D8 only (28D062; EDF5 also reads a second value, #edf5-follow-formation-radius). Of 233 object SGOs only the Walking Fortress Balam (vehicle501_fortressrobo, _ai) carries it, [50.0]. The base AI step writes +0x2DC = the route's +0x20 (-1 without one) and then copies +0x2DC / +0x2E0 from the leader (28DACF..28DB05). Native 3110 (case 14BCC3, low bits 0x26) +0x2E0 = a float (14BD1F); 18 scripts call it on squad leaders right after 1011 or a route (3101), with 15, 20 or 30 m or a variable (M119, mp002, the packs' dm101, m203 and m990 ...).

Basis: fn.py --writes --grep over 1000..600000 for +0x2D8..+0x2E0; --xref 9FEB00; kh156_natcase41.py; obj_keys_g.py edf41; find_call.py edf41 3110

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 28CBD0, 28CEAC, 28CEBC, 9FEB00, 28D062, 28DACF, 28DB05, 14BCC3, 14BD1F

Spread behavior of scripted mission squadsSource: unverified

Evidence: unverified · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-spread-effect

What the spread does in missions: a soldier with +0x2A bit 3 in a scripted squad whose leader got 3110 with 30 m keeps 9 to 30 m from it (3 to 10 m x 30 / 10), and the two ranger squads of M67 Battle of Giants (M71 online, mp004) that the JP wiki says follow a Balam keep at least 50 m from it (the Balam's +0x2D8, via 271220). A soldier following a player gets the player's -1 values, so s = 10 and 3 to 10 m. Which objects set +0x2A bit 3 is not read (00u (a)). Inferred from the lines above.

Basis: reasoning over #edf41-follow-offset-scale and #edf41-follow-formation-radius; the wiki note in catalog/missions-wiki.md

The look offset +0x1460 is re-picked inline when +0x1450 runs out (2B4198..2B42FA

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-look-pick

The look offset +0x1460 is re-picked inline when +0x1450 runs out (2B4198..2B42FA; EDF5 30D150, #edf5-follow-look-pick): timer = 150 + rand(450) frames; offset = a random direction x 10 (AB6790) + the used offset normalized x 3.5 (AB6754), set to length 10, y = abs(y) x 0.1 (AB64C8); then 2B8150(self, 1). While it points away from the follow offset (dot < 0) the timer runs three times as fast (2B4332).

Basis: the 2B3C30 dump; constants with flt.py

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2B4198, 2B42FA, AB6790, AB6754, AB64C8, 2B8150, 2B4332

Hypothesis: EDF4.1 followers moving ahead of stationary leadersSource: unverified

Evidence: unverified · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-ahead-cause

Why followers run ahead of a player standing still: the same cause as EDF5 and EDF6 (#edf5-follow-ahead-cause), the spot being the player's position plus a random world-space offset of 3 to 10 m, re-drawn every 15 to 30 s and never turned with the player. Inferred from the code.

Orders: none from the player, no stay state

No player act unlinks a follower: the callers of Unfollow 28EF10 are SetFollow, 28D520 and 28D6B0 (not read

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-no-orders

No player act unlinks a follower: the callers of Unfollow 28EF10 are SetFollow, 28D520 and 28D6B0 (not read; release paths, inferred), the leader's death in the update (2B3F38) and the network receiver 2B6EE0 (2B6FCD). SetFollow's callers are the friend natives 1011 and 3112 (#edf41-friend-natives-join-flag), the update's recruiting (2B3D67), 2B6EE0 (2B6FC3) and B7680, the handler of the debug console command friend_squad (registered at C1009, name 9B9D78), which casts the new object, sets its +0x2B8 = 1 and makes it follow. No ChaseLeader bit: guard goes to follow whenever a leader is set (#edf41-soldier-ai-states), so there is no ready-made stay state, as in EDF5 (#edf5-follow-no-orders).

Basis: fn.py --xref of 28EF10, B31A0, B7680; --str 9B9D78

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 28EF10, 28D520, 28D6B0, 2B3F38, 2B6EE0, 2B6FCD, 2B3D67, 2B6FC3, B7680, C1009, 9B9D78

Route and open questions

Untested follower formation mod pathSource: unverified

Evidence: unverified · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-follow-route

Route for a follower mod (untested): (a) formation behind the player: rotate the used offset where the update stores it (2B417F, before the lengthen at 2B4193) into the leader's frame (4.1's leader yaw field not read), or limit the draw at 2B4051 to the rear half; keep hysteresis. (b) Stay: no flag exists, so skip the anchor store at 2B3F0A (7 bytes) for flagged soldiers in a hook (keeps the squad and the HUD bar; the soldier holds around the leader's last spot), or Unfollow 28EF10 with +0x2B8 = 0 (else it re-joins within 10 m) and SetFollow B31A0(self, player, 0) to resume. (c) Online: SetFollow needs slot 23 (2B76A0) to sync; an anchor hook is local to the AI's owner. Open: the speed pick in 2B48E0, +0xE98, +0x510 / +0x530, 28D520 / 28D6B0, who sets +0x2A bit 3.

Basis: summary of this file

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2B417F, 2B4193, 2B4051, 2B3F0A, 28EF10, B31A0, 2B76A0, 2B48E0
