EDF6 compressed-mesh mass and triangleIsInterior: measured data versus unresolved semantics

EDF 6Game technical referencesPhysics / uncertainties
Technical reference · Source analysis only (unless a source fact is contributor-verified); no new EDF runtime test

The hknpCompressedMeshShape source census contains mass properties, a mesh domain and triangleIsInterior bit information. These can be extracted exactly; their physical purpose is not equally well established.

Mass properties. The examined source describes the domain bounding box as the basis of its stored volume and center. Its inertial terms correspond to 1.5× those of an equivalent solid box, using a formula of the form 1.5 × m × (b²+c²)/12 for a principal axis. The body's configured mass and the mass field of a compressed shape agree throughout the 82 convex-kind meshes, but only in 86 of 214 triangle-kind meshes. Therefore one cannot infer every body's active mass from that shape's mass property alone. The corpus also contains special source-specific density/mass selections.

Interior triangles. In the triangle-kind data, 112,419 of 372,156 possible keys are marked interior. The researchers found that flagged triangles do not have open edges in the surveyed files, but no tested combination of edge-closure, planarity, convexity, concavity or quad-half conditions reproduced the bit pattern well. The field was attributed to Havok authoring tools; its exact semantic rule remains unresolved. Do not replace it with a guessed geometry test and claim native identity.

These distinctions matter for reasoning about static geometry and possible collision behavior, but the wiki does not claim that changing such fields has been qualified through an actual EDF6 runtime test.
