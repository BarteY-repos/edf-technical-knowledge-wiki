What this source adds

This section incorporates public-reuse-authorized technical research, mainly from novae (ajane)'s EDF Knowledge Hub snapshot dated 14 November 2026, source revision 54f89cd. It is integrated into the larger EDF4.1/5/6 documentation rather than treated as a replacement for the existing wiki. Original contributors beyond novae are attributed on individual findings.

Research source ≠ freshly verified engine behavior. The contributor defined [verified] as reported in-game observation, [static] as binary/source analysis, and [unverified] as a hypothesis. Those grades remain intact. Addresses require the matching binary build.

Publication provenance and permission boundaries

The project owner relayed publication permission for both the EDF Knowledge Hub material and the Japanese mod製作メモ.xlsx workbook on 15 November 2026. The reported permission concerns incorporating research into a larger shared technical knowledge base; the source author quotations include “permission granted if you want to incorporate the unique parts of it into a larger, shared knowledge base” and “It’s fine to publish this.” The quotations were reported by the user; no signed license or independently verified author identity for the workbook is held in this reader.

The Knowledge Hub snapshot is attributed to novae (ajane). Its individual notes frequently credit other people, community tools and code. These third-party references should be read as technical evidence with attribution, not as automatic permission to redistribute a separate person's original prose, code or media. The original Knowledge Hub ZIP, original workbook and their embedded images are not included in the public standalone reader. A public full research archive requires its own rights decision.

Exact source-coverage reconciliation (R153)

The original, re-opened EDF Knowledge Hub export 14-Nov-2026.zip was checked byte-for-byte against its previously recorded SHA-256. Its machine-readable source comprises 2,753 records: 2,572 fact IDs, 149 crosswalk statements, and 32 negative/dead-end records. The current public wiki contains 2,556 of the 2,572 exact fact IDs in their mapped source appendix articles, all 149 crosswalk texts, and all 32 reported failed approaches and their reasons.

The remaining 16 fact IDs belong to the retired kh-edf6-mod-platform appendix. They document the independent platform's patch parser, package ownership, loader behavior, modified-game qualification, and compatibility scope. These are extension implementation claims, not original Sandlot EDF behavior; their exact source text is preserved in the original community ZIP and a separate maintainer-only historical archive, rather than restored to the public game-centric reader. The source IDs are retained in reference/R153_KNOWLEDGE_HUB_ALL_2753_SOURCE_LINE_DISPOSITION.csv as explicitly excluded records.

Important limitation: source ID/text retention is an exact preservation check, not an independent re-validation of all 2,572 claims or a claim that every topic has been semantically reconciled against the latest native binaries. The contributor evidence grades and build boundaries remain in effect.

R179: contributor-level publication review

The publication audit now separates 2,556 source-linked research assertions from the article-level source count. Of these, 791 credit the Knowledge Hub compiler, 650 name internal curation or automated research references, and 1115 cite named outside contributors or separate source projects. The categories describe the first-listed source credit, not authorship, originality or legal ownership.

In 92 Knowledge Hub pages, at least one attribution is not compiler-only. The structural scan found no fenced code blocks or embedded images in those pages; nevertheless 203 longer descriptions (over 1,500 characters) that credit outside sources require a focused wording check. A credit to a third-party patch or project does not itself prove that the patch author's expression was copied. Conversely, a source citation or permission to incorporate research does not authorize unlicensed reproduction of a third party's original prose.

The full project preserves technical claims, build IDs, source-basis fields and authors' names. The automated scan does not prove the originality of particular passages. We subsequently located and pinned 20 original documents by hajisensai, providing source paths for 85 of the 203 high-priority explanations. A full literal-text or translation-expression comparison remains not performed. The priority register is a maintainer audit, not a separate publicly hosted source dump.
