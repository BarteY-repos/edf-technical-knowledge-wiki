EDF5 versus EDF6 DLC completion: MainScript result flow and ending cues

ConversionMissions & ScriptingMission results and DLC presentationEDF 5EDF 6
Technical reference · Source-derived; separates static evidence and the limited modded-runtime results

The last mission of a downloadable-content campaign may complete normally, set the correct mission result, and still omit the DLC-specific ending scene and audio. The retained EDF5/EDF6 MAINSCRIPT.AS comparison explains why this can occur when EDF5 missions are hosted in new EDF6 MissionPack IDs.

EDF5 source-side behavior

For the final mission of a DLC campaign, the recovered EDF5 MainScript uses a post-mission DLCEnding() path when GetContentID()!=0. Its named presentation resources are:

  • Ending UI: app:/ui/lyt_EndingDLC.sgo.
  • Music cue: BGM_E5S08_EndingTelopOnline.

This is a result-side/MainScript event, not simply a special enemy-death event placed inside the last mission's MISSION.AC or MISSION.BVM.

Why EDF6 behaves differently with new pack IDs

The examined stock EDF6 MainScript handles the recognized content identifiers 0, 1, and 2 after the last mission. When EDF5's DLC1 and DLC2 content is instead hosted as custom identifiers 4 and 5, those custom IDs do not select an equivalent DLC-ending branch in the native handler. The mission can still proceed to ordinary result processing. This is a cross-generation/custom-ID compatibility boundary, not a claim that official EDF6 DLC modes omit their own endings.

Distinguish final mission clear from DLC ending

Native MISSION/ASCOMMON.H exposes two distinct in-mission clear presentations:

Call Cue/layout Wait Result classification
MissionClear_EDF5() Jingle_MissionCleared, lyt_HUiMissionCleared.sgo 6 s MISSION_RESULT_CLEAR
FinalMissionClear_EDF5() Jingle_MissionClearedFinal, lyt_HUiMissionComplete.sgo 10 s MISSION_RESULT_CLEAR

Both terminate through internal_Exit(MISSION_RESULT_CLEAR); the latter differs in UI/audio/time but does not reproduce the separate EDF5 DLCEnding() MainScript path. The original inspected EDF5 DLC final-mission BVMs use ordinary MissionClear, so replacing it with FinalMissionClear_EDF5 would change the authored in-mission sequence rather than restore source fidelity.

Presentation resource checks

The lyt_EndingDLC.sgo UI layout is present in the examined EDF6 resource corpus. However, the retained archive did not contain the target audio-bank ACB bytes needed to prove that EDF6 can actually resolve BGM_E5S08_EndingTelopOnline at runtime. Presence of a named cue in source text is not evidence that the target audio bank implements it.

A source-preserving custom MainScript branch for custom content IDs 4/5 was statically prepared in the research. This page does not present that as stock behavior or as a fully tested ending. A global MainScript override may also have compatibility effects unrelated to DLC endings.
