Air Raider drones: formation, movement and attack-order SGO fields

ConversionCommunity Japanese WorkbookGameplay & AIEDF 5EDF 6
Technical reference · Translated contributed workbook notes; sample data, negative experiments and explicitly labeled inferences

Resource structure

The contributed Japanese workbook analyzes a portable bombing drone whose object resource string is app:/object/pd605_Drone_grenade.sgo. It transcribes a custom_parameter tree with drone, formation, movement and order sections. The worked example is not an exhaustive schema for all Air Raider drones or an assertion that every named field is consumed identically across EDF versions.

The source distinguishes a drone's waiting/follow formation from attack-order area placement. Do not combine the two offset lists into one coordinate system.

Formation / idle-follow data

formation holds a list of per-drone offset vectors, rather than a single offset for the whole swarm. The author notes an example with 24 offsets for a 24-drone formation, and describes the waiting drones as following the Air Raider.

For the excerpted first formation member, the source records the triple [1, 2, 1] (stored as doubles). The exact axis convention of this idle-offset triple is not proven by these cells; do not assume the attack-area axis descriptions below automatically transfer to it.

Source: 解析!B893:K925, especially B901, B905:K923.

Attack-order fields and directions

The sample attack order contains type=4, trigger=1, a flight/attack offset, radius=5, delay with min=0 and max=60, an area list and is_end_to_suicide=0.

The author's directional annotations distinguish two different offsets:

  • Aircraft approach offset relative to a warning zone (order.offset): sample [0, 18, -9], described as left/right, height and depth. The annotated sign convention is positive = left, positive height = up, positive depth = farther inward (解析!B1009:K1016).
  • Individual attack area offset relative to a specified strike point (order.area[*].offset): sample [40, 0, 0]. The author again describes positive x as left, positive depth as farther inward, positive height as up; a height change can shift landing points because mounted weapons aim at an offset altitude (解析!B1029:K1038).

For each listed attack area, angle=0 changes the fan orientation when modified; a positive change was reported to rotate left. distance=250 specifies strike-range length in this example, apparently influencing aircraft motion along with projectile count. This is author-observed behavior, not a verified equation for aircraft speed. See 解析!B1040:K1047.

Safe editing rules

  • Preserve the number and pairing of formation entries when changing the drone count; investigate producer/consumer bounds before adding hundreds of offsets.
  • Measure values after changing only one field, and separately observe waiting formation, flight to target, warning-zone geometry and strike position.
  • Do not assert the exact meaning of aim_target=4, type=4, trigger=1, equipment=1 or is_end_to_suicide=0 without exact-build runtime evidence.
  • Cross-game/multiplayer serialization of these fields remains an independent question.
