Havok compressed-mesh broadphase: tombstones, shape keys and float32 bounds

EDF 6Maps & NavigationCollision & Physics
Technical reference · Native NW/HEIGEN MAD structural oracle; no fresh in-game test

The MAD/Havok collision pipeline stores compressed mesh primitives, keys and SIMD broadphase bounds. A reverse-engineered validator must distinguish real invalid geometry from unused primitive slots and unavoidable float32 rounding. Otherwise it can falsely condemn an unmodified native EDF6 map.

0xDEADDEAD primitive rows are inactive markers

In the native NW_EUROPE01 collision data, an older validator expected every 4-byte hkcdStaticMeshTree::Primitive row to produce a reachable BVH leaf key. This included rows whose four bytes are:

DE AD DE AD

These represent inactive primitive/tombstone entries, not reachable geometry. In the checked native file, seven occurred in compressed-mesh item 656 and three in item 1054. Their keys were correctly absent from reachable SIMD leaves.

Practical rule: when building the expected-key set, recognize these inactive rows. Do not create synthetic faces or change the native map simply to make the count equal.

Why a fixed bound tolerance can be wrong

Compressed vertices and SIMD bounding boxes may be reconstructed at different numerical precision. The original validator compared them using a global absolute bound tolerance 1e-4 (0.0001), regardless of map scale.

In the native large NW field mesh, the worst apparent bound underflow was approximately 0.00023284967483050423. Yet one float32 ULP at the relevant maximum coordinate magnitude was 0.000244140625—already larger than the observed difference. Calling the native shape corrupt in that case confuses representational uncertainty with a meaningful geometric error.

For each tested axis, the corrected validator used:

allowed_bound_error = max(
    1e-4,
    float32_ULP(max(abs(min), abs(max), abs(max - min)))
)

Here ULP means the distance between neighboring float32 representable numbers at the chosen magnitude. This is a rounding budget for structural validation, not permission to move vertices or relax collision geometry arbitrarily.

The most adverse measured HEIGEN bound difference was roughly 0.00006373971700668335. To check that the new test was not simply too permissive, a leaf's minimum X value was deliberately shifted inward by +0.01 in a read-only in-memory negative test. The checker flagged one genuine violation.

What this does not establish

A correct broadphase test means the analyzed collision structure is internally consistent under this particular validator. It does not alone prove that the map loads, that dynamic debris behaves correctly, that AI will navigate it, or that the game accepts any arbitrary authored Havok shape.

The underlying failure mode is a validator mistake, not a license to replace bad collision with placeholder data. Do not mistake other special key encodings, such as a 0xFFFFFFFF shape-key sentinel, for the same DE AD DE AD primitive tombstone. See compressed-mesh shape key and section formats.

Evidence boundary

This explanation comes from a bounded audit of native NW_EUROPE01 and HEIGEN507 assets plus specific authored control MAD files. The checker was validated offline, including a deliberate negative test. No new EDF6 runtime collision or gameplay test is implied.
