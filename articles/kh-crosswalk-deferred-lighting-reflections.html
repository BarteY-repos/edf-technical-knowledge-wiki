Deferred-lighting compute shader and where reflections come from (key: the shader's G-buffer bindings g_albedoRGB, g_normalXYZ, g_light_accumulate; RTTI LocalCubeMap@ren@xgl in EDF5)

  • edf41: three variants in EDF41.exe, reflections from one global environment cubemap, no local cubemaps, no SSR | ref: edf41/rendering.md#lighting-cs-global-cubemap | evidence: static
  • edf5: three variants in EDF5.exe, reflections from a grid of local cubemaps; an unreferenced, complete SSR shader set ships in the exe; water and glass are forward shaders on the global cubemap; windows are interior-mapped G-buffer shaders | ref: edf5/shaders.md#deferred-lighting-cs, #dormant-ssr-shaders, #water-glass-forward, #window-interior-mapping | evidence: static
  • edf6: three variants in EDF.dll, local cubemap grid as in EDF5, no SSR shaders left | ref: edf6/rendering.md#lighting-cs-local-cubemaps | evidence: static
  • Differences noticed: EDF 4.1 uses one global cubemap and also outputs view Z; EDF5 adds the local cubemap grid (and the cut SSR set); EDF6 keeps the grid, drops g_smooth_reflection_specpow from the G-buffer list and the SSR set, and adds brightness fog to xgl_system. All three were found by the same reflection-script read of every DXBC blob (2026-11-03).
  • Confidence in the mapping: medium (same pass by binding names in all three; code paths traced only for EDF5's creation in 5D6BD0)
  • Added: 2026-11-03 src: novae (ajane)

Base color of the same-named material shaders (key: SND_MECH.SGO, SND_CHARA_2COLORCHANGE.SGO, technique Deferred)

  • edf41: o0.rgb = b, 4 targets, reflect term to o3.y | ref: edf41/rendering.md#edf41-material-base-color-output | evidence: static
  • edf5: o0.rgb = b, 6 targets | ref: edf5/shaders.md#edf5-material-base-color-output | evidence: static
  • edf6: o0.rgb = b + f * (1 - b), 5 targets, sqrt(f) to o3.x, roughness from the old specular power to o3.y | ref: edf6/rendering.md#material-shader-load-base-color | evidence: static
  • Differences noticed: a model ported from EDF 4.1 or EDF5 into EDF6 with its old snd_* shader names and reflect textures looks whiter, more so where reflect.R is high; the register meanings changed, so old shaders cannot be dropped into EDF6 as they are (output count, VS outputs such as VIEW_POSITION). No gamma step between albedo sampling and o0 was seen; texture sRGB flags, light combine, exposure and environment maps were not compared.
  • Confidence in the mapping: high (same file names, same technique, disassembled side by side)
  • Added: 2026-11-07 src: hajisensai, All Forces source docs/edf41-material-shader-contract.md (round 24)
