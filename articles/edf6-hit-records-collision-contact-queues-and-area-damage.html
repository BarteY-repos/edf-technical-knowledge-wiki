EDF6 hit records: contact-point vectors, direct-hit queues and blast damage

EDF 6Game technical referencesDamage and combat
Technical reference · Contributor static analysis / source-graded observations; no new game runtime in R124

A projectile collision, a GameDamageInfo record and an HP change are three different stages of EDF6 combat. The source identifies a native hit message (0x10000000) that carries an attacker handle, team, damage, impact position, damage flags and a vector of individual contact points with rigid-body identities. A broadphase intersection alone is not yet that message.

What the hit record contains

In the examined steam-678ccb46 build, key offsets of the 0x90-byte record are:

Record offset Meaning
+0x10/+0x18 Weak attacker object reference
+0x20 Hit type; type 4 forces an unconditional part rate
+0x24 Team, −1 means no team rule
+0x30 World-space impact position
+0x50 Base damage; negative can represent healing
+0x58 Blast amount/radius-related field in relevant paths
+0x60 Bitfield affecting forced hits, rate and special treatment
+0x68 Shield multiplier
+0x70 Contact-point vector; 0x30 bytes per contact

Each contact entry stores a world point and Havok body ID. The ID is local to that machine's physics world, not an online global identity. Bosses that only accept hits on one specific part can translate the body ID into a ragdoll body index and reject other contacts; checking only the collision position cannot reproduce that native rule.

Direct projectile hit sequence

In a shape-cast hit, EDF6 first obtains the world impact fraction, point, normal and body. It resolves the body to a SceneObject and, when eligible, records that object and its contact into the bullet's own direct-hit queue. A queue entry is keyed by target. A second collision against a target already queued merges the contact points and does not simply double the queued damage. The queue is settled synchronously in the relevant bullet update/collision path; it is not a universal all-frame deferred hit bus.

The bullet's hit record lives in the bullet core (core+0x730, in the source the core is the bullet at +0x140), whereas the queue is at core+0x6E0. Some explosion-only bullets clear the direct-hit queue and use a separate blast path. A render-only contact or nearest bounding-box candidate should never be counted as a confirmed gameplay hit.

Area damage is another query

GameDamageInfo::ApplyAreaDamage performs a region query around its position and radius. Depending on the caller, it uses a collision-layer filter, a team rule, optional ignored object and optional already-hit set. The area query produces candidate game objects; the damage path then queues hit events for those that qualify. Stock caller examples include boss deaths and bullet explosions. A visual Explosion_Big call does not, by itself, invoke this damage query; see visual explosions.

Query admission precedes damage-record construction. The independent November bullet-filter survey places the default bullet stopping ray at collision layer 12, its damage sphere cast at 11 and the studied area-damage query at 13. These paths have distinct partner-layer sets: a body that is not admitted by an area's broadphase cannot be made damageable merely by changing its GameDamageInfo rate, while a stopping-ray intersection is not automatically a direct damage hit. See the source-reconciled body-layer example, including the still-unresolved imported-panel direct-hit discrepancy, and the complete native filter-table reference.

Evidence boundary
