Gatling spin/recoil experiments

A Japanese community worksheet records the interval between start of firing and the weapon model/camera reaching straight up for two Fencer weapons. At the assumed 60 frames/second conversion used by the sheet:

Tested weapon Start frame Straight-up frame Difference Workbook conversion
Fireblast Gatling 614 1529 915 frames 15.25 s
Flame Revolver DC2 144 246 102 frames 1.70 s

These are observations from a particular experimental setup, not evidence that their native recoil parameters are expressed as a raw timer or that the game always runs at a fixed 60 FPS. The worksheet later comments that a more negative value in a sampled custom parameter increases the tendency to aim upward, and that setting another recoil-decay parameter to 1 could allow continuous upward drift after shooting (解析!Q664, Q670). Another field appears to govern spin-up and may interact with firing interval; the author warns that some combinations fail to fire (Q672). The underlying fields were not recovered as a complete validated enum.

Shield protection display values

The workbook compares a shield's resource scalar with the angle shown in its description:

Variant Raw scalar Displayed protection angle
Standard 0.175 20°
B 0.2625 30°
F 0.35 40°

Source: 解析!A858:E865, especially A860:D863.

The workbook calculates display_angle / raw ≈ 114.2857 for each row and states that 1.57 would show approximately 180°, suggesting a radian relationship. Important qualification: the ordinary radians-to-degrees factor is 180/π ≈ 57.2958, not 114.2857. The observed ratio is approximately twice that factor (360/π ≈ 114.5916) within the samples' rounding. Therefore a plausible editorial hypothesis is that the resource stores a half-angle in radians while the text shows a full protection cone. This is an inference from values, not a verified acos/clamp or engine angle implementation. Avoid labeling the field simply “degrees” or simply “radians” without noting the factor-of-two ambiguity.

For example, the author uses 0.7 for an experimental shield, yielding a calculated description of approximately 80° in the sheet (解析!A865:D865). Whether runtime protection boundaries exactly match that UI value was not tested here.

Validation procedure

  1. Verify whether the scalar changes displayed text only or also the actual shield collision/defense cone.
  2. Test left/right and vertical incoming projectiles at controlled angles, keeping the same weapon and game build.
  3. Check 60/120 FPS regimes separately before converting recoil frames into seconds or using a fixed-step assumption.
  4. Preserve the worksheet's uncertain field names; a negative recoil sign does not establish the formula for all Fencer weapons.

Source: mod製作メモ.xlsx, 解析!A649:Q675 and 解析!A858:E865. The half-angle explanation is labeled as new inference; original source measures remain unchanged.
