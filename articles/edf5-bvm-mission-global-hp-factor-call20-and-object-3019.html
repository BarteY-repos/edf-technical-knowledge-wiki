EDF5 script health scaling: custom calls 20 and 3019, per-wave HP and float evaluation

EDF 5Game technical referencesMissions / native damage and HP
Technical reference · EDF5 source static analysis; no new runtime qualification

EDF5 has a script-wide enemy factor and a per-object durability adjustment. Treating both as a single difficulty setting obscures native behavior, particularly when waves spawned later in a mission differ in strength.

cuscall0 20 takes a float and writes the current script enemy multiplier to the audited build's script field +0x414 (initialized to 1.0). Later native spawn calls multiply their own HP argument by this active factor. The source survey found 28 call sites in 14 shipping missions, often in difficulty branches: in M065 the observed Hard/Hardest/Inferno values include 0.9, 0.8, 0.75. This factor changes subsequent spawns; it does not itself establish retroactive modification of every already-created enemy.

cuscall0 3019 instead takes (object handle, percent). The recovered handler multiplies an object's max HP at +0x1F8 by percent / 100 and assigns its current HP at +0x1FC the minimum of its old current HP × factor and the new maximum. This is not simply “set remaining HP percent.” The 141-script source set has 54 calls across 14 scripts, sometimes used for friendly vehicles (e.g. M041's combat frames and railguns at 10%).

The final HP of an individual spawn may therefore depend on its own SGO baseline, the per-spawn argument, the active 20 factor and later 3019 operations, with float32 multiplication order affecting exact parity. The source survey found factors other than 1 in 65/141 missions; in 38 missions the same SGO receives different HP factors on the same difficulty depending on wave. M015 has ordinary black-ant groups with factor 1.0 and repeating teleport-ship waves with factor 0.7; M009's final two spider groups use factor 0.5.

Do not apply these offsets blindly to EDF6 structures. Also do not equate HP scalar and attack scalar without inspecting each native actor class; the available attribution to JP wiki discussions is narrower than a universal engine proof.

Evidence: native EDF5 executable and source-script static census bvm-call-20-hp-factor, bvm-spawn-hp-factors, bvm-call-3019-durability, bvm-eval-call-3019, bvm-switch-form; no new live damage measurement.
