At a glance

What this page answers: ktaro's six-sheet Japanese notebook contains community experiments on weapons, drops, stars, ant assets, effects and localization.

How to read it: Use the focused articles for the basics and this page for deeper cell-referenced examples, negative tests and reconciliation history.

Evidence boundary: The workbook is not Sandlot API documentation. Its figures and screenshots are not included in this offline release, and its observations were not universally rerun.

The source workbook mod製作メモ.xlsx is a community research notebook, not official Sandlot API documentation. Its six sheets cover analysis, item drop rates, weapon stars, ant models, EFS effects and weapon text translation. Earlier wiki pages summarize its major domains; these additions focus on previously unintegrated mechanics, negative experiments and exact source-cell trails.
