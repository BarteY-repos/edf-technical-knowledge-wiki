EDF6 Shield Bearers: barrier collision, teams and projectile stopping

EDF 6Game technical referencesCollision & Physics
Technical reference · Native-data and static code observations; no new in-game runtime test

A Shield Bearer barrier in the examined EDF6 build is a physical collision/query object, not merely a transparent visual effect. It also is not the same mechanism as a Hector's carried, breakable shield.

Native owners and layers

  • The relevant E513_SHIELDBEARER variants (_L, _XL, _FIXED*) are AlienTrailer objects.
  • Each includes an AlienTrailer_Barrier subobject. The source identifies two barrier layers of 0x680 bytes, one at owner +0x18C0 and one at +0x1F40 in this build.
  • A layer holds an Havok body and BarrierInfo. The examined body uses collision layer 14 and carries a key-2 reference to BarrierInfo. The barrier info includes the team, copied from the owner each frame.
  • The projectile collector considers a key-2 barrier when the BarrierInfo team is hostile to the projectile side. The subsequent contact handler stops the shot at the barrier surface, including rounds that otherwise pierce normal targets.

This is why a barrier can be visible yet not block every object equally: a collision layer, a team relation and the bullet's collision-handling path are all involved.

What the shield does not prove

Question What static evidence supports
Hostile projectile reaches barrier Eligible for barrier hit/stop handling
Piercing projectile The studied handler still stops it at barrier contact
Friendly projectile Hostility filter can exclude barrier interception
Vehicle hull enters barrier Stock vehicle collision blocking not established; the examined helicopter barrier query was gated on a field without a proven native writer
Shield is invulnerable to every possible attack Not proven by the interception routine alone

The source lists model radius families around S 50 m, L 100 m, LL 200 m and XL 470 m, but those are inferred from model data, not independently game-measured collision radii. Use them as approximate model context rather than hard-coded universal barrier spheres.

Distinguish other collision questions

A barrier hit is not the same as a direct bullet's stop/hit layer filtering or an explosion's area-damage query. See EDF6 projectile filters and direct versus area damage. A historical EDF4.1 mission incident involving a Shield Bearer and airstrike also needs its own mission-specific proof; the barrier mechanism alone does not establish the outcome.
