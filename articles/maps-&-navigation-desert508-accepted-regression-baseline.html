Earlier crash discriminator versus the later accepted MAD baseline

The separate R12 + source-derived Gate501 compound experiment used a MAD with SHA-256 42656061ca1e6af2dd17d66a3e21ec1c7cea455fe810a3667fa426b68c3d8d16. The tester reported four long runs without the previously reproducible 0xFFFFFFFF Havok shape-key crash (EDF+0x15B2B02). This is a strong bounded runtime positive for that earlier candidate and reproducer, not universal crash freedom and not the same byte identity as the later accepted MAD described above. The accepted baseline has a separate SHA-256 and its own tests. Retain both stages rather than rewriting the R12 hash into the final MAD identity.
