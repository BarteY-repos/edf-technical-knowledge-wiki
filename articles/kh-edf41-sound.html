EDF 4.1 — Sound (community source notes)

EDF 4.1Community Knowledge HubAudio
Source-backed research / case study · Contributed research; per-claim static, verified, unverified; not independently re-tested

This is a source-attributed research appendix, not a chronology or a replacement for topic reference pages. The observations were contributed in the EDF Knowledge Hub export, snapshot 2026-11-14, revision 54f89cd, compiled by novae (ajane).

Read each statement with its evidence grade and exact build. Verified means a reported game test, static means reverse engineering or source inspection without a reported game test, and unverified is a hypothesis. None of these observations was newly tested for this wiki release.

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-sound-controller

[CC8508] is the sound controller: its player at +8 plays music (current music handle at +0x20 / +0x28), its player at +0x78 plays voices, radio and some menu cues (handle +0x90). The shared play 3E46E0(player, narrow cue, volume, float, handle) releases the handle and returns 0 when the player is not ready ([player+8] 0); otherwise it looks the cue up by name in the player's first bank ([player]+0x58, 807C), then in its second (+0x70, 3E2D40, the id gets bit 16), and plays it through 3E3C50 on [player]+0x48. 17 call sites: the 2 music entries (#edf41-bgm-play, #edf41-bgm-crossfade-stop) pass +8, the other 15 +0x78.

Basis: fn.py read of 3E46E0 and of the argument setup at each of its 17 callers (--xref 3E46E0)

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): CC8508, 3E46E0, 807C, 3E2D40, 3E3C50

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-console-sound-commands

The dev console registers its sound commands in C07C0 (command table at [CC8518]+0x440, as 56C1E0 calls): bgm (handler BB840), cross_fade_bgm (BB930), stop_bgm (BBA20), preset_se (twice), se (twice), stop_se, voice (BBB10, plays on +0x78), and the talk debug commands disp_talk_id, talk_idx, talk, talk_condition, talk_progress, talk_pause. There is no seek_bgm (EDF5 has one).

Basis: fn.py read of C07C0 with wide strings resolved (scratch kh149_fnstr.py edf41)

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): C07C0, CC8518, 56C1E0, BB840, BB930, BBA20, BBB10, C1C25, 9BA1D8

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-bgm-play

Music start: 3E7C10(controller [CC8508], narrow cue) is SoundController::PlayBgm (log string A25F08, "SoundController::PlayBgm::%s %f"): it takes a volume from 3E7D40 and plays through 3E46E0(controller+8, cue, volume, 1.0 [AB6644], controller+0x20). Its three callers: the console bgm (BB840), mission script (BVM) native 300 (interpreter case 14BEE7, 14EFD0), and the AngelScript void PlayBgm(const string &in) of the menu and system scripts (347470, registered at 3453F8, declaration A0BB60; its wide argument is made narrow by 518CA0).

Basis: fn.py reads of 3E7C10, its three callers (--xref 3E7C10) and the registration block; native number from the interpreter's tables (scratch kh156_natcase41.py: low 7 bits 0x2C)

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 3E7C10, CC8508, A25F08, 3E7D40, 3E46E0, AB6644, BB840, 14BEE7, 14EFD0, 347470, 3453F8, A0BB60, 518CA0

Music cross-fade and stop: 3E7C80(controller, cue, frames) fades the playing music out over the frames (3E2EC…

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-bgm-crossfade-stop

Music cross-fade and stop: 3E7C80(controller, cue, frames) fades the playing music out over the frames (3E2EC0 on [controller+0x28]), releases +0x20 (1675C0), plays the new cue through 3E46E0 on +8 and sets its fade-in over frames x 1/60 (AB6460). Callers: the console cross_fade_bgm (BB930) and native 302 (case 14BF34, 14F0A0, a cue and a seconds float). Native 301 (case 14BEF8) fades out inline: 3E2EC0([controller+0x28], seconds x 60 [AB6840]), then 1675C0(controller+0x20); the console stop_bgm (BBA20) does the same with a frame count. AngelScript also has StopBgm() (347530, A0BB50) and StopBgmFadeout(int tm) (A0BB30).

Basis: fn.py reads of 3E7C80, BBA20 and the interpreter cases (low 7 bits 0x2D, 0x2E); the seconds-to-frames step inside 14F0A0 not read

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 3E7C80, 3E2EC0, 1675C0, AB6460, BB930, 14BF34, 14F0A0, 14BEF8, AB6840, BBA20, 347530, A0BB50, A0BB30

For a jukebox: every music start passes 3E7C10 or 3E7C80 with the cue as a narrow string in rdx

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-bgm-jukebox-hooks

For a jukebox: every music start passes 3E7C10 or 3E7C80 with the cue as a narrow string in rdx; hooking those two sees all of them (mission, menu, console, cross-fades). The shared 3E46E0 also serves voices, so a hook there must test rcx = [CC8508]+8. No seek play exists in 4.1. The scripts use the natives as EDF5 does (300 PlayBGM, 301 FadeOutBGM, 302 cross-fade): 300 at 594 sites in 147 scripts (147 of them Jingle_MissionCleared), 301 at 442, 302 at 135 sites in 85 scripts, with cues such as BGM_E4M01_MainBGM_A, BGM_E4M05_Totsugeki, BGM_E4M08_EarthEater.

Basis: callers above; script counts with scratch kh155_bvm_sound.py edf41 2C 2D 2E AC AD AE 2F AF (File_Storage/Logs/catalog/kh156_bvm_sound_edf41.txt; no call with low byte 0x2F)

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 3E7C10, 3E7C80, 3E46E0, CC8508

NPC voice lines, the pick: 2C3720(TalkManager, out, object, table name, .., seed) is EDF5's 325820 (../edf5/s…

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-talk-line-pick

NPC voice lines, the pick: 2C3720(TalkManager, out, object, table name, .., seed) is EDF5's 325820 (../edf5/sound.md#edf5-talk-line-pick) at other offsets: it finds the table in the object's talk SGO (+0x228, 51AD10) and makes a weighted random pick (a value in 0..9999 / 10000 times the summed weights, entry element 2). An entry is skipped when its element 3 is 0 or more and the byte TalkManager+0x39+[object+0x230] has none of its bits, or when the float TalkManager+0x3C is outside [element 11, element 12]. A table whose element 2 is 1 draws from TalkManager's generator +0x48, else from the seed, which 2C3090 draws from the global generator [C64A08]. Weight 0 never plays: the data route to silence a line (untested).

Basis: fn.py read of 2C3720; element numbers from the 12-byte SGO value offsets (+0x20, +0x2C, +0x8C, +0x98)

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2C3720, 51AD10, 2C3090, C64A08

Queuing a line: RandomTalk (soldier-ai.mdedf41-npc-talk-lines) plays through 2C2A90(TalkManager, object, tabl…

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-talk-line-queue

Queuing a line: RandomTalk (soldier-ai.md#edf41-npc-talk-lines) plays through 2C2A90(TalkManager, object, table index), which picks a channel with 2C2FF0 from the table's element 1 float, builds the line in 2C3090 (pick, cue name, delay) and inserts it (2C72B0). The delay in frames is (a random 0..9999 / 10000 x (element 5 - element 4) + element 4) x 60 plus a float argument, at least 1, so entry elements 4 and 5 are a delay range in seconds (EDF5's same elements: ../edf5/sound.md#edf5-talk-entry-fields).

Basis: fn.py reads of 2C2A90 and 2C3090 (the delay math at 2C329F..2C3363)

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2C2A90, 2C2FF0, 2C3090, 2C72B0

Who fills a talk cue's %s (open in soldier-ai.mdedf41-talk-sgo-tables): 2C3960 replaces %s in entry element 0…

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-talk-voice-variant

Who fills a talk cue's %s (open in soldier-ai.md#edf41-talk-sgo-tables): 2C3960 replaces %s in entry element 0 with a letter from the table A9F070 (A to H) indexed by the speaker's +0x234. Object setup (28D465) sets +0x234 to a random index below element 3 of the object SGO's game_object_talk_setting (from the object's own generator +0x210), so each NPC keeps one voice actor; element 1 goes to +0x238 (a speaker kind) and element 2 to +0x230 (the mask group the pick reads). So U001E%s names up to six cues, U001EA..U001EF, one per voice; silencing a line means every letter.

Basis: fn.py reads of 2C3960 and 28D3F0..28D465; the table's eight pointers read from the exe (image base 140000000)

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2C3960, A9F070, 28D465

A talk line reaches the audio in 2C4730(channel, ..), TalkManager's channel update: the line's frame countdow…

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-talk-line-start

A talk line reaches the audio in 2C4730(channel, ..), TalkManager's channel update: the line's frame countdown +0x10 runs out, its cue (+0x28, wide) is made narrow (518CA0) and played on [CC8508]+0x78: 3E47E0 with a position from the speaker's matrix +0x90 and two floats 4.0 and 400.0 (inferred: near and far distances) when +0x74 is 0, else 3E46E0 (unplaced); handle +0x20. It logs "TalkManager(再生できない:%s)" ("cannot play", A03B70) when the playback's status is 3, and sends the line's animation (+0x48) to the speaker as message 0x80000014 (inferred from the vtable calls). One hook at the two plays (2C488B, 2C48C8) sees every NPC talk cue by name: the code route to silence lines (untested).

Basis: fn.py read of 2C4730 (3E47E0's only caller)

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2C4730, 518CA0, CC8508, 3E47E0, 3E46E0, A03B70, 2C488B, 2C48C8

Mission radio voices: BVM native 4000 (case 14BB09, low 7 bits 0x20) calls 155660(script, cue), which checks…

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-radio-voice-native

Mission radio voices: BVM native 4000 (case 14BB09, low 7 bits 0x20) calls 155660(script, cue), which checks TalkManager [CC84F0] (its playback at +0x30 and two channel floats against AB69A4; returning early otherwise, inferred: it waits for the previous line) and plays the cue through 2C2520, which stops the previous radio voice (+0x28) and plays the new one unplaced on [CC8508]+0x78 at volume 1. The scripts call 4000 at 902 sites in 147 scripts: MusenBegin and MusenEnd 147 times each (the radio click), the rest story cues such as E013_14_E, E115_016_S.

Basis: fn.py reads of the interpreter case, 155660 (first 40 lines) and 2C2520; script counts with scratch kh155_bvm_sound.py edf41 20 A0

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 14BB09, 155660, CC84F0, AB69A4, 2C2520, CC8508
