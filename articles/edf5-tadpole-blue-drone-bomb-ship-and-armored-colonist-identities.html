EDF5 Tadpoles, blue drones, bomb ships and armored Colonist SGOs

EDF 5Game technical referencesEnemies / identification
Technical reference · Source analysis only (unless a source fact is contributor-verified); no new EDF runtime test

Tadpoles and the inherited DragonSmall name

EDF5's DragonSmall class covers the Tadpole family. The source catalogue lists ordinary Red Tadpoles at a 50 SGO durability and Green Tadpoles at 20; the alternate *_HS variants can use smaller models. The source contributor identified three color-based Tadpole types in play. These names do not imply that the EDF4.1 dragon is present in EDF5 gameplay.

Blue drones and bomb ships

e507_goldufo_hi is the Blue Drone variant with diffuse tint (0.6,0.6,0.8,1) and game_object_level_adjust=1.5. The audited mission script for EDF5 offline mission 7 spawns 50 of these on Hardest/Inferno only. That is a specific mission/difficulty source condition, not an assertion that all drone encounters change similarly.

e507_goldufo_big is the Bomb Ship: its definition uses ai_big_bomber_info and base durability 3500. The DLC Mission Pack 2 source census records it spawning in missions 3, 10 and 13, with respective counts 3, 2 and 1. This is a mission-script count, not an independent runtime population measurement.

Armor-clad Colonists

The e503_armorfrog_* definitions add armor_model, armor_ragdoll and armor-break reactions not present in corresponding e503_frog_* definitions. Humanoid class alone therefore cannot establish whether a Colonist is the armored variant. Inspect the SGO subfamily and armor fields.
