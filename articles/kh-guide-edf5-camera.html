EDF5 Camera Modes, Vehicle Ride and First-Person Hooks

EDF 5Community Knowledge HubGameplay & AI
Technical reference · Editorial synthesis of contributor static/game observations; per-claim grades below

R86 cross-build qualification: EDF5 camera offsets/hook sites do not transfer directly to EDF6. Camera reconciliation.

Overview

EDF5 does not treat a vehicle camera as a new scene-tree parent of the soldier. The camera keeps the soldier as its target while its mode changes from follow to ride. This matters for camera mods, especially when extracting the final view matrix or attaching first-person view to a head bone.

Format, API or behavior

The studied Steam build uses a mode object at camera +0xAE0; follow and ride have separate routines. The final rotation rows are at +0x1A0/+0x1B0/+0x1C0, while the eye is at +0x1D0. A per-frame hook at the tail of CharacterGhostCamera::Update was tested. The suggested first-person implementation is not itself established as a working complete mod.

Camera mode state machine — static

This detailed research record is also present in the linked source appendix. Read the original source and evidence context; the literal duplicate is preserved below for comparison.

Full duplicated research record — expand (651 characters)

The player camera runs one of two modes: Update calls the mode object at camera+0xAE0 (call [vtable+8], edx = 1, at 9F45F); follow mode 9F590 switches to ride mode A0130 when the target's ride control block [target+0x1170] has users (9FFDC-9FFEE: lea of A0130, state installed through camera+0xAE8, old state called with edx = 2), and ride mode switches back when it has none (A033C-A034E). Member-function-pointer state at camera+0xAE8 (state object [+0x10]). Ride mode reads the seat record [target+0x1160]: [seat+0x200] == 1 builds the camera from the bone matrices at seat+0x208 / +0x218 (A01A2); the ride update switches on [seat+0x200] at A03B4.

Camera final matrix — static

This detailed research record is also present in the linked source appendix. Read the original source and evidence context; the literal duplicate is preserved below for comparison.

Full duplicated research record — expand (682 characters)

The final world matrix is camera+0x1A0 / +0x1B0 / +0x1C0 (rotation rows) and +0x1D0 (eye position, w = 1). Follow mode writes +0x1D0 at 9F7E4, 9FB03, 9FF9C and the rows at 9FFAD-9FFBC; ride mode writes them at A077D-A07A8; the optional shake A5900 (called at 9F49D) multiplies the rows by +0x200; nothing writes them after the tail. The render submit (vtable slot 5, A0E00) copies them into the render record at A1068; slot 3 9EB50 copies them to +0xA90..+0xAC0 (probably the previous-frame copy, unverified). Systems that find the camera through the per-view table [125AB68] (0x188 bytes per entry, camera weak pointer at +0x238) read +0x1A0..+0x1D0 directly (e.g. 302760, 47A670).

Camera tail hook site — verified

This detailed research record is also present in the linked source appendix. Read the original source and evidence context; the literal duplicate is preserved below for comparison.

Full duplicated research record — expand (298 characters)

The end of Update, inc dword ptr [rdi+0x424] at 9F4F3 (6 bytes, rdi = camera), is a working per-frame hook site: the target is still locked in the local [rsp+0x20] and the camera fields are final for the frame. Hook: E9 to own code, replay the inc, jump back to +6. Only one patch can own the site.

Camera target stays soldier — verified

While the player rides a vehicle the camera target is still the soldier, and the soldier has no scene parent (the vehicle is not its parent in the scene tree); find the vehicle through the ride link (player.md#ride-link-fields).

Camera first person feasibility — unverified

A first-person view looks feasible by writing the eye +0x1D0 at the tail (camera-final-matrix, camera-eye-lerp-and-view-source); ride mode already has a bone-based camera path (target+0x1160, 3AF840), a possible head-bone source.

Version notes and limitations

Do not treat the verified tail-hook site as proof that every camera mode is safe to override; in heavy combat the reported scene-camera projection occasionally becomes invalid for whole frames. Offsets are for steam-5d11de53, not arbitrary EDF5 builds.
