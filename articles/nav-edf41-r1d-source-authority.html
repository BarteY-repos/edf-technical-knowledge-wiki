EDF 4.1 R1D: exact oracles, source selection and carve boundaries

EDF 4.1Maps & NavigationNavigationEDF 6
Technical reference · Native source-corpus and exact oracle, offline reconstruction only; no EDF6 in-game runtime tests

At a glance

What this page answers: The R1D target NAV is known as an exact oracle, but the complete source eligibility and carve process that produced it is still unresolved.

How to read it: Distinguish the reproducible R1C LOW tile output from the R32 body-selection candidate and remaining body-local carve uncertainty.

Evidence boundary: Matching partial counts or a saved target hash is not completion of the EDF4.1→EDF6 NAV converter or a gameplay PASS.

Applies to: EDF 4.1 source maps and EDF 6-target navigation conversion, particularly the NW_HILLYCITY_LIGHT reconstruction. Evidence: exact retained output hashes and structural source-corpus audits; reconstruction tests are offline only. Nothing in this article qualifies an EDF 6 runtime deployment.

What is actually known

The R1C LOW navigation tile set has been reproduced byte-exact from retained source/tooling. The historical R1D target is known byte-exact as an oracle, but its complete original eligibility/carve pipeline has not been recovered. An independently constructed, source-semantic R32 body-selection candidate reproduces the required classifier/placement population. It does not reproduce the target HKT bytes.

R32 source-semantic selection candidate

The strongest retained independent reconstruction uses:

  1. A source-MAD group-union XZ-area metric, with constituent geometry positioned by body motionState, to rank the natural top 65 groups.
  2. A raw source cached-AABB body classifier with max_xz >= 5.75, not an arbitrarily rounded proxy.
  3. MAPB activation under the historical census: 1,405 placements, of which 251 have exact-zero rotation, 1,153 are yaw-only and 1 has mixed X/Z rotation.
  4. 4,994 pre-deduplication footprint instances. Source-semantic FixB duplicate-shape handling removes 23, leaving 4,971.

The R100 identity audit has a crucial capture limit: only 63 of the 65 selected groups have an observed MAPO base/collision source pair; the other two groups have zero captured signatures, not competing signatures. The source audit README incorrectly states that all 65 have a signature; see the exact 65-group census.

The R32 active population uses 479 distinct body identities. It is a reconstructed candidate, not a recovered copy of the old historical body-eligibility function. The retained classifier's lost implementation is still a provenance gap, even when the numeric census is exact.

The earlier 4,913, 4,916, 4,944, 4,948 and 5,026 populations came from different experimental geometry/filter orderings; do not present them as later tolerances of one common algorithm. Separately, a 4,971 population produced through a contrived rank-24/rank-78 group swap is a forensic control, not a plausible source-derived top-65 ranking.

Body-local carve is different from group selection

The retained body-local carve lineage uses the local rounded body AABB/piece plus the MAPB placement. It does not re-apply the source rigid body's internal motionState when constructing each individual carve footprint. That motionState belongs to group-ranking geometry, not automatically to body-local carving.

The current source-semantic R32 footprint population and retained finite carve predicates produce candidate 277-face sets, but no exact historical LOW HKT. The best retained R32 natural 277-face candidate at the R75 closure has 381,544 bytes (+32 from the historical R1D LOW length), with exact NVMD and correct face topology; its HKT hash differs. The older 381,528 bytes (+16) control comes from the non-natural group-swap population and should not steer source-recovery work.

Full MAPO quaternion+relative-position transforms have also been checked as post-selection geometry (see MAPO component semantics). All eight natural variants preserved the 4,971 footprint count, but the complete finite carve enumeration found zero 277-face outputs. This is negative evidence for that specific candidate family, not proof that MAPO rotation is unrelated to rendering or placement.

What a converter should do

  • Preserve the exact source MAPB / MAPO group, placement, rotation and resolution census.
  • Preserve the raw cached-AABB classifier semantics if reconstructing the R32 candidate; never adjust 5.75 to force a historical count.
  • Distinguish group-union XZ ranking, body eligibility, post-selection source identity, and body-local carve geometry.
  • Apply provenance-based FixB semantic duplicate handling, not a global shape_hash collapse on unrelated strict groups.
  • Use a retained finite set of natural carve predicates rather than brute-force 277-face subset search.
  • Keep the exact R1C, historical R1D and R32 candidate identities distinct.

Do not assume: a candidate that passes all these population rules has recovered the historical R1D HKT. The last-mile source/carve identity remains open.
