EDF5 Item Drops, Collection Limits and Online Authority

EDF 5Community Knowledge HubGameplay & AI
Technical reference · Editorial synthesis of contributor static/game observations; per-claim grades below

Overview

EDF5 item boxes are managed by a native crate manager with separate live-field and collected-count limits. In multiplayer, spawning is authority-gated and the network sends spawn identity; each client subsequently simulates the crate.

Format, API or behavior

The source records a default 128 crates on the field and a separate collected-item cap of 0x400 (1024). Their patches are independent. Online spawn dispatch includes RNG seed, serial and position; the local receiving path builds the corresponding crate. This makes source authority and deterministic identity important when altering pickup limits.

Drop authority check — static

Offline the drop always runs; online it runs only if [[125AC08] → 7E6E0(...)] vcall +0x18 returns true, an authority check (almost certainly the host: the source's inference). 125AC08: globals.md#global-network-session.

Version notes and limitations

The author infers that the online authority predicate is the host; the exact role was not independently revalidated here. Do not conflate the field cap with persistent progression or total boxes dropped.
