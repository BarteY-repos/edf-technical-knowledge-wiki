VehicleBase

The VehicleBase constructor 2E6FD0 (after the GameObject constructor 28CBD0

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-vehiclebase-ctor-keys

The VehicleBase constructor 2E6FD0 (after the GameObject constructor 28CBD0; vtables AA0028 and secondary A9FF98 written at 2E7016 / 2E7020) reads the SGO keys animation_model (A055C0), ragdoll (A055F0), vehicle_riding_position (A05630), vehicle_weapon_setting (A05600 / A056D0) and vehicle_damage_effect (A05110 / A050E0: [0] a float, [1] an int, [2] four floats); a missing weapon node logs VehicleBase::VehicleBase( weapon node not found. %ls) (A05660). Seats: the seat builder 2E7820 runs per vehicle_riding_position entry (loop 2E7370..2E738F), skips an entry without exactly 6 values (2E7871), reads the seat SGO's keys Animation, DispCharacter, Type, LookTarget, Param, XAngleAdjust (default 1.0) and appends a 0x180-byte seat (EDF5 0x340) to the vector at +0x320 (data +0x328, count +0x338; 2E7B4C..2E7B8A); the count goes to +0x340 and the mask (1 << count) - 1 to +0x344 (2E7391..2E73A6), 2E7BE0's float to +0xAF0.

Basis: fn.py reads of 2E6FD0 and 2E7820; key strings by scratch exe_strfind.py and a UTF-16 decode

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2E6FD0, 28CBD0, AA0028, A9FF98, 2E7016, 2E7020, A055C0, A055F0, A05630, A05600, A056D0, A05110, A050E0, A05660, 2E7820, 2E7370, 2E738F, 2E7871, 2E7B4C, 2E7B8A, 2E7391, 2E73A6, 2E7BE0

Vehicle weapon setting vector entries

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-vehicle-weapon-seats

Vehicle weapons: the constructor reserves vehicle_weapon_setting's entry count in a vector of 0x40-byte entries at +0x350 (data +0x358, count +0x368); per row it looks up the weapon node by name (50B210), stores it at entry +0x10 and the vehicle at +0x30, and files the entry under a seat: the row's [1] is a seat index, and a seat's own weapon list (seat +0x38: data +0x40, count +0x50) gets the entry, or every seat's list when [1] is negative (2E7566..2E766B). EDF5 keeps no per-seat list in its constructor (#edf5-vehiclebase-ctor-keys in ../edf5/vehicles.md).

Basis: fn.py read of 2E6FD0

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 50B210, 2E7566, 2E766B

The VehicleBase vtable AA0028 has 36 slots (0..35

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-vehicle-vtable-slots

The VehicleBase vtable AA0028 has 36 slots (0..35; slot 36 holds the next table's RTTI locator B21858), against EDF5's 46. Slots read: 2 the update (2E8310, #edf41-vehicle-update), 29 SetUp(setup list) (base 2E8A10, #edf41-vehicle-setup-hp; overrides: Tank 301 / 403 / 404 and the Rocket 3056A0, Striker 302470, Bike 2F55D0 (#vehiclebike-408), Begaruta 2F04F0, Helicopter302 2F6970, 501 2DB700, 502 2E3870, RescueTank 300630, CarDefault 2D06E0), 31 the per-shot reaction (base 2E7D70; Tank301 and Striker 3025C0, Bike 2F5E00, Begaruta 2F19B0, Rocket 301F20, each calling the base), 32 FindSeat (base 2E9080, Striker and RescueTank 300BE0; #edf41-boarding-checks).

Basis: vtables of the RTTI vehicle classes side by side (scratch rtti_vt.py, rvaval.py vt), slots read with fn.py

Vehicle health and weapon damage setup

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-vehicle-setup-hp

SetUp's base 2E8A10 sets HP and the weapon damage factor as EDF5 does (../edf5/vehicles.md#edf5-vehicle-setup-hp): max and current HP (+0x168, +0x16C) = durability +0x160 x setup[0][0] x a, and +0x398 = setup[0][1] x b. When the flags byte +0x2A has bit 3, a = f / 10 (AB6790) x ModeList[mode][6][difficulty][5][0] and b = f / 10 x [5][1] (f the level factor +0x25C, difficulty [save+0x44], config [save+0xC0], save [CC84A0], as in enemies.md#edf41-hp-same-steps); otherwise a = b = +0x39C (1.0 from the constructor). So ModeList value 5 is [vehicle HP, vehicle damage] (offline all [1, 1]; online, Easy to Inferno, [1.8, 1.8], [2.0, 1.5] twice, [1.8, 1.5] twice, as KH chat 130 read them from config.sgo), applied only to vehicles with bit 3.

Basis: fn.py read of 2E8A10

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2E8A10, AB6790, CC84A0

The update 2E8310 runs 2E6C20 on every weapon entry and calls slot 31 for a weapon whose +0x864 is 1

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-vehicle-update

The update 2E8310 runs 2E6C20 on every weapon entry and calls slot 31 for a weapon whose +0x864 is 1; it sets the team from the riders as EDF5's does (../edf5/vehicles.md#edf5-vehicle-update): while HP +0x16C > 0 and flags +0x2A bit 3 is clear, the team is that of the live rider in the highest-numbered occupied seat (rider seat+0xD8, its control seat+0xE0; team +0x180; default 5), and on a change the vehicle leaves the old team list (2C9390 on [CC8510]) and joins the new one (EE300), flag +0x1E8 bit 6 (2E8487..2E85AE; EDF5 calls 2DBE20 instead). Then it clears the seat change bits, word +0x348 &= +0x344 (2E85B4), and copies +0x390 / +0x394 to each seat's +0x170 / +0x174 before clearing them (EDF5 +0x480 to seat +0x2F0). The change bits are read per class (Car, Tank, Begaruta, the helicopters, e.g. Helicopter302 2FE782); no shared boarding-sound function like EDF5's 355630 was found.

Basis: fn.py read of 2E8310; readers of +0x348 by fn.py --grep over 2CA000..310000

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2E8310, 2E6C20, 2C9390, CC8510, EE300, 2E8487, 2E85AE, 2E85B4, 2FE782

Who may ride: GameObject init 28CBD0 reads game_object_vehicle_mask (9FEAC0, 28CF20) into +0x184 (28D024..28D03C

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-boarding-checks

Who may ride: GameObject init 28CBD0 reads game_object_vehicle_mask (9FEAC0, 28CF20) into +0x184 (28D024..28D03C; EDF5 +0x220). CanRide 2E8ED0(vehicle, human) passes when some seat has human+0x184 & seat+0x30 non-zero, no rider (seat+0xD8 null) and the human (position +0x90) within the riding point's radius + 0.5 (AB656C) of the point built from the MAB record seat+0x60 and the bone matrix seat+0x68. Unlike EDF5's CanRideSeat (../edf5/vehicles.md#edf5-boarding-checks) there is no team test, no second mask and no bit-7 rule. Its one caller is the visitor A9D598 slot 1 29DCF0 (inferred: the boarding prompt). FindSeat, slot 32 2E9080, runs the same tests and takes the first seat that passes: rider +0xD8 = the human, weak link +0xE0, change bits (1 << i) and (1 << i) << count into word +0x348, then 2E9A70; 2E92B0 is the variant that skips seats whose entry in a caller's int list is negative (only caller: Striker / RescueTank slot 32 300BE0). 2E9530(vehicle, human, seat, kick) seats by index (2E9640 empties a seat).

Basis: fn.py reads; xrefs of the key string, 2E8ED0, 2E92B0, 2E9530

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 28CBD0, 9FEAC0, 28CF20, 28D024, 28D03C, 2E8ED0, AB656C, A9D598, 29DCF0, 2E9080, 2E9A70, 2E92B0, 300BE0, 2E9530, 2E9640

Player ride-button boarding path

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-ride-button-path

Boarding by the ride button: the per-player update 2B2170 calls 29DD50(human) when the ride byte +0x999 is set (2B21E1..2B21ED; EDF5 +0xB58). It returns when the vehicle link +0xF50 is set, +0x320 bit 2 or +0x204 != 0; otherwise it walks team 5 and then the human's own team (+0x180; 2C9740 on [CC8510]) with the visitor vtable A9D948, whose slot 1 29DE20 casts each object to VehicleBase (8D8F9C) and asks FindSeat (slot 32, call [r8+0x100] at 29DE78), then sets +0xF50 = the vehicle, +0xF58 = the seat, the weak link +0xF60, +0xFE0 = 0, +0xFE8 = 0, +0xFEC = 1.0. After a ride it calls the online sender 2A1080 and starts the action 2A44F0 on +0xBC0 (292AA0). 29DEE0 leaves (2E9640) and copies the vehicle's +0xAF4 to +0x100C. Soldier ride fields do not sit at a fixed distance from EDF5's (ride byte 0x1BF lower, seat 0x208 lower).

Basis: fn.py reads and xrefs

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2B2170, 29DD50, 2B21E1, 2B21ED, 2C9740, CC8510, A9D948, 29DE20, 8D8F9C, 29DE78, 2A1080, 2A44F0, 292AA0, 29DEE0, 2E9640

Online vehicle ride network event

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-ride-net-events

Online ride events: 2A1080 logs OnlineEvent_VehicleRide :: %x (9FFEB0) and sends only online (the mission's online flag), not for a remote copy (+0xC8 bit 0) and only with a live ride link +0xF60, carrying the seat index and the counter +0x1008 (incremented at 2A149C; EDF5 +0x1468); the leave sender 2A14D0 logs OnlineEvent_VehicleLeave :: %x (9FFE90). The receiver 2A1EE0 (a switch over message types; table entry A9D610, callers 2ABA20 and 2B8790) logs netVehicleRide(%x)(%d) (9FFEE8) and netVehicleLeave(%x) (9FFED0), seats the copy with 2E9530 (2A2120) and empties with 29DEE0. The message numbers were not read.

Basis: string xrefs (scratch exe_strfind.py) and fn.py reads

Addresses (build-specific RVAs unless indicated): 2A1080, 9FFEB0, 2A149C, 2A14D0, 9FFE90, 2A1EE0, A9D610, 2ABA20, 2B8790, 9FFEE8, 9FFED0, 2A2120, 29DEE0

DummyVehicleRider used for AI vehicle seats

Evidence: static · Build: steam-57aab039 · Fact ID: edf41-vehicle-ai-rider

AI riders, as in EDF5 (../edf5/vehicles.md#edf5-vehicle-ai-rider): each creator builds a DummyVehicleRider (vtables A90360 / A902D0, constructor C6D30) and seats it with 2E9530, linking it with 56BAD0; 2E8C50 runs first (not read; EDF5's 354A90 clears the weapons' flags there). The creators: the mission native 1010 CreateFriend 1510F0 (cmp edx, 0x3F2 at 14C60F; its error CreateFriend:ポイントが見つからない%ls "point not found", 9ECAC8), Transporter_Formation slot 33 2C1040 (vtable A9EEB0; mode 1 seats the rider, mode 0 stores its factor in +0x39C at 2C11B7) and the console command test_dummy_rider (BF040, registered at C2F54, string 9BA9C8); 4.1 has no ai_vehicle string. Setup blocks: 2E9B40 reads mission_setup (A05720) for CreateFriend (1512B4) and the formation (2C11D5) and calls SetUp (slot 29); 151BC0, a mission native called at 14C7CB (number not read), reads mission_setup itself (151D44); vehicle_setup (A05700) is read by 2E9AF0 (one caller, B8B50) and in B8880 and BE990 (inferred: console commands).

Basis: RTTI vtables (scratch rtti_vt.py), xrefs and fn.py reads

VehicleBike (vtable AA0670): the gun is posed by 2F5900, which calls 2D6BE0 with the gun's local matrix passed twice, building X(-pitch) then Y(-yaw) and keeping the translation, before the base class builds the world and syncs the physics. Slot 29 2F55D0 -> 2D06E0 -> 2D07E0 sets up the Havok vehicle kit from the request's setup[1] four numbers: [0] the friction of every wheel with grip (hkpVehicleData wheel +0x0C; maxFriction +0x14 = twice it; a wheel at 0, the sidecar's, stays 0), [1] the slip angle (+0x18), [2] the top speed (2D19F0 writes +0x1190; the final drive ratio follows from it, so wheel torque goes as 1 / top speed), [3] the engine's maximum torque (engine +0x1C). vehicle_tire rows are read at 2CC7AD..2CC803: [8] -> +0x64 (2CBF2A presets 1 for the first two wheels: steering), [9] -> +0xA8 (default 1), [10] -> +0x68 (default 0.25); the 408's rows (front [8] = 1, [9] = 1, [10] = 0.0; rear 0, 1, 1.0; side 0, 0, 0.0, with grip [2..4] all 0) read as rear drive, front steering, a free sidecar wheel ([9] / [10] as "driven, with this share" is inferred from every 4.1 vehicle's values: tanks 1 / 0.1 on all wheels, the Striker's rear axles 1). The 408 model: physics proxies body, handle, front_sus, front_tire, rear_sus, rear_tire, side_tire, gun (the gun on a two-axis hkpRagdollConstraintData to body), all on layer 0, group 1; vehicle_dactive_node lists gun, front_sus, handle, front_tire as animation-driven; bike_gun_ctrl [-30, 20, -15, 30] with the operator on seat 1; the gun row's turn parameters [90, .015, .1] and recoil ['AimRecoil', [.002618, .0062832]]; seat class masks driver 9 (Ranger and Air Raider), gunner 15. The Ranger's vehicle_bike_driver pose (ARMYSOLDIER.CAS, the same every frame) puts the pelvis at (0, 0.926, -0.079) and the hands te_l / te_r at (±0.426, 1.2516, 0.4442), just inside the handlebar grips. Five requests drop the same Vehicle408_motorbike.sgo and differ only in their setup ([0] armor, [1] the four numbers, [2] the machine gun): SDL1 (WEAPON688) 1.5, [2.0, 0.9, 30, 100]; the broken mod (700) 1.8, [0.25, 0.9, 30, 100]; R (721) 5.0, [2.0, 1.25, 30, 225]; Z (739) 10.5, [2.0, 1.2, 50, 250]; the burst turbo (753) 22.0, [2.0, 1.2, 60, 400].

Basis: the author's static reading ("H"; the tire-column meanings "M") and its read of 4.1's Root.cpk (SGO, SHKT, CAS)
