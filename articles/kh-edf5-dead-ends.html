EDF 5 — Dead Ends (community source notes)

EDF 5Community Knowledge HubTroubleshooting
Source-backed research / case study · Contributed research; per-claim static, verified, unverified; not independently re-tested

This is a source-attributed research appendix, not a chronology or a replacement for topic reference pages. The observations were contributed in the EDF Knowledge Hub export, snapshot 2026-11-14, revision 54f89cd, compiled by novae (ajane).

Read each statement with its evidence grade and exact build. Verified means a reported game test, static means reverse engineering or source inspection without a reported game test, and unverified is a hypothesis. None of these observations was newly tested for this wiki release.

More facts

Attempt that did not work — 1

Attempt: shrinking particle .dds sprite images

Why it failed / limitation: crude: the game draws the same sprite at very different sizes (a beehive vs. a giant walker's dirt clouds)

Suggested direction: a screen-space size cap per particle in ParticleRender (idea only, not built)

Source: user, novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 2

Attempt: player+0x280 bit 0x10 as an invulnerability bit

Why it failed / limitation: it is the DEAD flag (set at 2DB7F0 when HP <= 0)

Suggested direction: bit 1 (set_invincible)

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 3

Attempt: enlarging xgl_user_param only in the pixel shaders (shader packs v01-v03)

Why it failed / limitation: in game: heap corruption, crashes at start of missions with NPC tanks, on Retreat, at the drone mission-end fade (ntdll C0000374); static cause: 11 files also have vertex shaders binding xgl_user_param, so one effect reported two sizes for the same material cbuffer

Suggested direction: append the same parameters to every vertex shader that binds it (pack v04 / v05). The source records later in-game tests with pack v05 and no further crash, but never states the crash fixed

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 4

Attempt: letting the camera pass MapObject_Fmex (PROP PASS bit 512, v19), then only steep Fmex contacts with |normal.y| < 0.5 (v20)

Why it failed / limitation: verified in game: Fmex is the ground mesh (plus some baked-in props), so the camera sank under the ground; with the slope rule it flipped outside the level in caves and on steep slopes

Suggested direction: PROP MAX SIZE, pass only objects whose AABB width <= 10 m (v21, not tested in game)

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 5

Attempt: letting the camera pass every MapObject_Model (v19/v20)

Why it failed / limitation: in game: MapObject_Model also builds big level pieces (underground base rooms, halls, exit ramp); passing them puts the camera outside the level or under the ground

Suggested direction: PROP MAX SIZE (v21, not tested in game)

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 6

Attempt: ModelDebris / ModelDebrisManager as the scenery-sink mechanism (session 1)

Why it failed / limitation: its tick is only an elapsed timer that jumps to cleanup; it is the debris-chunk system

Suggested direction: the CrashModel tick D7830 (layer 30 and impulses, physics-havok.md)

Source: novae (ajane), session 1 · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 7

Attempt: searching for code references to Havok's hkpDisableEntityCollisionFilter to explain the sink (session 1)

Why it failed / limitation: zero direct references; inconclusive

Suggested direction: the collision-layer change in D7830 (layer 30 collides only with layer 0), user-confirmed with crates

Source: novae (ajane), session 1 · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 8

Attempt: the MapObjectDestroy/2/3/4 strings as destruction-effect code (session 1)

Why it failed / limitation: they are level-authoring instance names

Suggested direction: the MapObject class tick and vtable (map-objects.md)

Source: novae (ajane), session 1 · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 9

Attempt: collecting crates after the fail screen appears (v08 sweep mode 2)

Why it failed / limitation: in game: crates collected while the result screen is up are neither shown nor kept

Suggested direction: sweep before the failed-result layout (MISSION FAILED banner, v23)

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 10

Attempt: mission-clear work in the ApplyResult script native (ClearHook 3E3168, v18-v20)

Why it failed / limitation: in game: crash after the fade; it runs after the mission is torn down, so the player pointer is stale

Suggested direction: EndMission 114BB0 (v21) or the MISSION CLEAR banner via ShowLayout 126EE0 (v22), both verified

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 11

Attempt: vacuum flights timed to a fixed duration (SINK) (v10)

Why it failed / limitation: long durations need enormous launch speeds (60 s needs about 290 m/s up)

Suggested direction: arrival-based collection (v11)

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 12

Attempt: tuning vacuum speed against an idealized physics model (sessions 2-3)

Why it failed / limitation: in game: flights took 6-10 s; the engine clips crates to about 57 m/s and gravity is 14.7, not 9.8

Suggested direction: read the engine's own limits first, raise the per-body limit (v13, verified)

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 13

Attempt: re-solving the vacuum aim with a floored flight time each re-aim (v11 draft, v12 "lagging" rule)

Why it failed / limitation: the crate hovers g·T²/2 below the target (21 m at a 1.5 s floor, 176 m at T = 6 s), caught in simulation

Suggested direction: let the planned time count down

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 14

Attempt: a shrink tied to the planned flight time or a distance window (v13-v16)

Why it failed / limitation: in game: crates shrank away 6-20 m before reaching the player at 50-100+ m/s

Suggested direction: shrink after the catch (absorb, v17, "much closer to pleasing")

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 15

Attempt: switching to every-frame control in the last frames of a flight (v15 terminal guidance)

Why it failed / limitation: in game: close-range wobble; a change of cadence reads as a kink

Suggested direction: one steady re-aim cadence for the whole flight (v16)

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 16

Attempt: the per-difficulty byte table at E92AA4 as the loot-retention rule

Why it failed / limitation: it is not the retention rule

Suggested direction: the result tally 199120 and [0x125AB30]+0x4C (mission-flow.md)

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 17

Attempt: taking the global [0x125AB68] for the decal manager because creation wrappers pass it

Why it failed / limitation: it is a world/scene object with about 530 users

Suggested direction: confirm a singleton by its vtable/RTTI (DecalManager)

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 18

Attempt: finding an acid-splat count limit in the spawner 1B5850, AcidBullet01, ClaySplashEffect and EffectLimitter

Why it failed / limitation: none found; the instant loss of old acid splats is still unexplained

Suggested direction: novae (ajane) test in lessons-blood-decals.md section 5 (huge OVERLAP_LIMIT and MAX_DECALS: if splats still vanish, the limit is outside the decal system)

Source: novae (ajane) · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 19

Attempt: scaling the blood spray's far where it is loaded (1AFB46, BLOOD_REACH v03-v06)

Why it failed / limitation: it also pulls the projector back and feeds the rank, so splats grew wider; novae (ajane) noticed

Suggested direction: hook only the uses you mean, here the ray (1AFB4F..) and the far plane (1AFD57) separately (v07); list every use of a value before scaling it (decals.md#blood-spray-far)

Source: novae (ajane), lessons section 4 · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 20

Attempt: editing the generator by string replacement

Why it failed / limitation: one replacement silently deleted four settings, another failed to match and dropped an anchor

Suggested direction: keep tests that check every setting loads and every anchor resolves, and rerun the whole suite after every edit

Source: novae (ajane), lessons section 4 · Build: steam-5d11de53

Attempt that did not work — 21

Attempt: an Alt+Shift hotkey chord

Why it failed / limitation: Alt+Shift is Windows' keyboard-language switch

Suggested direction: Alt plus a letter key (v08/v09 use Alt+N/J/M/K)

Source: novae (ajane), HANDOFF lesson 6 · Build: steam-5d11de53
