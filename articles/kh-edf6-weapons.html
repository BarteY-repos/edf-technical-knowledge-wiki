Evidence: static · Build: steam-678ccb46 · Fact ID: shield-attribute-resists

Object +0xB68 is the core's +0xA28 (0xB68 - 0x140), and the only meaningful byte reads of +0xA28 are in the hit filter (232BCF): 232D02..232D53 skip the team test when +0xA27 and +0xA28 are both 0, also when either team (the target's +0x314, the bullet's +0xA00) is -1; for a friend (relation 1) the hit is dropped (232E63, the bullet passes through) when +0xA29 is set, or when the target lacks +0x380 & 0x100000 (that bit's meaning not traced). +0xA27 is set together with +0xA29 at 231D90.
