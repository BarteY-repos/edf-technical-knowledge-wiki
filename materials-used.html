This reference brings together research and documentation used to understand Earth Defense Force 4.1, 5 and 6. The goal is to acknowledge the community and help readers find the original material without repeating a long credit line after every technical observation.

Research collections and technical sources

  • EDF Knowledge Hub — technical research compiled by novae (ajane) and named external contributors (source revisions 54f89cd and 5b589ca). Some compiled English research records are reproduced in their original wording, explicitly labeled as Knowledge Hub source text in expanded record panels. Permission to incorporate this research into a larger shared knowledge base was conveyed to the project owner; this is not a blanket license from every third party cited inside those records. All 1,993 newly identified fact IDs have been reconciled into this reference. That does not constitute independent in-game verification, complete preservation of every crosswalk editorial row, or permission to redistribute an original research archive. Changed and removed historical records still have documented provenance limits. Research coverage and evidence boundaries.
  • mod製作メモ.xlsx — Japanese community research notes provided by ktaro, covering game mechanics and technical details. Integrated research overview. The original workbook is not distributed here.
  • EDF Modding Wiki — community documentation on Miraheze, including format and game-system descriptions associated with KittopiaCreator and other authors. Community wiki.
  • Earth-Defence-Force-Documentation — the community-maintained GitHub Wiki, including legacy format references, examples and observations from multiple researchers.
  • EDF6 All Forces — original code and reverse-engineering research published by hajisensai. Source repository.
  • EDF6VR — source tools and documented archive/decompression tests by momotori01, consulted for particular resource-format findings.
  • Shader and rendering research — additional technical observations shared by metoxys, including map, foliage and water rendering.
  • Model and Blender-related reference work — tool-build and interoperability evidence associated with FevGrave and other community tool authors.
  • Project research and test corpora — the wiki's retained EDF 4.1/5/6 source studies, binary observations, measured conversions, negative tests and technical reconciliations. These are distinguished from third-party research and from runtime validation.

Source wording and reuse boundaries

Knowledge Hub records: The expanded panels marked EDF Knowledge Hub record — original wording preserve the compiled source text, sometimes with presentation or formatting edits. The Hub's compiler and individual credited researchers remain identified. Permission was reported for incorporating this research into a shared knowledge base; no independent public reuse license is granted to readers here, and it does not automatically extend to separate third-party documents that the Hub cites.

Independent researchers and other community sources: Findings, class and field names, offsets, measurements and references from ktaro, hajisensai, FevGrave, KittopiaCreator, metoxys and other contributors inform the wiki. The editorial policy is to explain their research in independently written language unless a specific work's license or permission allows literal reuse. No blanket express permission for verbatim prose was recorded from these authors, and no blanket prohibition from them was recorded either. Source credit is not a claim of endorsement, consent or a ban.

Evidence and attribution

Technical confidence still depends on what was tested: source-file observations, executable analysis, interpretation, converted content and successful in-game tests are not interchangeable. Newer reproducible findings can refine older descriptions. Acknowledgments on this page do not establish the correctness of any particular claim.

For readability, repeated Attribution: paragraphs in technical articles are hidden by default when JavaScript is enabled. Their original text remains in the pages and can be revealed using Show claim credits at the bottom of an article. Technical evidence, build identifiers, measured values and links to original research are otherwise retained.
